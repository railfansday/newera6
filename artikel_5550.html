eLEND expands credit eligibility for FHA cash-out refis, conventional loans

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

eLEND launched CreditEdge, expanding eligibility for FHA cash-out refinances to 500 credit scores and conventional loans below 620, subject to AUS approval. Loans on primary residences must be locked by Dec. 31, 2026, across retail, wholesale and nondelegated correspondent channels.   AI Summary

New Jersey-based mortgage lender eLEND is expanding credit score eligibility for Federal Housing Administration (FHA) cash-out refinances and conventional loans through a limited-time incentive designed to give borrowers with lower credit scores more financing options.

The program, called CreditEdge, allows FHA cash-out refinance borrowers to qualify with credit scores as low as 500 and conventional borrowers to qualify with scores below 620, subject to approval through automated underwriting systems (AUS).

The expanded guidelines apply to primary residences and are available through eLEND’s retail, wholesale and nondelegated correspondent channels. Loans must be locked by Dec. 31, 2026, to qualify.

“The Fed held rates steady last week, and with inflation still weighing on the economy, affordability can’t wait on rate relief,” said Rob Pieklo, president and CEO of eLEND. “That’s why we’re focused on what we can control. Expanding credit score requirements, backed by AUS approval to evaluate each borrower’s ability to repay, gives our broker partners more ways to get deals done and helps more people get into homes.”

For FHA cash-out refinances, the minimum credit score is 500. Fannie Mae- and Freddie Mac-backed conventional loans are eligible with scores below 620, although a minimum score of 600 applies when private mortgage insurance is required.

All loans must receive an Approve/Eligible finding from Fannie Mae’s Desktop Underwriter or an Accept finding from Freddie Mac’s Loan Product Advisor.

“Our broker partners tell us credit score requirements are one of the main reasons they have to turn borrowers away,” said Michael Brenning, chief operating officer of eLEND. “That’s changing. With expanded credit score requirements on FHA cash-outs and conventional loans, our partners can help clients tap into their equity, get more first-time homebuyers in the door, and close more loans before year-end. And with every loan backed by AUS approval, we’re expanding access without compromising on responsible lending.”

eLEND said the program pairs expanded credit score requirements with automated underwriting to maintain its lending standards. AUS evaluates a borrower’s broader financial profile, including income, assets, debts and payment history, rather than relying solely on a credit score.

CreditEdge is part of a broader effort by eLEND to expand its product offerings for mortgage brokers and lending partners. Recent additions include an expanded renovation lending suite featuring the AI-powered Ready4Remodel platform, eXPRESS HELOC and debt-service-coverage ratio (DSCR) loans. The company also said jumbo AUS is coming soon.

American Financial Resources, which rebranded to eLEND last year, has produced a year-to-date volume of $405.7 million, according to RETR data.

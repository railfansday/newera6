How To Watch Robert Pattinson's Primetime At Home

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The year of Robert Pattinson will continue until morale improves — and, hopefully, for some time after that, too. While fellow "The Odyssey" co-stars Zendaya and Anne Hathaway have stolen the spotlight for similar reasons throughout 2026, the "Primetime" actor has quietly appeared in quite a few high-profile roles throughout the last several months. A24's "The Drama" (also co-starring Zendaya) left quite the impression on audiences and at the box office, as did the aforementioned retelling of "The Odyssey" by Christopher Nolan. And, as if his memorable line about getting "these beggars out of here" wasn't enough, he's set to follow all that up with a turn in "Dune: Part Three" later this December.

But don't sleep on "Primetime," the other major production by "You Can See Everything" co-director Lance Oppenheim this year. Starring Pattinson as Chris Hansen, the host behind the controversial TV show in the early aughts "To Catch a Predator," the film takes an unflinching, stylish, and occasionally surrealistic approach to the real-life figure who publicly shamed and embarrassed so many troubled minds with nefarious intent. But was this done for heroic and well-intentioned reasons? Or is there more to be uncovered beneath that bleached hair, distinctive voice, and wild-eyed chase for ratings and pop-culture buzz?

Well, audiences will now get a chance to decide for themselves, as "Primetime" is being made available to watch at home. The news comes a little over a month after its release in theaters, where it's actually still playing in certain locations. But for those who may have missed it the first time around, read on for all the details about how to catch it from the comfort of your living room.

The dark and unsettling satire that is "Primetime" is officially coming home, which is certain to lend a strong sense of irony to anyone watching this A24 movie about one of the most notorious television shows ever made. Based on the real-life controversy surrounding host Chris Hansen and his "To Catch a Predator" reality series, the film comes from director Lance Oppenheim and stars Robert Pattinson, Merritt Wever, "Superman" breakout Skyler Gisondo, Bokeem Woodbine, Tony Revolori, Anna Faris, and even singer/songwriter Phoebe Bridgers.

But, for now, audiences can look forward to experiencing "Primetime" in all its unsettling, R-rated drama at home.

RE+ will host another clean energy job fair this year

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Job seekers hoping to launch or advance a career in clean energy can connect with employers and explore new opportunities at the RE+ Clean Energy Job Fair on Nov. 18, at the Las Vegas Convention Center. Hosted by GRID Alternatives, the Women of Renewable Industries and Sustainable Energy (WRISE) and Green Jobs Board, the fair is free for job seekers, and everyone who registers will receive a complimentary pass to the RE+ Expo, North America’s largest clean energy industry event.

The job fair will run from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. and attendees can connect with hiring companies. The fair is open to people of all skill levels.

“The future of clean energy is about building sustainable careers. When we connect job seekers with employers, we bridge the gap between opportunity and talent, strengthen the workforce, and ensure that the people who need access to these careers have a seat at the table.” said Lyric Darru, grants manager of workforce development at GRID Alternatives. “By connecting job seekers directly with employers and opening the door to the broader RE+ expo at no cost, we’re helping more people explore pathways into clean energy careers and ensuring the benefits of this growing industry reach more communities. We thank our partners and sponsors for their support in bringing this event to Las Vegas.”

In 2025, more than 37,000 people attended RE+, an event with more than 1,300 exhibitors from businesses in solar, energy storage, electric vehicles, grid technologies and other sectors. This year’s conference will take place November 16–19 at the Las Vegas Convention Center.

“WRISE is excited to co-host this important event with GRID again this year,” said Lauren Friedman, senior manager of programs and events at WRISE. “The continued success of the job fair year over year speaks to the lasting strength and enthusiasm for our industry. We’re glad to make these opportunities available to the WRISE membership and our larger clean energy community.”

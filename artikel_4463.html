A British Marque Is Building a New Audi Quattro with a 10,000-RPM V-8

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Audacious Automotive IV is built on the B7 RS4 and has a claimed top speed of 200 mph.

Few cars have as famed a history as the Audi Quattro, and even fewer cars have been reimagined as many times. The newest tribute to Audi’s legendary Quattro is the Audacious Automotive IV. It’s being built by a small British manufacturer whose passion drove it to fit its creation with a V-8 that revs to 10,000 rpm.

The IV combines B7-generation Audi RS4 underpinnings with the look—and in some cases, bodywork—of the short-wheelbase Audi Quattro. While the Quattro was famous for its five-cylinder engine, Audacious felt the RS4’s 4.2-liter V-8 was a better fit for its tribute car.

Coincidentally enough, the builder says the IV will be offered with four engine configurations. The standard setup takes the 4.2-liter V-8 and adds a supercharger, increasing power to 592 ponies. Strengthened internals and improved cooling from Audacious’ Stage Two conversion bring that figure to 740 hp. Plus, there are supercharged and naturally aspirated options that raise the engine’s redline from an already lofty 8250 to an otherworldly 10,000 rpm. It doesn’t seem like the higher redline offers any additional power, but it should sound the business.

Whichever engine customers choose, every IV will use the RS4’s original six-speed manual gearbox. Audacious claims the Quattro recreation will accelerate from zero to 60 mph in 3.4 seconds and reach a top speed of 200 mph, though the brand doesn’t disclose which configuration will reach that terminal velocity.

Customers can order their IV with metal bodywork sourced from an original Quattro donor, or they can have their car built from a new carbon-fiber body that doesn’t require any donor car. According to a report from Autocar, the company expects the latter to make up around 80 percent of its orders. The automaker is targeting a curb weight of around 2755 pounds for the carbon versions and around 3140 pounds for the donor cars.

Like the original Quattro, the interior has been overhauled to be a bit more raw, though at least in the examples shown, the whole thing is draped in leather. With every car being hand-built, Audacious promises that no two IVs will be exactly the same.

Of course, as a coachbuilt model produced by a team the size of Audacious Automotive, production will be extremely limited. It doesn’t hurt that exclusivity sells, and with the IV being limited to just 50 hand-built units worldwide, that number shouldn’t be too hard to hit. There is the price to consider, though, and the IV doesn’t come cheap, with a starting price of roughly $468,650 (based on current exchange rates).

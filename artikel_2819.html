Late scratch of Kelly Oubre Jr. leaves Pacers with six players

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

INDIANAPOLIS -- Just before tip-off Monday, the Pacers added one more to their list of wounded and will take on the Hawks with just six available players.

Forward Kelly Oubre Jr. was a late scratch with a bruised left knee joining 10 other players who were already ruled out. The Pacers will have to make two injured players active just to reach the league minimum of eight, but there are just six apparently healthy enough to play.

Former Purdue All-American Braden Smith, guard Quenton Jackson, forwards Jarace Walker and Jalen Slawson and center Jay Huff are starting. Forward Kobe Brown is the lone available substitute.

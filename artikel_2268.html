‘Surely’: Reporter makes bold prediction regarding ‘outstanding’ Middlesbrough summer recruit

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Middlesbrough signed the attacker from Tottenham Hotspur in the summer and he penned a long-term five-year deal at the Riverside Stadium running until June 2031. He has since adapted well to life at Boro and has been prolific for Kim Hellberg‘s side.

Lankshear, 21, has made 16 appearances in all competitions this season, 13 of which have come in the Championship. He has chipped in with an eye-catching 15 goals already.

Journalist Cottier has delivered his verdict, as per a report by BBC Sport: “Fifteen goals in all competitions is an outstanding number to have reached before the end of October and Middlesbrough look to have quelled any concerns that they cannot be in this promotion race for the long haul. They are exciting, creative and solid at the back – and in Lankshear they have a young Englishman surely destined to get senior international recognition in the not-too-distant future.”

Middlesbrough beat Charlton Athletic 3-0 at home last time out and remain top of the table above Swansea City, Wolves and West Brom after 13 fixtures. They are back in action on Wednesday away at Wrexham as they aim to keep their momentum going for as long as possible.

Middlesbrough’s decision to snap up Lankshear from Tottenham Hotspur has turned out to be a masterstroke. He has established himself as Boro’s most prized asset.

Boro lacked a proper number nine in the last campaign and that was mainly the reason why they missed out on the top two. They then lacked a cutting edge in the play-offs, hence why they eventually lost to Hull City in the final at Wembley in May.

Lankshear has answered their prayers though and he has more than proved that he knows where the back of the net is. He is already on 15 goals and November has only just begun.

The ex-Arsenal and Sheffield United man played six times during his days at Tottenham Hotspur before they sold him permanently. He also had loan spells away from Spurs at West Brom and Oxford United to get some experience under his belt and to boost his development.

Middlesbrough ace Lankshear is one of the most exciting young English players around at the moment. The fact he is playing every week at Boro and not sitting around on Tottenham Hotspur’s bench will be doing him the world of good.

Hellberg’s men are on a roll right now but they can’t afford to be complacent as they aim to claw themselves into the top flight this term. They will need to be at their best to get another three points against Wrexham later this week.

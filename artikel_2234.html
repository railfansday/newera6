James Penrice hit with former referee verdict on 'instant red card' vs Aberdeen

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

James Penrice should have been "instantly" sent off for Rangers in their Premier Sports Cup semi-final against Aberdeen on Saturday evening.

That's the view of former FIFA referee Keith Hackett after he watched the defender catch Dons' Afeez Aremu high in a clash at Hampden.

Gers saw off their foes to reach the showpiece finale next month after Ryan Naderi found the net in his side's narrow 1-0 victory. But it could have been different had Penrice been shown a red card by referee Ryan Lee.

Penrice clearly caught Aremu on the hip with his studs and the debate raged on whether or not the force was enough to see Penrice given his marching orders. But after the whistler did not even produce a yellow for the challenge, VAR official John Beaton refused to intervene and the player continued.

Then he helped tee up Naderi for the winner.

Hackett had his say on Football Insider and insisted the former Hearts ace was lucky to avoid punishment. In fact the retired official could hardly believe the outcome.

He said: "I have to ask how James Penrice was allowed to stay on the field of play, when his high boot was in contact with the chest of his opponent.

"That was clearly serious foul play, and should have seen a red card produced instantly. The fact that no action was taken comes as a surprise. Why was there no intervention by VAR?"

Rangers will now face the winner of the Edinburgh Derby in the first cup final of the campaign.

Fitness influencer Liam Fitzgerald dies aged 29 after motorcycle crash

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Fitness influencer Liam Fitzgerald has died following a motorcycle crash, his partner Jamie Lyn Rin has announced.

Fitzgerald, who was based in Miami and had more than 1 million followers on Instagram, was 29.

He posted content on fitness, style, and finance and ran a personal training business offering one-on-one coaching via his website.

His motorcycle was a regular feature of his Instagram, appearing in multiple posts on his page since he acquired it in December 2025.

Rin, who is also an influencer, shared the news on Instagram, revealing the crash happened in the early hours of October 30.

A post shared by Jamie Lyn Rin (@jamielynrin)

“It is with the heaviest heart that I share the devastating news that Liam passed away following a motorcycle accident on October 30th at approximately 2 AM,” she wrote.

“He suffered severe injuries, and despite the efforts of those who tried desperately to save him, they were unable to bring him back.”

Rin asked fans to keep his family and loved ones in their thoughts, adding: “Please give us grace, love, and privacy as they navigate this unimaginable loss. Rest peacefully, Liam we love you so much.”

No further details about the crash have been made public as of writing.

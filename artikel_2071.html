Ally McCoist in public Rangers transfer plea to star man - don't go anywhere yet

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Ally McCoist believes Olwethu Makhanya should stay at Rangers, despite his impressive start earning acclaim.

The South African defender has become a key player for the club since his £3 million move from Philadelphia Union.

He has impressed at both centre-back and the left side of defence, making him a vital part of Derek McInnes' plans.

His form will likely catch the eye of other clubs, but Rangers legend McCoist thinks the 22-year-old should stay put and continue his development at Ibrox.

"I don't think Makhanya should be in any rush to go anywhere."

He acknowledged Makhanya's strong start but reminded him that the weekend's performance was a reminder that there are no easy games and he is a target.

"From that point of view, there's no doubt that with the way he's started, there will be clubs looking at him.

"But I would be advising him to stay for a while, get the job done, keep getting rave reports, and see where it takes him."

McCoist is also positive about Rangers' progress under McInnes and believes the club's ambitions should remain high, despite European disappointments.

The former striker emphasised the importance of domestic success, with the Premiership title being the ultimate goal.

He said: "I don't think anything has changed.

"I think Rangers have got to set out right now to win every domestic trophy they can.

"You have to be looking to win the league title, that is the most important one at the start of the season for a team like Rangers.

"The Cups I always say are a more than achievable bonus, but you've got to get the league title."

McCoist concluded that while Makhanya's value may be increasing, Rangers is the ideal platform for him to continue building his reputation before considering a move.

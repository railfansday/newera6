“I stayed to help the team” – Modric explains renewal decision, derby win and title desire

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Luka Modric was always going to be essential against Inter and the AC Milan veteran proved his quality at San Siro again.

In midweek, Modric was singled out by Raffaele Palladino, who felt that the game changed in Bologna’s favour with his entry to the pitch. It’s a statement that certainly feels a little strange with Modric’s experience, but it was true.

After all, at 41, not every game is made for Modric and Ruben Amorim has to pick the right moments with him.

Nevertheless, a start against Inter meant that he was able to control the game from the first minute and set the tempo of the game, rather than trying to find a new one, which he very much did. Thanks to that, Milan claimed all three points.

Speaking at full-time to CBS Golazo, Modric explained how the team recovered after the defeat in midweek, the journey of the team and why he chose to stay, as MilanNews relays.

“An incredible victory, an incredible performance from the first minute. I think this was perhaps our best game of the season. Overall, we deserved the win and we’re really happy after the disappointing result against Bologna midweek.”

How did you recover after the defeat against Bologna?

“We have to stay positive. We can’t complain too much about the last match because we know the next one is coming. It’s an important match and there’s no better opportunity to test ourselves against Inter, and we did just that. Unfortunately, if we want to achieve something important, we can’t afford to lose games like the one in Bologna.”

“We’re growing as a team, and we’ve got new ideas. There will be ups and downs at times, but we have to try to minimise them if we want to win titles.”

What do you think of the atmosphere at San Siro?

“It was incredible. Since I arrived here, the atmosphere has always been fantastic, in every game, but especially in games like the derbies. Tonight, from the warm-up, they were incredible, and that kind of atmosphere helps you win even more.”

After the disappointment of failing to qualify for the Champions League last season, you decided to stay at Milan. Why?

“I’m happy at Milan; I feel loved and appreciated here. I spoke with the club and the new coach and I wanted to stay because I’m happy here; I love this game and I enjoy it. I stayed to help the team and the younger players grow.”

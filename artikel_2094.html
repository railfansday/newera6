O'Neill explains second Celtic star missed Falkirk match due to family bereavement

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Martin O'Neill has confirmed Oliver Sorensen missed Celtic's match against Falkirk due to a family bereavement.

The on-loan Parma midfielder was not involved in the matchday squad for the trip to the Falkirk Stadium on Sunday, as Celtic fell to a 2-0 loss.

After the match, O'Neill confirmed the sad news that Sorensen was absent due to mourning a family bereavement.

Auston Trusty was not involved for Celtic as he grieves the tragic loss of his father.

O'Neill said: "We've had a couple of boys who are away, Auston Trusty is away at this minute.

"I think with his father dying and Oliver Sorensen had a family bereavement as well too."

Reflecting on the defeat against Falkirk, O'Neill said: "Yeah, naturally disappointing. I'm disappointed to have lost the game, first of all.

"But we had a lot of chances. A lot of chances. The goalkeeper was magnificent all afternoon. Yeah, but naturally we've lost the game, so it's very disappointing.

"They were very poor [goals], from our viewpoint. The first one, we hadn't cleared it, and the second one we've allowed somebody to just run through and pick a spot.

"But we had some really great chances, we just weren't able to convert."

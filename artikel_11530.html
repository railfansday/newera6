UTSA opens season with lopsided loss at San Jose State

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

San Jose State dominated on the interior to send UTSA to a season-opening loss, as the Spartans toppled the Roadrunners 95-74 on Monday at Provident Credit Union Event Center in San Jose, Calif.

San Jose State closed the opening half with a 17-2 run across just more than five minutes, taking an 18-point lead into the break. The Spartans kept the cushion at double digits the rest of the way, leading by as many as 27 points.

Joey Chammaa scored 27 and Mekhi Mason added 26 as San Jose State scored 46 points in the paint, shot 23 of 30 (76.7%) on two-point shots and built a 43-22 advantage in rebounding.

With Claunch suspended following DWI arrest, UTSA faces new questions

Preston Turner and Shey Eberwein each scored 15 points to lead UTSA, while Isaiah Coleman had 12 and KJ Tenner notched 11 with a team-high six assists. The Roadrunners also forced 21 turnovers.

UTSA was led by acting coach Nick Bowman, who stepped into the role when coach Austin Claunch was suspended Wednesday following his arrest on suspicion of DWI.

The Roadrunners will play their home opener against VMI at 4 p.m. Saturday.

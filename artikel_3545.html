The Jewelry at the Center of Humphrey Bogart and Lauren Bacall’s On-Set Romance

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

In many photographs from the 1950s, Lauren Bacall appears alongside Humphrey Bogart with a small golden object between her lips. Curiously, it is a small gold whistle hanging from a bracelet. Not exactly the kind of accessory one would expect to find in a Hollywood star’s jewelry box. Yet, for Bacall, that particular piece held a very special meaning. Bogart himself gave it to her, inspired by a line the actor delivered in the film where the two met and fell in love.

In 1944, Bacall was just 19 years old when she made her film debut alongside Bogart in To Have and Have Not, directed by Howard Hawks. Bogart, 44 at the time, was already one of Hollywood’s most famous actors. On set, their relationship developed into something more than professional. They fell in love, and in May 1945, got married.

It was here that the origin story of the unusual bracelet began, with a line from Bacall that became one of old Hollywood’s most memorable: “You do know how to whistle, don’t you Steve? You just put your lips together and blow.” The scene, a candid moment of trust between the two characters, was a standout in the film, and it was this line that inspired the idea for the bracelet.

The result was a 14-karat-gold bracelet with a small pendant whistle, also made of gold and fully functional. The piece has a rather minimalist look, with smooth, polished surfaces and no particular decorations, except for a few personal engravings.

On one side of the tag are the words “Betty Bacall,” a reference to the actor’s original name, Betty Joan Perske, and on the other, “The Whistler.” On the whistle itself, the initials “B&B”—for Bogart and Bacall—are engraved, along with the number “21,” the meaning of which is still a mystery.

Bacall wore the bracelet in her daily life, carrying with her that small memento of the film that marked the beginning of her relationship with Bogart. In 1954, during a television interview with journalist Edward R. Murrow, the actor herself revealed the meaning of the whistle, explaining its connection to the famous line from To Have and Have Not.

Decades later, in September 2023, the bracelet made headlines again, when it was auctioned by Julien’s Auctions. The initial estimate ranged from $100,000 to $200,000, and on September 8, it sold for $158,750—a substantial sum for a small gold whistle. Though, considering who it belonged to—and especially who gave it to her—it’s not all that surprising.

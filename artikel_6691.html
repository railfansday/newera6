Beyoncé Shares a Rare Look at Blue Ivy Carter’s Casual Style in a New Family Photo

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Earlier this month, Blue Ivy Carter joined her father, Jay-Z, during night one of his Jay-Z 30 Tour in Los Angeles, playing piano for “The Story of O.J.” and “Dead Presidents.” While the 14-year-old didn’t take the stage for night two, she was still at the show, supporting him.

Beyoncé recently posted personal family photos of the night on her site, including a shot of Blue with her and her mother, Tina Knowles. Blue is dressed casually, wearing a striped jersey with shorts and tan boots. Beyoncé, meanwhile, opted for a LaPointe look from the brand’s spring/summer 2027 collection.

Last week, Beyoncé also posted photos from night one of Jay-Z’s Los Angeles tour stop on her site, where she included another shot of Blue with her and Knowles. The three women held hands backstage.

Blue is eager to perform, her dad told GQ back in March. He reflected on her musical talent, saying, “Blue is a crazy pianist, but she won’t let us get her a teacher. She doesn’t want it to be a job. But she has perfect pitch. If she hears a song, she’ll be like ‘Play it again’ and then she’ll teach herself. That’s just talent, she doesn’t work at that. She worked at this [being a dancer on Beyoncé’s tours], and it makes me proud that she fought for something that she really wanted to do. I don’t think we’re going to be able to get her off that stage now.”

Beyoncé also spoke about her eldest daughter’s interest in performing during her 2024 GQ interview. “Blue is an artist,” she began. “She has great taste in music and fashion. She is a fantastic editor, painter, and actress. She has been creating characters since she was 3. She’s a natural, but I did not want Blue onstage [dancing during my tours]. Blue wanted it for herself. She took it seriously and she earned it. And most importantly, she had fun! We all watched her grow more and more every night before our eyes.”

She added that she still tries to keep Blue and her youngest children, Rumi and Sir, out of the spotlight as they grow up. “One thing I’ve worked extremely hard on is making sure my kids can have as much normalcy and privacy as possible, ensuring my personal life isn’t turned into a brand,” Beyoncé said. “It’s very easy for celebrities to turn our lives into performance art. I have made an extreme effort to stay true to my boundaries and protect myself and my family. No amount of money is worth my peace.”

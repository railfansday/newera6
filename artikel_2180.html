England vs Czechia: TV channel and kick-off time as Chelsea’s Morgan Rogers eyes Wembley start

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Chelsea supporters can watch England face Czechia on ITV1 and ITVX tonight, with Morgan Rogers hoping to return to the starting line-up at Wembley.

The attacker scored England’s final goal in their 7-0 victory over Croatia on Saturday after coming off the bench.

England’s Nations League fixture kicks off at 7.45pm BST on Tuesday 6 October at Wembley Stadium.

The FA’s official match guide confirms the date, venue and UK broadcast arrangements.

This is England’s final match of the extended September and October international window.

ITV1 will show the match live, with coverage beginning at 7pm BST, 45 minutes before kick-off.

Viewers can also stream the broadcast through ITVX’s live ITV service.

Rogers started England’s 2-0 victory in Czechia last week before Thomas Tuchel named him among the substitutes against Croatia.

He entered that match in the 69th minute and scored after collecting Jude Bellingham’s pass and rounding the goalkeeper.

Tonight’s starting XI remains unconfirmed. The FA expects team news around 75 minutes before kick-off, approximately 6.30pm BST.

After this fixture, attention returns to Chelsea’s next run of matches, beginning against Bournemouth at Stamford Bridge on Saturday.

If You're Driving With a Suspended License, Maybe Don't Do 105 MPH in This Particular Chevy Camaro

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The driver was arrested after Arizona police reportedly recorded him traveling 30 mph over the limit, with a license suspended three times over.

If you've already been cited multiple times this year for driving on a suspended license, you shouldn't be behind the wheel. You certainly shouldn't be driving a sixth-generation Chevrolet Camaro with no hood, no front clip, and a massive Hi-Ram intake sticking out of the engine bay. And if you insist on driving that Camaro anyway, you probably shouldn't be doing 105 miles an hour in a 75-mph zone. Well, one Arizona driver allegedly managed to do all three, and his reward was a trip to jail.

According to the Arizona Department of Public Safety (AZDPS), a Highway Patrol State Trooper was on traffic duty on Interstate 10 on Sunday, October 25, when spotted the hard-to-miss Camaro in the left lane allegedly traveling at more than 100 mph. And in a delicious twist, in order to catch up to the speeding car, the police deployed a sixth-generation Camaro of their own.

The trooper pulled the Camaro over and asked the driver if he had a valid driver's license. The driver reportedly responded that he did, but unfortunately, a records check revealed that wasn't exactly true. It turns out the driver apparently had quite a history of license suspensions: according to authorities, his license was suspended in January 2026 for financial responsibility issues, followed by another suspension in March for street racing, and a third in June for failing to appear in court.

When the trooper asked whether he knew his license was suspended, the driver reportedly admitted that he believed it was. (Apparently, that wasn't enough to convince him to stop driving his attention-magnet Camaro.) And this time, it cost him more than just a citation; the driver was arrested on charges of criminal speeding and driving on a suspended license.

If three license suspensions and multiple citations weren't enough to teach this driver a lesson, perhaps time behind bars will do the trick. In the meantime, the winner of this Camaro-versus-Camaro showdown wasn't the one with the massive aftermarket intake; it was the one with the flashing lights.

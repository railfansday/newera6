Leeds United confirm death of fan who suffered medical emergency before Manchester United game

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Leeds United have confirmed the death of a supporter who suffered a medical emergency before last month’s game against Manchester United.

Colin Westby, a season ticket holder, was transferred to hospital following the incident on Sunday, October 18.

He was placed into a medically induced coma and folllowing a period of unresponsiveness, the decision to withdraw life support was made.

Colin was 68 years old and Leeds will pay tribute to him in the 68th minute of their game against Tottenham Hotspur on Saturday (November 7).

A club spokesperson said: “Leeds United are heartbroken to learn of the passing of supporter Colin Westby at the age of 68.

“Colin suffered a medical emergency ahead of our Premier League fixture with Manchester United on Sunday 18 October at Elland Road

“After being transferred to hospital, he was placed into a medically induced coma and sadly following a period of unresponsiveness, the decision was made to withdraw life support.

“Colin was a long-standing season ticket holder at the club, attending matches for over 50 years. The thoughts of everyone at Leeds United are with Colin’s family and friends at this sad time.

“We will pay tribute to Colin in the 68th minute of our next Premier League fixture with Tottenham Hotspur, on Saturday, November 7.”

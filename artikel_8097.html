FBI Operation Summer Heat 2.0 Nets Thousands of Violent Crime Arrests

In four months, the violent crime initiative also seized enough fentanyl to kill 52 million people

The FBI and its law enforcement partners are announcing the results of Summer Heat 2.0, a four-month violent crime initiative that resulted in 8,927 arrests, the seizure of deadly fentanyl and cocaine, and 5,013 dangerous illegally possessed weapons taken off American streets.

“This year’s Summer Heat was our second straight nationwide offensive to crush violent crime all over the country, and the results were outstanding,” said FBI Director Kash Patel. “Even after last year’s unprecedented numbers, all 56 FBI field offices stepped up and delivered even higher numbers this year—arresting almost 9,000 subjects, up 3% from Summer Heat last year; seizing 63% more fentanyl; identifying or locating 1,410 children, 34% higher than last year; and recovering over 5,000 weapons, up 120% from 2025. Major cities across America saw increases as high as 80% in arrests compared to last summer alone. When this FBI elevated ‘crushing violent crime’ to one of our top priorities, we moved 2,000 FBI personnel out of Washington and permanently into the field—making the decisive call to go on offense. This FBI isn’t waiting for violent criminals, drug traffickers, and weapons dealers to devastate our communities any longer. As a result, under the Trump administration’s leadership, we have achieved the most prolific reduction in violent crime in U.S. history.”  Between June 1 and September 20, the FBI and its 56 field offices surged resources alongside state and local partners to execute federal warrants on violent criminals and fugitives, dismantle violent gangs and transnational criminal organizations, identify and rescue child victims, and resolve violent crime cases in Indian Country.

The FBI’s Summer Heat 2.0 initiative achieved additional victories with 687 kilograms of fentanyl seized: an amount equal to 52 million lethal doses. Of the 8,927 arrests, more than 5,900 arrests fell under the FBI’s violent crime and gang program.

A Homeland Security Task Force (HSTF) takedown across two states, Operation Turf War, kicked off the FBI’s 2026 Summer Heat initiative. FBI Pittsburgh, in coordination with West Virginia’s HSTF, netted 32 narcotics and weapons-related arrests. In a separate effort in July 2026, FBI Los Angeles executed 17 federal arrest warrants and 49 search warrants in connection with Operation Hard Ball. The operation united FBI Los Angeles with law enforcement from Canada and Europe to investigate an organized crime group accused of a litany of criminal acts. And in yet another example of the success of Operation Summer Heat, FBI San Juan and its partners led Operation Taking Back West, which resulted in the indictment of 81 members of a violent criminal gang.   The FBI first launched the Summer Heat initiative for three months in 2025 to deploy a multi-pronged offensive to crush violent crime. The announcement of Operation Summer Heat 2.0 results follows on the heels of historic drops in crime across the U.S., according to the FBI’s 2025 UCR Summary of Reported Crimes in the Nation.

Coby White to make season debut for Hornets after missing team's first 7 games with strained calf

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Charlotte Hornets are getting one piece of their projected starting guard tandem back in the lineup.

Point guard Coby White was not listed on Monday's injury report, and the team announced he will make his 2026-27 season debut versus the Detroit Pistons.

White, 26, strained his left calf during training camp, sidelining him through the preseason and the Hornets' first seven games of the regular season. In his absence, Dennis Schröder has played well, averaging 11.7 points and 5.4 assists.

However, Charlotte presumably signed White to a 3-year, $74 million contract so he could be the starting point guard. The Hornets acquired White from the Chicago Bulls at last season's trade deadline, and in 21 games, he averaged 15.6 points with 3 assists while shooting 39% on 110 3-point attempts.

In addition to re-signing White to a new contract, the Hornets made his role clear by trading point guard LaMelo Ball to the Minnesota Timberwolves in exchange for center Naz Reid, several draft picks and first-round swaps.

White's best season was during the 2024-25 campaign when he took on more of a scoring role for the Bulls. He averaged 20.4 points and 4.5 assists, shooting 37% on a career-high 584 3-point shots. Over the past three seasons, White has scored 19.1 points per game with 4.6 assists and 3.9 rebounds.

While he may not be as much of a scorer or playmaker as Ball, the Hornets are hoping White will be more consistent. The eight-year veteran has shot 52% or better during the past four seasons. Additionally, Charlotte is looking for Kon Knueppel to assume more of a playmaking role when he returns.

Knueppel will comprise the other part of the Hornets' starting backcourt once he becomes available. He is out for Monday's game, but is close to returning from a strained left hamstring he sustained before training camp. Knueppel was assigned to the team's G League affiliate to practice last week before being recalled to the Hornets.

Charlotte announced on Monday that the team picked up Knueppel's third-year option for $11 million. He also has a $13.9 million team option for 2027-28 as part of his four-year rookie contract.

Following Monday's matchup with the Pistons, the Hornets (3-4) visit the Brooklyn Nets (1-5) on Wednesday and host the Boston Celtics (5-1) on Friday.

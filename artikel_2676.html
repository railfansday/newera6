Bothfeld scores twice as Nauset girls soccer beats Vineyard

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Nauset girls soccer team closed out Cape & Islands Atlantic Division play in convincing fashion, pulling away from Martha's Vineyard for a 4-1 victory.

After a patient start from both teams, the Warriors broke through in the 29th minute when Vivienne Ryder found Maddie Coccoro, who raced 50 yards down the sideline before finishing over the goalkeeper.

Caroline Bothfeld doubled the lead seven minutes into the second half off a pass from Chloe Burns, then added her second goal in the 68th minute by finishing Peyton Hill's free kick.

Freshman Talia Nadeau capped Nauset's scoring with an impressive 70-yard counterattack, dribbling through four defenders before scoring at the near post.

Martha's Vineyard (9-9) scored in the 79th minute, but Nauset secured the win to improve to 12-3-3 overall and 9-2-1 in league play.

Nantucket 1, St. John Paul II 0: The Whalers (6-11) snapped a four-game losing streak by beating the Lions (3-13-1).

Ariel Thompson, Serafina Mecca, Mila Smith, Florence Carlson and Brynn Robinson all stood out for the Lions.

Bourne 1, Dennis-Yarmouth 0: The Canalmen (3-13-2) ended their season with just one loss in their final four games, while the Dolphins finished the season at 1-15-2.

Sienna Silva scored the lone goal in the first half, while Addison Parr made several good saves to earn the shutout in goal.

Nantucket 4, Abington 2: The Whalers (12-4-2) ended their regular season with a big win over fifth-ranked Abington in Division 4.

Martha's Vineyard 7, Sandwich 0: The Vineyarders (10-3-4) have won four of their last five games, while the Blue Knights finished their season at 1-12-2.

Archbishop Williams 2, Monomoy 1: The Sharks (9-5-4) finished the regular season with their first loss since Sept. 18.

Milford 5, Barnstable 1: The Red Hawks closed out their season at 4-13-1.

Bourne 1, Mashpee 0: Ben Buckley scored the lone goal of the game for the Canalmen (5-11-2) as they beat the Falcons (3-13-2).

See Hoda Kotb & Ex Joel Schiffman Halloween Reunion With Daughters

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Hoda Kotb and her ex-fiancé Joel Schiffman wore matching deviled eggs costumes while celebrating Halloween with their daughters Haley, 9, and Hope, 7.

Hoda Kotb and her ex-fiancé Joel Schiffman had an egg-cellent Halloween together.

The former couple reunited to spend the spooky holiday with their daughters Haley, 9, and Hope, 7, wearing matching deviled eggs costumes as they went trick-or-treating as a family.

In a video posted to Kotb's Instagram Oct. 31, the Today alum and Schiffman donned red devil horns and large cutouts of an egg, while their kids dressed as Poppy and Viva from the Trolls movies.

Posing in front of her home, Kotb wrote in the caption, "Happy halloween."

And this wasn't the first time the 62-year-old reunited with Schiffman, 68, to celebrate a special occasion with Haley and Hope. In September, the two were on hand to send their girls off to their first day of school.

"It’s here," Kotb captioned Instagram photos of the family drop-off. "4th and 2nd grade! Whaaaatttt?"

And though it's been four years since the former morning show host ended her engagement to Schiffman, her bond with the financier will always remain strong.

“I loved Joel then, and I love Joel now,” Kotb said on Today last year. “I just had to remind myself that it was a different kind of love.”

In fact, she believes their current dynamic is "the right relationship for us to have."

“We will co-parent in a beautiful way,” Kotb shared, “but I also think our ‘us’ is more of a, like, we’re dear friends now. And that’s beautiful, and it’s enjoyable and it feels right."

That's also because she and Schiffman are "honest" about their co-parenting relationship.

“Kids catch pretending. They know that Joel and I care so much for each other, and they also know that we love them dearly," Kotb added. “I really adore Joel, and they know it. I’m not pretending, I’m not faking it. So, once they see that, they go, ‘Ah, OK. This is good. This is good for us.’”

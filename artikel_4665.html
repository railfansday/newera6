Hotter Honda Civic Type R HRC Is ‘In Development’ for American Buyers

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

And the brand is using the SEMA Show to show off an electrified Baja Trophy Truck and a Prelude built for racing, too.

Honda still has not actually confirmed plans to sell a hotter Civic Type R with an HRC badge on it, but the Japanese brand has spent much of the past year hinting at just that. A concept has been shown off; development has been recorded in a YouTube video; and a window for a reveal shared by an executive has even come and gone.

The latest development, though, is the clearest sign yet of a coming retail product. Officially, Honda is confirming that the Civic Type R HRC Concept (seen above) is “in development for the U.S. market.”

Granted, that stops just short of a confirmation of plans to actually build it, but a statement of intent to develop a car for a specific market is the clear final step before announcing that said model will reach those shores as a production offering. Honda will also be bringing the concept to the 2026 SEMA Show in Las Vegas, so American buyers can get a first look at it next week.

While the concept has been shown off a few times, Honda still has not said exactly what is different between the HRC Type R and its more basic relative. The development video offered some hints on the aero side, showing off a new front bumper design along with a changed rear wing. Both the version shown initially and the slightly-less-camouflaged version shown in that video highlight what looks like some sort of winglet above the back of the flared front fenders, an aggressive element that could be the visual signature of the HRC variant.

In addition to the Type R HRC Concept, Honda is bringing a few other offerings to SEMA. One is the latest Passport trophy truck, set to be the first hybrid to ever enter the SCORE World Desert Championship. Two-time Baja 1000 winner Bryce Menzies is taking it to that race in what Honda says is an attempt to claim an overall win.

Honda is bringing a club racer based on the Prelude, too. This is a Hasport Performance build, replacing the transmission-free hybrid setup in the Prelude with both the 2.0-liter turbocharged I-4 engine and six-speed manual transmission from a Civic Type R. While this build is sure to appeal to Honda fans who want a Prelude Type R, it appears to be more a proof of concept for tuners than anything resembling a hint at a product that the brand insists is not in the pipeline any time soon.

It's time for the world of high quality wired earphones (or IEMs) to get excited, as it's the annual 11/11 sales period, and there are some awesome discounts on already well-priced products.

This year, I want to focus on six of my favourite IEM deals from HiFiGo – one of the biggest operators in the space. These are all earphones that I've actually tried and tested myself, and that I feel hit the sweet spot of excellent sound quality, build quality and value for money.

My absolute favourite would be the Juzear Light Shuttle, which I reviewed only last month. It is my 'neutral with bass-boost' release of the year, and it's currently down from $190 to $155 over at AliExpress.

All six of these IEMs are featured either on my list of the best budget IEMs currently on the market, or my pick of the best IEM under $500.

This is the last IEM that I reviewed, and I was really impressed by its warm/neutral sound, with a textured bass response and clear, clean, yet not over the top treble. Plenty to like about this one, especially its new price of $121.

This is the cheapest IEM on the list, and it's a really good buy. Made of all-metal shells, it's more durable than most $40 IEMs out there, and its take on a V-shape sound signature is fun and energetic. A good all-rounder, though things do get a little spicy up in the treble some times.

This 8-driver IEM is the most expensive IEM on the list, but it's also my favourite. It does musical with smooth upper mids like few other IEMs that I've heard, and there's a fairly big bass shelf in there too for fun. This is smooth and exact, and $251 is an absolute bargain down from $389. Read my Aful Performer 8S review for more.

This two dynamic driver and four balanced armature hybrid IEM is a bit of a classic – and for good reason. It's one of the first to popularise the 'neutral with bass boost' sound signature, otherwise known as New Meta. That means its well balanced in the mids, but has the excitement of elevated bass. One of the best IEMs on the list.

I haven't reviewed the 1900 yet, but I was impressed with its bright/clear sound. It reminded me of its more expensive brother the Binary EP321 – but $100 cheaper. At $157 I think this is another super strong option for the HiFiGo sale.

The MiracleOne is the most recent IEM that I've received, and there's lots of like. The comfort is S tier, and that goes a long way when you've got a crowd-pleasing sound to back it up. Honestly I think this single DD isn't the best option at its retail price of $200, but at $155 it becomes a compelling option.

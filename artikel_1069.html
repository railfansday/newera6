The Premier League table looks healthy for sixth-place Brentford but that will change quickly if they don't halt their winless run, with just two points separating them and Tottenham Hotspur, in 14th.

Keith Andrews' side have had a strange start to the season, with just two wins but also only one defeat from their eight league games, while they have not tasted victory since beating Chelsea 3-0 on September 18.

The Bees suffered a disappointing Carabao Cup loss at Sunderland in midweek, so the visit of struggling Nottingham Forest looks like the perfect opportunity to get back on track.

Yehor Yarmolyuk serves the final game of his three-match suspension following his red card against Liverpool earlier this month, while Josh Dasilva, Antoni Milambo, Rico Henry and Kaye Furo remain sidelined due to injury.

Forest start the weekend in the relegation zone after taking just six points from their first eight fixtures, yet boss Oliver Glasner remains confident they are on the right track.

He is justified in thinking that, with his side performing better than their results suggest, but they need to start converting their chances after scoring only six times so far this season.

It is therefore worrying that Forest could be without striker Dan Ndoye, who is doubtful due to a hamstring issue, while the trip to Brentford may come too early for midfielder Nicolas Dominguez.

There is better news surrounding Neco Williams, who is available despite suffering a knock during last Friday's 1-1 draw at Ipswich Town, where he scored the opener to help end Forest's three-match losing run.

The Bees will be confident of taking all three points against a Forest side who are struggling in front of goal.

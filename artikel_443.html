These string lights are IP67 rated and can withstand extreme temperatures, so you can use them just about anywhere. That said, you’ll need an outdoor outlet, and if you aim to get a good outdoor lighting display going, I recommend a weatherproof electrical box.

While it may take you a few minutes to get your head around, I recommend using the Govee app because it is packed full of fun features. There are well over 100 presets to choose from, but you can also have the app generate effects for you based on your interests.

They may have Christmas in the name, but these string lights are so versatile that you can find effects to suit any scenario, from spooky Halloween animations to classy white light that works year-round. I've used these outdoors for a couple of years now, and they’re still going strong.

Google rolling out software updates for these Nest Cam and Doorbell models

Alongside updates to several of its other smart home devices, Google is rolling out software updates to several Nest Cam and Doorbell models.

Google does note that these updates are only available if you have migrated older Nest devices to the Google Home app, a process that’s been widely available to all users and Nest devices for months, if not years.

Also noteworthy here is that Google is referring to Nest Hello, its original doorbell camera, as “Nest Doorbell (wired, 1st gen).”

Google details a changelog including improvements to Wi-Fi reconnections, faster Night Vision switching, and a fix for the status light on Nest Cam IQ.

These updates are rolling out now and will install automatically in the background.

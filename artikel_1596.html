Juventus vs Lazio: 1-1 Draw Predicted in Turin

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Juventus host unbeaten Lazio at the Allianz Stadium on Sunday, 18 October, with Luciano Spalletti’s side five points behind the visitors; Sports Mole predicts a 1-1 draw.

The 7.45pm fixture closes the roundup’s Sunday Serie A programme and gives Juventus the chance to cut into Lazio’s lead. The Bianconeri come into the match after European action on Thursday, while Lazio arrive with their league unbeaten run intact.

Sports Mole does not publish confirmed or projected starting elevens for either side in its roundup. It describes Juventus as having excelled in Europe and Lazio as in-form, but provides no formation, selection details or squad update to support a more specific lineup call.

There is also no injury list or confirmed availability information for either club in the preview. The focus is the matchup between Spalletti and Gennaro Gattuso, rather than the personnel decisions each coach may make.

Juventus’ recent league draw at Cagliari offers some context for the challenge of turning performances into points; the result left them looking for greater consistency. The match also comes after the Bianconeri’s European outing, a demanding schedule detail noted in the preview.

The prediction is a stalemate, with the source expecting Lazio to frustrate Spalletti and leave Turin with a hard-earned point. Its reasoning centres on an intriguing contest between the two former Italy coaches and Juventus’ finishing, which it suggests could again prove costly.

That forecast makes the game less a straightforward home-favourite call than a test of whether Juventus can convert their European momentum into a league win. Lazio’s unbeaten start and five-point advantage give the visitors a strong position, but the prediction stops short of backing them to take all three points.

Elsewhere in the same Sunday roundup, Sports Mole also previews AC Milan against Atalanta BC. For Juventus, the immediate target is to close the five-point gap on Lazio; the predicted draw would leave that margin unchanged.

Country Star Jessie James Decker Sprays Passenger’s Bare Foot That Invades Her Armrest

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Country singer Jessie James Decker shared video of herself spraying the bare foot of a passenger that had invaded her plane armrest. She holds her nose, takes aim with a small mint-green bottle, and the foot quickly disappears. Then she covers her mouth and laughs. Her Instagram video says, “Someone’s foot smells so bad on the plane.” She asked her followers how they would have handled it. Several suspected she was just messing with her husband, former football player Eric Decker. She refuted that: “Have you seen Eric’s feet? Toeeeee UP.” Jessie James Decker’s airplane video Decker is a country pop singer whose 2017 album Southern Girl City Lights debuted at number one on Billboard’s country albums chart. Her sister is Sydney Rae Bass, whose husband Anthony Bass complained that United Airlines made his pregnant wife clean up their child’s spilled popcorn a few years ago. In April, an American Airlines first class passenger shoved a woman’s feet off their armrest, only to see her put them up on the window. The suggestions in that discussion included spraying cologne. Here Decker puts that into practice. Somehow though this keeps happening? I’d want that foot gone, too. I’d ask the passenger to move it, and get a flight attendant involved if they refused. Some Facebook commenters insist this isn’t the crew’s job. They shouldn’t have to deal with it, and neither should a passenger. Each of us is supposed to contain our bodies within the space of our assigned seats.

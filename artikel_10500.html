Alphabet reports Q3 2026 revenue of $127.6 billion

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Alphabet today announced Q3 2026 earnings with $127.6 billion in revenue. These numbers range from July to September.

Revenue is up 25% from $102.3 billion in Q3 2025, with operating income at $40.58 billion and net income of $26.17 billion for this quarter (versus $34.9 billion a year ago). For comparison, Alphabet reported $119.8 billion in revenue and $112.1 billion in net income last quarter.

We had a strong quarter and are now on a half-trillion dollar revenue run rate. Our deep full stack approach is driving momentum across the business, with Alphabet revenues growing 25% year-over year.

Our AI investments are driving overall Search query growth, including commercial queries. In fact, our year-overyear query growth rate is the highest it’s been in the last five years.

Google Cloud revenues grew 103%, powered by huge demand for our AI infrastructure and AI solutions. We’re also seeing strong demand for our TPU systems across a wide range of customers.

Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon are now leading at the frontier. Overall, our first-party model APIs are now processing nearly 32 billion tokens per minute, more than four times what we processed a year ago.

We’re excited by the range of big opportunities ahead of us.”

In Q3 2026, YouTube ad revenue was $11.2 billion (versus $10.26 billion a year ago), while Cloud reported $30.71 billion (compared to $15.16 billion).

“Google subscriptions, platforms, and devices” — which includes hardware, Play Store, and non-advertising YouTube revenues — reported $14.29 billion, compared to $12.87 billion the same quarter last year.

“Other Bets” continues to lose money. It reported $372 million in revenue compared to $344 million in Q3 of 2025. It lost $2.04 billion compared to $1.4 billion in the same quarter of last year.

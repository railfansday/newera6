What Time Should You Eat Thanksgiving Dinner?

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Every family has Thanksgiving rules. Some are spoken (“Dinner’s at 3 sharp!”), others understood (Mom gets the wings), and plenty are hotly contested. We asked Bon Appétit staffers, friends, and their kids to settle the holiday’s thorniest questions. Turns out, there’s no one right way to Thanksgiving—just plenty of opinions.

If you don’t cook, you’d better clean! —Meredith, 41, Golden Rule follower

As someone who hosts, I have a system, and I want everyone to stay out of my way. —Catherine, 41, channeling inner peace

My mom makes me dust everything. And be honest, no one is going to see the baseboards. —Alice, 8, at your service (begrudgingly)

Guests should bring dessert or flowers. Let me figure out the wine! —Rachel, 41, future somm

You need to look nice for Grandma. —David, 48, upstanding gentleman

Bring out the stretchy pants! —Haleigh, 31, comfort queen

Brown sweater and, like, beige pants. —Amanda, 12, loves a uniform

Federal Judge Alvin Hellerstein of New York dies at 92

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

In this courtroom sketch, U.S. District Judge Alvin Hellerstein of the Southern District of New York presides during a pretrial hearing in a drug trafficking case for former Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, on July 22, 2026, in New York. (Photo by Jane Rosenberg via the Associated Press)

U.S. District Judge Alvin Hellerstein of the Southern District of New York died Wednesday at age 92.

Hellerstein was known for presiding over the federal narcoterrorism conspiracy case against ousted Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, according to a story from Law.com. He also oversaw more than 14,000 lawsuits that were filed by victims of the Sept. 11 terror attacks.

On a personal level, the judge is being remembered for his empathy and morality, which was informed by his Jewish faith.

“He didn’t shy away from pushing the boundaries of the law if he thought they would be unjust otherwise,” Evan Farber, a partner at Loeb & Loeb who once clerked for Hellerstein, told Law.com.

Hellerstein graduated from Columbia Law School in 1956, and he became a clerk for U.S. District Judge Edmund Palmieri of the Southern District of New York, Law.com reports. He was a military lawyer with the U.S. Army and a partner and a litigator at Stroock & Stroock & Lavan before being nominated to the federal bench by then-President Bill Clinton. He was confirmed unanimously in 1998.

He took senior status in 2011, but he never slowed down, Law.com reports.

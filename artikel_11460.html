Consider this your holiday-season reminder: Jingle Ball 2026 is almost here, and tickets are officially on the radar. The iHeartRadio Jingle Ball tour runs Dec. 1 through Dec. 17, visiting nine cities across the country. While tickets are available through primary ticket seller Ticketmaster, resale marketplaces like StubHub, SeatGeek and Vivid Seats are worth watching for last-minute listings and potential discounts. The 2026 Jingle Ball tour lineup varies by city, with major names including Olivia Rodrigo, Jonas Brothers, Hilary Duff, Charlie Puth, Sombr, RAYE, Alex Warren, KATSEYE, OneRepublic, Ludacris, Tove Lo and Jason Derulo. Los Angeles gets Olivia Rodrigo, while New York welcomes Charlie Puth and Sombr. Other highlights include KATSEYE in Dallas/Fort Worth, Alex Warren in Detroit and Jonas Brothers in Washington, D.C.

Don’t be confused if you see a local radio station’s name instead of iHeartRadio when searching for Jingle Ball tickets. Each city has its own local radio station that organizes the show as part of the iHeartRadio network, so the station’s name may vary depending on the city.

StubHub is a popular resale marketplace where fans and professional ticket sellers list seats for concerts and other live entertainment. Since sellers choose their own prices, shoppers can browse options above or below the original ticket price. Its FanProtect Guarantee offers added purchase reassurance by helping buyers secure valid tickets. It’s a useful option for comparing available seats and tracking prices as show dates approach. As of publication, iHeartRadio’s 2026 Jingle Ball tickets start at $42 at StubHub.

SeatGeek gives shoppers another place to compare 2026 Jingle Ball tickets through a resale marketplace that lets buyers browse and compare available listings. Its Deal Score feature helps buyers evaluate listings by weighing factors such as ticket price and seat location, making it easier to spot budget-friendly options. Eligible purchases are also covered by its Buyer Guarantee, which promises valid entry and ticket delivery before the event. As of publication, 2026 iHeartRadio Jingle Ball tickets start at $36 at SeatGeek.

Vivid Seats connects concertgoers with independent ticket sellers and offers a 100% Buyer Guarantee covering ticket authenticity, delivery and valid entry. Shoppers who qualify can also receive a 10% discount, including military personnel, first responders, medical providers, nurses, teachers and students. Verification through ID.me is required on its website. It’s another marketplace worth comparing when searching for available seats. As of publication, iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026 tickets start at $37 at Vivid Seats.

Jennie’s Halloween Costume Is a Throwback to One of the Most Iconic Y2K Movie Looks

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A lot of people want to be like Jennie, but this year, Jennie wanted to be like 2 Fast 2 Furious’s Suki.

Jennie took it back to the early aughts for Halloween, channeling the street racer played by Devon Aoki in the 2003 film from the franchise. For the occasion, the singer recreated the character’s signature pink-and-purple ensemble, complete with hot-pink corseted chaps. She finished the look with a long, straight wig that mirrored the character’s in a warm brown-blond shade and a statement belt featuring bedazzled hardware, channeling Aoki’s original styling.

In the film, Suki is part of a crew of street racers in Miami who compete in high-stakes races, and her character is particularly special in part because she brings a distinctly colorful, playful wardrobe to the series. Her pink chaps in particular became a defining part of her on-screen identity.

In a 2020 retrospective of Aoki’s most iconic outfits, i-D singled out that ensemble as her most memorable from the film. More than two decades after the movie’s release, the outfit still has a hold on pop culture.

Jennie’s carousel also offered a glimpse at the festivities surrounding the costume. Among the photos were moments with Charli XCX and other friends at Kendall Jenner’s Halloween and birthday party in Los Angeles, held at Bar Marmont on October 30. Charli also arrived in a movie-inspired costume, channeling Lee Holloway, Maggie Gyllenhaal’s character from the 2002 film Secretary.

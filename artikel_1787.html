Milano is red and black: Instant reaction to AC Milan 3-1 Inter

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Stefano recorded his reaction to AC Milan’s crucial win against Inter straight after the full-time whistle, highlighting the individual brilliance that got them over the line.

The Derby della Madonnina is always a special game and one you want to win, but Milan were under a bit more pressure to do so this evening. An unbeaten run to maintain, a gap to decrease in the standings and a point to prove after a poor mid-week display.

Fortunately, they got almost everything spot on and were thus able to clinch a 3-1 win. Diego Moreira was the key player with one lovely goal and two assists, while several of his teammates also performed on a very high level.

Straight after the game finished, Stefano sat down to record his instant reaction to the game. It was definitely a fun one for him to dissect as Milan showed their quality in attack, while making all the right moves in the second half to kill Inter’s momentum completely.

AC Milan: Christian Pulišić extends until 2031

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

It’s now official: Christian Pulisic continues his adventure with AC Milan. The Lombard club announced this Friday the extension of the American international’s contract until June 30, 2031. Arriving from Chelsea in the summer of 2023, the 28-year-old striker quickly established himself as one of the major elements of the Rossonero offensive sector. In 141 appearances for Milan, he scored 44 goals, confirming his importance in the club’s project. A strong mark of confidence from the Milanese management, which intends to build its future around key players.

This extension also rewards the consistency and influence of Pulisic, who has returned to his best level after being disrupted by physical problems. Despite a complicated start to the season, the American has gradually regained an important place in the Milanese eleven, in particular thanks to his offensive efficiency. According to the Italian press, his new contract should allow him to receive an annual salary of five million euros net, excluding bonuses. With this commitment until 2031, AC Milan secures one of its main offensive assets for the coming seasons.

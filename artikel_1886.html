Manchester United injury update: Amad Diallo, Marcus Rashford, Luke Shaw latest news and return dates

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Manchester United’s winless run extended to nine matches on Saturday afternoon as they were beaten 2-0 at Chelsea, but the club’s injury situation did improve slightly.

Kobbie Mainoo made a long-awaited return to the squad, calling time on a weeks-long absence with a muscular problem. He came off the bench in the second half to formalise his return to action but could not affect a comeback as Lisandro Martinez was sent off, leaving his team-mates fighting an uphill battle.

Unfortunately for United, though, Marcus Rashford and Luke Shaw remained absent, with an important Champions League match against Roma to come on Tuesday.

With that in mind, here is all the latest Manchester United injury news and potential return dates...

Amad Diallo got the nod at Stamford Bridge and enjoyed a lively first-half before being forced off shortly before full-time.

He went down off the ball and received treatment before being replaced by Joshua Zirkzee. It appeared he had picked up a muscular issue of some description.

Carrick did not offer an update on his condition at full-time, suggesting his issue is not serious.

Potential return date: Tuesday, November 3 vs Roma (H)

There was optimism Marcus Rashford would be fit to face Chelsea on Saturday, but he was again left out of the matchday squad.

He has been struggling with a hip issue since early October but has returned to team training and seems to be closing in on a comeback.

Potential return date: Tuesday, November 3 vs Roma (H)

Luke Shaw may be approaching a return from the mysterious “knock” which has left him sidelined since United drew 1-1 with Tottenham earlier this month.

Carrick has previously used Mainoo as a yardstick to estimate his return, saying Shaw’s recovery would be longer than the midfielder’s, but by how much is not clear.

With Mainoo now fit again, though, Shaw may be approaching a return to the pitch.

Benjamin Sesko is facing an extended absence with the same shin injury which he picked up in spring and delayed his first appearance of this campaign.

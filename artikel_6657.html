Crispy Rice Omelet

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

On mornings when there’s almost nothing in the refrigerator, this crispy rice omelet is the back-pocket meal that’s even faster than turning to a drive-thru. Although it’s ready in just 10 minutes and made in a single pan, you’ll forget it was a struggle meal after just one bite of its craveable crunchy outside and soft, cheesy center. The perfect crispy rice omelet features gorgeously crunchy toasted rice and egg that’s well cooked through without becoming rubbery, and this recipe promises the perfect bite.

Use leftover cold rice:Rice dries out as it cools, making it easier to crisp up in the pan. If you try to use freshly cooked rice, it will still have a lot of extra moisture, making it prone to clumping up—and it might even burn before it completely crisps up.

Use the right pan:Either a nonstick or cast iron pan will work here. In testing, I found that cast iron gives a more consistent golden color and crunchier result, but can result in rice that’s almost too crunchy (if that’s a thing!)—and there was always the *tiny* risk of the rice sticking. The nonstick pan, on the other hand, gave that extra bit of insurance against sticking, and while it didn’t yield such a picture-perfect color, it did result in consistently crispy rice. Whichever you use, I highly recommend sticking to one that’s 8″: It’s the perfect size for this amount of rice and egg.

Pre-salt your eggs:Seasoning the eggs 5 to 10 minutes before they hit the pan allows the salt time to unravel the proteins, resulting in more tender eggs. I recommend this tip anytime you’re making scrambled eggs, and it’s an easy tip to incorporate here if you whisk and season the eggs before starting the rice.

Watch the heat:While it’s best to crisp the rice on medium-high, it’s important to drop the temperature before the eggs go in. If you cook them too hard and fast, they’ll be rubbery.

Delish Official:Eggland's Best Eggs are the Official Eggs of Delish. That means they're the only eggs we're using in our recipe content.

Storage:This omelet is actually great left over, since the rice remains crisp, even after refrigerating. Store in an airtight container for up to 3 days. You can rewarm it (gently!) in the microwave, or eat it cold.

Apple TV’s new crime thriller series ‘Nocturne’ is now streaming

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The first two episodes for Apple TV’s latest crime thriller, “Nocturne,” are now available to stream. Watch the trailer below.

Starring Liev Schreiber (Ray Donovan), Zazie Beetz (They Will Kill Yuo), and Stephen Graham (Adolescence), Nocturne is based on the bestselling crime novels “Lazarus” and “The Sandman,” by Lars Kepler.

The first two episodes of the show were directed by Tim Van Patten, whose previous credits include another Apple TV series, “Masters of the Air“.

Early reviews of Nocturne have been largely positive, with particular praise for Graham’s “chilling” performance.

Here’s Apple’s official synopsis of “Nocturne“:

The series tells the story of Jonah Lynn (Liev Schreiber), an ex-soldier turned homicide detective who, tired of working the tough streets of Philadelphia, moves to a small town in western Pennsylvania for a quiet life. But, as the town and his family come under attack from the diabolically cunning serial killer Jurek Walter (Stephen Graham), Jonah must protect all that he holds dear. When the desperate search for Jurek’s last missing victim forces Jonah to send his surrogate daughter, FBI Agent Saga Bauer (Zazie Beetz), up against Jurek, how far will Jonah go?

With the first two episodes now streaming on Apple TV, new episodes will air every Friday through December 25.

Apple TV is available for $14.99 per month or as part of the Apple One bundle. It features hit TV shows and movies like Ted Lasso, Severance, The Studio, The Morning Show, Shrinking, and Silo.

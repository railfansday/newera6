Zendaya Recreated an Iconic Halle Berry Fashion Moment for Halloween

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Zendaya didn’t miss out on all the fun this Halloween. The actress dressed up as one of Hollywood’s most enduring screen and style icons, Halle Berry, thanks to a special vintage pull.

On Saturday, Zendaya’s longtime stylist, Law Roach, shared a series of photos of the Dune actress recreating a particularly memorable early-aughts look that Berry wore: a butter yellow, brown, and white printed string bikini top with silver accents and matching low-rise, wide-leg trousers from Lanvin’s fall 2000 collection. Zendaya completed the look with pointed-toe heels.

Roach thanked Lanvin’s artistic director Peter Copping and Alix Long in his post. Copping took the creative reins at the French fashion house in 2024, and Cristina Ortiz was Lanvin’s womenswear designer when the fall 2000 collection debuted.

Berry first wore the two-piece to the New York premiere of X-Men in July 2000. She accessorized with a belly chain and tinted sunglasses.

Berry saw the tribute and posted her approval on X. “She won,” Berry wrote of Zendaya.

The fashion homage feels particularly fitting given that Zendaya has been drawing comparisons to Berry since debuting her pixie cut.

In fact, when Zendaya first unveiled the look at the 2026 Emmy Awards in September, Roach confirmed that the haircut was inspired by Berry’s iconic short hairstyle.

Berry later embraced the tribute, sharing Roach’s interview on her Instagram Story and writing that Zendaya “murdered the night.”

This isn’t the first time Zendaya has used fashion to pay tribute to another star. In 2013, she channeled the late Aaliyah for Keep a Child Alive’s Dream Halloween, recreating the singer’s iconic Tommy Hilfiger campaign look. More recently, she honored Cher at the 2024 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in a vintage Bob Mackie design.

Zendaya and Roach have more fashion moments ahead: The actress’s Dune: Part Three press tour is just kicking off.

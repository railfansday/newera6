Rian Johnson's New Apple Project Has Officially Conquered the Global Top 10

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Rian Johnson has spent the better part of two decades making movies that look absolutely gorgeous, whether it's the dusty, sun-drenched landscapes of Star Wars: The Last Jedi or the wonderfully stylish murder mysteries of his Knives Out franchise. His cinematography, thanks for his longtime collaborator Steve Yedlin, is always truly on point but, while most filmmakers are content to leave their tricks on the big screen, we're all getting the chance to make our lives look like a Rian Johnson movie.

Johnson's new iPhone photography app, Chromagraph Camera, has officially reached No. 1 on Apple's U.S. App Store Photo & Video chart, having also topped the overall paid-app rankings. Developed alongside Yedlin and programmer Eric Cameron, the $12.99 app allows users to capture photographs using image-processing techniques developed for Johnson's movies, including Knives Out, Glass Onion, and Wake Up Dead Man.

Chromagraph removes the filters that Apple adds automatically to its imagery and instead, it captures RAW image data and processes it independently, while using custom techniques to control color, contrast, texture, grain, and halation. In other words, the images come out looking like you're in a Johnson movie. Benoit Blanc would be thrilled.

Johnson revealed that the project began because he wanted a better way to take photographs on his own iPhone, which is something we've all experienced, really. Having a cinematographer as one of your best buds is handy, though, and so the pair decided they'd make their own app to make photography the way they think it should be.

The app takes an approach similar to traditional film photography, with users capturing their images before seeing the finished results in a separate gallery. From there, they can adjust the color, exposure, grain, and texture while preserving the original RAW files, before exporting their photographs as JPEG or HEIC images.

Yedlin has worked with Johnson throughout his filmmaking career, including on Looper, Star Wars: The Last Jedi, and the three Knives Out movies, and Chromagraph effectively brings a version of how he shoots movies into an iPhone. And it's gone viral already, with Johnson's original announcement reportedly attracting approximately six million views within its first day, and shooting it straight to number one in the App Store. Bodes well for Johnson's next movie!

Chromagraph Camera is available now on the Apple App Store for $12.99 as a one-time purchase.

All the ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 11 Wedding Day and Reunion Spoilers You So Desperately Crave, Right This Way

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Only one Boston couple reportedly says “I do”—and even that gets messy.

Another chaotic season of Love Is Blind has almost come to a close…but of course, no season is officially over until fans watch the reunion special. Even though the Love Is Blind season 11 reunion doesn’t drop until Sunday, November 8 at 9 p.m. ET, plenty of spoilers have been making the rounds on le internet (mostly thanks to the expertise of Rikkii Wise of @storytimewithrikkii and the Reality Receipts podcast), and I’ve rounded them all up for you to peruse. Major spoilers ahead, obviously.

Just keep in mind: These are all just rumors at this point, and fans won’t know anything for sure until the reunion finally airs. But if you’re wondering what kind of secrets might be spilled at this season’s reunion, I’ve got you covered.

According to Rikkii, Erika “will say she can’t see herself saying yes” ahead of the wedding because of their very different lifestyles and she’ll “give Blake the boot.” However! Rikkii also mentioned that the two were reportedly pretty touchy-feely at the reunion, so perhaps there is still something there.

Despite those seemingly telling Instagram comments, Rikkii said that Wilnie and Wilson won’t make it down the aisle either, instead deciding to break up after the bachelor and bachelorette parties. Apparently, the two just couldn’t see eye to eye about household responsibilities (among other issues...), but at the reunion, they make clear that they’re still friends.

Though Aneha and Cam shockingly make it to their wedding day, Rikkii said that Aneha ends up saying no...though she tried to make it seem as though she and Cam will keep seeing each other. (Spoiler alert: They don’t.) Rikkii also revealed that Aneha’s family attended the reunion, where Cam claimed that he would have also said no on the wedding day, had he been given the chance.

Yep, Sarah and John were apparently the only Boston couple to get married, but the love didn’t last. Per Rikkii, it’s revealed at the reunion that John had an “emotional affair” with an escort, causing the two to separate after just about a year of marriage.

Following her split from Rob, Rikkii said that Michaella gave things a try with resident beekeeper Matt, but the relationship didn’t last. Sorry to everyone who was shipping them!

Again, apologies to everyone shipping these two, because they apparently reveal at the reunion that they’re simply friends and nothing more. Siiiigh.

Nuno gets a huge slice of positive news ahead of must-win Cardiff City encounter | West Ham News

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Under fire Nuno Espírito Santo has a couple of games coming up that are in, in effect, must-win if West Ham are to get back on track with their promotion push and keep up with league leaders Middlesborough.

Next weekend the Irons travel north west to take on Blackburn Rovers after a Wednesday night trip in three days’ time to Cardiff City’s home for a crucial encounter under the lights.

Names are already being thrown around was possible successors should Nuno falter in the last two games heading into the two week international break.

Lose both ties that lie ahead and the Portuguese will surely be given his marching orders by West Ham’s board, who are reluctant to part ways with their chosen manager but cannot be immune to the dissatisfaction being voiced from just about every quarter following yesterday’s dèbacle.

Walker Peters is unlikely to make Wednesday night’s game but opponents Cardiff face much greater challenges

Right on time, however, there’s some good news for Nuno: Wednesday’s opponents are in something of a major injury crisis, as revealed by walesonline today:

Cardiff City’s injury crisis has deepened ahead of West Ham’s visit, with Brian Barry-Murphy facing mounting selection headaches and fresh concerns”

The Bluebirds’ defensive choices look particularly bleak, with Will Fish suffering a hamstring injury and Perry Ng also struggling, whilst Alex Robertson with an Achilles problem and Krystian Bielik are both expected to be absent.

Other players confirmed unavailable include Gabriel Osho due to suspension, as well as Dylan Lawlor and Calum Chambers, who are both sidelined through injury.

West Ham, conversely, might just escape with Kyle Walker Peters the only absentee –  if Dinos Mavropanos continues his near miraculous recovery from last weekend’s knee injury.

Which might just give West Ham the edge they need to go and grab three points and put the horrors of the last week – and the uncertainty over Nuno’s future – behind them.

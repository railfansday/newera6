Adam Back: Pension Funds Are Bitcoin’s “Third Wave” of Adoption

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Adam Back explains why Bitcoin’s third wave—pensions, managed funds, and model portfolios—is still to come, and how Swiss banks are already preparing.

The first wave of Bitcoin adoption was individuals. The second was the ETFs. Adam Back, CEO & Co-Founder of Blockstream, explains why the third wave of model portfolios, managed funds and pension funds is still largely to come, and how Swiss banks are getting ready for it.

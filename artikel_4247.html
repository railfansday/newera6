Hustle Castle celebrates offers you a new in-game event to celebrate nine years since launch

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

My.Games have had plenty of interesting releases over the years. But one of the more popular has been their humorous medieval RTS Hustle Castle, which recently celebrated nine years since its launch! Now, it's the perfect time to jump back in, with a new in-game event set to kick off soon.

The Viral Adventure, which kicks off on November 5th, is a pretty straightforward anniversary event. You'll meet up with the character Sir Mole and be tasked with completing his challenges in order to gain exclusive rewards. Simple, yes, but if you've been putting off getting back into Hustle Castle, it's the perfect time to jump back in.

I think it's fair to say that My.Games have been quite successful over the past few years, and this is just the latest example. But despite that, I don't think they've necessarily had the crossover success that other 'big' companies such as Rovio or Supercell have. Even if that's a bit of a stiff comparison, My.Games remains firmly mobile in terms of audience.

I'm not saying they should suddenly break out into the ARPG genre, but aside from War Robots there's none in their catalogue I can think of which have a major pop-culture footprint. But who knows? Maybe in the near future we might see that change off the back of the success of games such as Hustle Castle.

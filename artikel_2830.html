Vote for Argus Leader Athlete of the Week from Oct. 26-31

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

We're really starting to whittle down the remaining South Dakota high school football teams, and volleyball is headed that way shortly.

Here are the nominees for the Argus Leader's high school Athletes of the Week for Oct. 26-31. ArgusLeader.com readers vote to decide this week's winner. Voting closes at 11:59 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 6.

Athletes are listed in alphabetical order by last name.

Doe was a bear to bring down from the jump, as his 67-yard TD run on the first play from scrimmage ignited Harrisburg's 41-24 win. The senior back unofficially had 201 rushing yards and three scores on 29 attempts, plus an 11-yard reception.

Fokken flew around for Brandon Valley in the Lynx's 42-0 quarterfinal shutout of Sioux Falls Roosevelt. The senior linebacker notched nine tackles and two sacks in the dominant effort.

Lual had a big day on the interior of Tea Area's defensive line in a losing effort against Sturgis. The junior lineman recorded three tackles for a loss, with one being a sack. He totaled five tackles.

Starks had one of his biggest days of the season in Sioux Falls Lincoln's 42-7 win over Sioux Falls Washington in the 11AAA quarterfinals. The senior wide receiver had three touchdowns on five receptions, tallying 121 yards.

Wachal was the latest Sioux Falls Jefferson defender to post eye-popping numbers in the Cavaliers' 52-10 win over Rapid City Central. The junior linebacker recorded a team-best seven tackles, with one for a loss, and forced a fumble.

Basel's dominance in the middle of the floor helped Sioux Falls Lincoln rip through Sioux Falls Roosevelt, 3-0. The junior middle blocker had 11 kills and two digs in the outing.

Melcher continued to be a steady hand all over the court for Brandon Valley. The senior outside hitter recorded eight kills, eight digs and four aces in the Lynx's sweep of Mitchell.

Munce was steady in the middle for Harrisburg during another dominant week for the Tigers. She had 10 kills and five blocks in a 3-0 sweep of Dakota Valley, then added nine kills and six blocks in a sweep of Yankton.

Peterson was effective on offense and defense for Sioux Falls Washington in the Warriors' 3-1 win over Watertown. The senior had 14 kills, three blocks, two aces and five digs in the winning effort.

Robinson came up huge time and time again for O'Gorman as the Knights held on to beat Sioux Falls Jefferson 3-2. The Creighton commit went for 18 kills, three aces, three digs and two blocks in the narrow victory.

Amazon’s latest Echo smart displays and speakers reinforce Google Home’s lost lead

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Amazon just launched a new lineup of Echo smart speakers and a new smart display, all while Google Home’s lineup remains pretty quiet.

Amazon has five new devices launching – two smart displays, a “Photo Frame,” and two speakers.

Starting with the speakers, the latest Echo Dot has “a built-in smart home hub” and upgraded audio and starts shipping today for $100. Meanwhile, Echo Studio builds on that foundation with even-better audio and, optionally, a built-in camera. Those start at $220.

On the smart display side, there’s the Echo Time, which is like an Echo Dot but with a small circular display built-in. Amazon touts “hundreds of clock faces” and “Omnisense” which can track your sleep just like Google’s second-gen Nest Hub, and it starts at $200.

Echo Show 16, meanwhile, is a big 16-inch smart display meant to live on your wall with support for smart home controls, the ability to show a family calendar, and full Fire TV built-in as well. It starts at $500 and, like everything else mentioned so far, is built around Amazon’s AI-enhanced Alexa.

Rounding out the lineup is the Alexa Photo Frame, a simpler smart display that has Alexa built-in but focuses on showing photos from your Amazon Photos library. It has a 10-inch anti-glare display and a design that looks like a normal photo frame and starts at $200.

Google Home really is just so wildly behind isn’t it.

In looking at Amazon’s lineup, I can’t help but feel like Google is just wasting potential here. A smart speaker that doubles as a Nest Cam would be awesome. The same goes for a giant Nest Hub that doubles as a Google TV.

I still don't understand how nobody is questioning Google when they still don't support basics stuff like:

casting from the phone using a "video" app (YouTube, Netflix, etc) to Google Cast speakers -> AirPlay has supported this for a decade

being able to set Google Cast speakers as audio output devices of Google TV devices (and not the extremely limited - and frankly, basically useless - implementation that there's only between Google TV Streamer and the new Google Home Speaker) -> both Amazon and Apple (as well as many competitors) have supported this for several years, including with with surround setups (4 speakers)

Their latest Google Home Speaker was a major disappointment. I almost have no doubt that a new Google Home Hub and a new Google TV Streamer will be disappointments too.

It's years that I keep wishing for Google to prove me wrong, but all they do is keep digging themselves deeper in their grave.

And, seriously, how does Google not sell (or at least partner with someone) a Google Photos digital photo frame. Google Photos is a wildly successful platform and that would sell like hotcakes. Big “how am I not in that movie” energy.

I’ve said it before, but Google Home had such a lead in the smart speaker and smart display market, especially with Google’s ecosystem tie-ins, but Amazon is currently running laps around it with products that Google should be selling. Throw in that even Apple has a smart display now where Google hasn’t even teased a new Nest Hub, and it just feels all kinds of bad to be in Google’s camp right now.

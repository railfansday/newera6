Erling Haaland cuts off his trademark blond hair

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Erling Haaland has unveiled a dramatic new look after cutting off his trademark blond locks.

The Manchester City striker revealed his new buzzcut hairstyle on Sunday morning ahead of his side’s Premier League opener at home to Bournemouth later in the day.

Later in the episode, Haaland called a host of famous faces, including Oasis singer Noel Gallagher and former City manager Pep Guardiola, to get their opinions on his transformation.

Pep Guardiola's reaction to Erling Haaland's new haircut. 😭😂📲 Watch: https://t.co/4KvxC9e5mx pic.twitter.com/QQBCKKpGkP

Haaland, 26, has not sported short hair since his time at Borussia Dortmund, where he played three seasons before joining City in 2022.

Haaland removed his hairband during a 4-1 win over Arsenal in 2023 on a raucous night at the Etihad as City beat their title rivals to move one step closer to winning the league. There will be no need for one now, though, with a close-cut look to mark a new campaign.

It is a new-look City in more ways than one this term with head coach Enzo Maresca looking to kickstart a new era following Pep Guardiola’s exit.

Maresca is hoping for an immediate return to form in City’s Premier League opener after a chastening 3-0 defeat by Arsenal in the Community Shield last week.

So far, only Elliot Anderson has arrived but Maresca is hopeful the club will be active in the transfer market. Rodri has left for Barcelona while Savinho is closing in on a move to Tottenham.

“I’ll try to find a new Rodri – it is what it is,” the former Chelsea manager said. “Rodri, before he arrived here, he didn’t win the Ballon d’Or, he didn’t win the Champions League, he didn’t win the Club World Cup. Then he joined the club and then after he achieved many important things. Now the target for us is to try to find the new Rodri.

“When David Silva was here, everyone was worried - Kevin [De Bruyne] arrived. When Sergio Agüero was here, everyone was worried – Erling [Haaland] arrived. When Ferdinando left, Rodri arrived. It’s just about this.

“When they leave, you need to be good and recruit the next one. They are all similar – David, Kevin, Fernandinho, Rodri, Agüero, Erling. It’s just normal, it’s a change. It’s not a problem, just go again and try to do the right things.”

Maresca added: “Hopefully we can do the things that we need to do. It doesn’t help that there are players that for different reasons want to leave, this is for sure.

“But we need to adapt and we don’t want to keep players here that don’t want to stay.

“The normal thing in transfer windows is that you have to wait because all the clubs try to keep players until the last week and then sell for more money.

“It’s normal, I’m not worried about that. When the transfer window closes and we have all the squad there, for sure it will be different.”

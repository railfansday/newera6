Instant Pot Mac &amp; Cheese

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Instant Pot meals are THE hack to make dinner prep and clean-up a walk in the park. If it’s one of those nights when you can’t be bothered to pull out all the pots and pans for an elaborate meal but still want a tasty dinner, this recipe is for you. Mac and cheese is already the ultimate comfort food, but this recipe makes it practically effortless. Just toss everything into the pot, let the Instant Pot work its magic, and get ready for a big bowl of cheesy goodness. Before you know it, you'll have the cheesiest mac and cheese on your hands, perfect for a satisfying weeknight dinner or an easy, last-minute BBQ side.

Start by grabbing your Instant Pot or any other pressure cooker. Pour in the water, then add the macaroni, butter, mustard, paprika, and a teaspoon of salt. Secure the lid, set it to Manual on High Pressure, and let it cook for 4 minutes. Once it’s done, quickly release the pressure according to your machine’s instructions. When the pressure has fully released, go ahead and open the lid.

Stir in cheddar, Fontina, milk, and Parmesan until melted and combined.

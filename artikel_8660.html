Get the Tilt Motion VISA® Credit Card if you have little or no credit history and will pay your balance in full every month. It charges no annual fee or deposit and can approve you without a credit score. Skip it if you’d carry a balance, since its interest rate runs 29.49% to 34.49%.

If you have no credit score, or Tilt can’t decide on your score alone, it asks you to link a bank account and judges your application on the income and spending it shows.

Tilt, the company formerly called Empower, now owns Petal, and the Motion uses the same cash back program as the Petal 1 Visa.

It suits borrowers with steady income and little or no credit history who pay the statement balance in full each month, since the card then costs them nothing. Anyone likely to carry a balance should get a card from a federal credit union instead, whose rates the National Credit Union Administration caps at 18%.

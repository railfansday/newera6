The Sky Blues host Sunderland, the only team below them in the Premier League, and another defeat would leave fans fearing the worst after the brief high of back-to-back wins either side of the international break.

Defender Bobby Thomas has been troubled by a calf issue, with 18-year-old Manchester City loanee Stephen Mfuni poised to step in if needed.

Manager Frank Lampard is already without injured quartet Aurele Amenda, Josh Eccles, Luke Woolfenden and Haji Wright.

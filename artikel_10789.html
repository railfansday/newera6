Rockstar reportedly teaming up with Nike for $130 GTA 6 sneakers

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Rockstar Games is reportedly teaming up with Travis Scott and Nike to release a collection of Grand Theft Auto 6-themed sneakers in time for the game’s launch.

With GTA 6’s release coming on November 19, Rockstar has been ramping up its marketing campaign, including promotional events across Miami, and the rollout of singles taken from the game’s soundtrack album.

Among the artists featured on the album is Travis Scott, whose streetwear brand Cactus Jack has reportedly teamed up with Nike and Rockstar for a collaboration set to coincide with the release of the game.

Sneaker Market Romania, the country’s largest sneaker community, shared news of the collaboration. According to the website, the sneakers will be included as part of the Nike Holiday 2026 seasonal catalog, which spans October to December.

Though they expect the sneakers to release on November 19, the release date of GTA 6, neither Rockstar or Nike have officially confirmed the collaboration.

The base design of the sneaker is said to be the Nike Air Force 1 Low, with three color schemes: Black/Fire Pink, White/Fire Pink and Multi-Color.

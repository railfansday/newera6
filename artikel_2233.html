Wolves and Swansea City handed major boost involving Peixoto & Matos

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Wolverhampton Wanderers and Swansea City have received a major managerial boost after their respective head coaches were linked with the vacancy at Liga Portugal side, Sporting CP, across the weekend.

Indeed, it has been widely reported that Sporting are nearing an agreement with Olympique Lyonnais boss, Paulo Fonseca, which increases the likelihood of Cesar Peixoto and Vitor Matos staying put.

Both clubs enjoyed emphatic victories on Saturday and are well-placed inside the Championship's early-season promotion picture as a result.

Wolves made it two home victories in the space of a week by seeing off Cardiff City 3-0 at Molineux, whilst Swansea responded to successive losses with a 4-0 thumping of Blackburn Rovers.

As such, the aforementioned managerial news will be the latest lift for both sets of supporters and the respective squads in WV1 and SA1.

It was reported on Saturday that the two Championship managers had emerged on the radar of Sporting, who have terminated Rui Borges' contract after just under two years in charge.

Peixoto's move to Molineux in the summer was his first managerial role outside Portugal, having finished sixth in the top-flight last season with Gil Vicente, whilst Matos also enjoyed a brief spell with Maritimo after coaching at Liverpool and RB Salzburg.

Fonseca was also namedropped in said report, and it has since been claimed by RMC Sport that the 53-year-old has said his goodbyes to his squad at the Groupama Stadium, with Lyon currently sat third in Ligue 1 after nine matches.

Sporting are also third in the Portuguese top-flight at present, but are already nine points behind leaders Porto, with Borges' final match in charge being a 2-0 away win against Casa Pia on Friday.

It is, of course, a positive and a hindrance to clubs if their head coach is being eyed by clubs of a greater stature in terms of history or current divisional status.

Since taking charge 12 months ago, Matos has won 22 of his first 45 Swansea matches across all competitions, giving his side a strong platform to build from after a strong end to last season.

As well as being linked with the Sporting vacancy, which is set to be filled by Fonseca, the 38-year-old recently emerged as a potential target for UEFA Conference League holders, Crystal Palace, who have endured an underwhelming start to Pierre Sage's reign as they sit inside the Premier League's bottom three.

Given the strength-in-depth at both his and Matos' disposal, both clubs will hope for stability in both the short and long-term to give themselves the best possible chance of success.

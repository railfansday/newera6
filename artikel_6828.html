The Best Knee-Length Skirts Worth Adding to Your Closet

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

From polished pencils to effortless denim, these are the skirts to shop now.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

There’s a reason the knee-length skirt has outlasted every trend cycle thrown at it. Sitting right at the knee, it’s not too short or too long, and it flatters everyone. This is the hemline of quiet confidence. It fits nearly every body type, plays well with flats, boots, and heels alike, and lets the fabric determine the overall vibes.

A structured A-line in wool crepe means business. A tailored pencil in a silky satin feels sexy and feminine without trying too hard. And the humble denim knee-length? Underrated, effortless, and endlessly styleable.

Ahead, we’ve rounded up the knee-length skirts worth your money, from investment-worthy classics to budget-friendly finds that look far pricier than they are.

Structure and movement define the overall silhouette of this pleated wool-blend skirt. It works just as well at the office as it does on the weekend, and the chocolate brown shade is a rich alternative to black. Plus, we love the unexpected slit.

Blending sporty and casual influences, this gabardine skirt has a lightweight structure and endless styling possibilities. It’s a relaxed weekend option that steps away from the usual shorts or joggers.

Silk and satin skirts continue to dominate the skirt category. This one feels feminine and timeless, with a delicate lace trim that adds a soft, romantic flair. Pair it with chic ballet pumps or tall brown boots for an autumnal twist.

The amount of wear you’ll get from a classic black silhouette at such an affordable price makes this knee-length style a must-have. Between the style, color, and fabric, you already know it has endless styling possibilities.

This silk tweed skirt pairs classic tailoring with a textured, luxe finish that feels heirloom-worthy from the first wear. Dress it up with a crisp blouse or down with a knit and loafers.

This misty gray knee-length skirt pairs a retro-inspired shape with a raw hem for an effortlessly modern edge. Style it with a fitted tee and sneakers for everyday ease, or a tucked-in blouse and ankle boots when you want a little more polish.

This panel-pleated wool-blend skirt channels a schoolgirl spirit with a polished, modern twist. Pair it with knee-high socks and loafers for a nostalgic nod, or a fitted knit and cap-toe block heels for an elevated everyday look.

This denim circle skirt hits right at the knee with a swingy, playful shape and a well-loved texture that only gets better with wear. It’s filled with rich character you’ll reach for season after season.

Turkey Noodle Soup

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Making Thanksgiving dinner is a huge project, and once you’re done, you may never want to see a kitchen again. But you have to eat, and you have to do something about the mountain of leftovers you probably have piling up in your fridge. This turkey noodle soup satisfies both those needs while also being completely delicious. It has everything you love about the classic—veggies, herbs, warming broth, and hearty noodles—but adds in leftover Thanksgiving turkey for a little something special. It's what we look forward to cozying up to after the hectic holiday has finally calmed down.

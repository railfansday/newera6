Bears elevate TE Stephen Carlson for Monday night vs. Seahawks

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Bears have made one roster move for Monday night’s game against the Seahawks.

Chicago announced tight end Stephen Carlson has been elevated from the practice squad to the gameday roster for the Week 8 contest.

Carlson, 29, has not yet appeared in a game this season. He appeared in two last year for Chicago, playing 14 offensive snaps and 12 special teams snaps.

Carlson entered the league as an undrafted free agent in 2019, spending his first few seasons with the Browns before joining the Bears in 2023. He’s appeared in 28 career games with seven starts.

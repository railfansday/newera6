Manage a Dessert Shop with an Actual God in Honkai: Star Rail's Climactic PS5 Update

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

If you’re a regular of HoYoverse’s turn-based RPG Honkai: Star Rail, then you’ll probably have been waiting for 5-Star character Aha’s arrival, as part of v4.7 on 11th November.

The anticipated Aeon is effectively a god, bringing outrageous buffs to all characters on the path of Elation. She’s going to be essential if you’ve been pulling for any of the characters featured in the Planarcadia arc, like Sparxie and Silver Wolf Lv 999.

Her introduction to the gacha pool will coincide with a climactic conclusion to the current storyline.

I must admit, I’m a little relieved to hear that because while I’ve undoubtedly enjoyed the vibes of this new planet, the dev’s lore-heavy writing is losing me a lot lately. I personally find it difficult to keep up with the constant proper nouns.

Other activities in this update include a dessert shop management minigame that looks like a ton of fun, as well as new rules and content for the auto-chess inspired endgame mode, Currency Wars.

As mentioned, all of this new content will debut on 11th November, which is the same day as NTE’s new update goes live. That’s a lot of gacha game to get through before GTA 6.

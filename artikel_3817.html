How to get Solid Gold A$AP Rocky in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Fortnite’s Treasure Hunters season is shaping up to be perhaps the most generous season of all time, with loads of free cosmetics up for grabs. One of the rarest and most special items you can get is Solid Gold A$AP Rocky.

Lots of the mechanics featured in this season are unlike anything we’ve seen before, so figuring out how to get your hands on this freebie and others like it can be tricky. To ensure you don’t miss out on this epic prize, here’s how to get Solid Gold A$AP Rocky in Fortnite Treasure Hunters.

Solid Gold A$AP Rocky is a random treasure chest loot item, which means there’s a chance you might find him while looting anywhere around the Fortnite island. He is not a guaranteed drop at any point, and is one of the rarest items in rotation, so it generally takes a lot of searching and quite a bit of good luck to acquire him.

Anytime you open a treasure chest, you might only find standard loot, or you may instead also be rewarded with some extra special freebies. Solid Gold A$AP Rocky is one of the potential prizes included among the bonus goodies you may find.

Since there’s a pretty expansive pool of potential bonus items you might find while looting, actually getting Solid Gold A$AP Rocky can be tough, as many of the other possible items have better drop rates than he does. Still, all you can do is loot as much as possible and hope luck favors you.

You’ll know you’ve found Solid Gold A$AP Rocky or another special item from the bonus pool when you get a gold pop-up near the top middle section of the screen. This pop-up will highlight the prize you found, which is then granted to you upon returning to the lobby after the round ends.

Solid Gold A$AP Rocky can potentially be found in any treasure chest around the island. There are no fixed spawn locations for this loot drop, so anytime you find and open a chest while he’s featured in the loot pool, there’s a chance you may unlock him.

Solid Gold A$AP Rocky is available to find in the loot pool from Nov. 1 to Nov. 7. The featured skin switches over to Lil Uzi Vert at 8am CT on Nov. 7, so you have about a week to search for it before it leaves the pool.

After his initial availability period ends, it’s unclear when or if Solid Gold A$AP Rocky will be available to obtain. He could potentially return to the loot pool near the end of the season, may be added as a shop asset you have to buy with V-Bucks, or may be altogether unobtainable once the initial treasure hunt run ends.

Once we know what his availability looks like after his run in the loot pool ends, we’ll provide an update here. If you’re determined to grab him for free, though, it’s a good idea to go hunting for him before Nov. 7, as this is the only guaranteed way to get him for now.

If you’re looking for some tasks to keep you busy while you hunt for Solid Gold A$AP Rocky, there’s plenty to choose from. You might consider collecting a map fragment at The $.$. Rocky, finding Devil Fruits, and checking out all Treasure Hunters battle pass skins.

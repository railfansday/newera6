This $60 lifetime language-learning deal puts your speaking skills to the test

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

TL;DR: Lingzio combines structured language lessons with AI conversation practice and pronunciation feedback. Get lifetime access to its Premium Plan for $59.99 (reg. $199).

A 100-day language-learning streak is impressive, but it doesn’t necessarily mean you’re ready to hold a conversation. Lingzio combines traditional lessons with AI conversation practice and pronunciation feedback to help you figure out where you need work. For a limited time, you can score a lifetime subscription to its Premium Plan for just $59.99 (reg. $199).

Choose from six languages: Spanish, French, German, Italian, Japanese, and English. You can keep up to three languages in your account and switch between them whenever you like, with your progress saved for each one. Only one language can be active at a time.

The lessons follow the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), covering levels A1 through C2, from beginner to advanced. There’s no limit on how many lessons you can complete, and you’ll also get unlimited vocabulary flashcards, listening exercises, a built-in grammar guide, and progress tracking.

For speaking practice, Lingzio includes an AI tutor that lets you work through conversations instead of just reading dialogue on a screen. You can also use voice recognition to check your pronunciation and identify areas that need more attention.

The Premium plan includes 20 minutes of AI conversation practice, or roughly five scenes, and 150 pronunciation checks each month. Those allowances reset on the first of every month without rolling over, although you can purchase additional AI conversation time if, say, you have an upcoming trip and want to bring your A-game.

Whether you’re preparing to order food abroad, speak with international clients, or learn a language your family uses, the combination of lessons and speaking exercises gives you several ways to practice.

Lingzio works through a browser on computers, phones, and tablets, with no separate app to download. You’ll need an internet connection for lessons and a microphone for speaking exercises. Each license supports up to three devices, and this deal is available to new users only.

Normally $199, get lifetime access to Lingzio Premium for just $59.99.

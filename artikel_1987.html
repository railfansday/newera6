Zadok Yohanna: The £22m summer arrival is also making good progress as the winger builds up his fitness. Hurzeler confirmed last week that he is back training with the first team squad.

Pascal Struijk: The £18m summer arrival from Leeds is still battling a thigh injury but Hurzeler said last week that he could be available for Getafe and Brentford.

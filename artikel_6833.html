Jacquard Is Having a Moment—Here’s How to Wear It Without Looking Overdone

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Statement blazers, muted florals, and metallic touches make jacquard feel modern.

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Jacquard is having a moment, and it’s easy to see why. Woven rather than printed, these richly patterned fabrics carry their designs in the very structure of the cloth: florals that rise in subtle relief, geometric motifs that catch the light, tone-on-tone brocades that shimmer when you move. It’s a fabric with the soul of couture and, increasingly, the versatility of everyday dressing.

But a textile this expressive comes with a question every fashion lover eventually asks: how do you wear it without looking like you’ve raided the sofa upholstery?

The answer, as it turns out, is balance. Jacquard works best when it’s allowed to be the star, so the rest of your outfit should play a supporting role. Think a statement blazer paired with clean white tees and straight-leg denim, or a sculptural jacquard miniskirt grounded by a fitted knit and sleek ankle boots. For evenings, a jacquard slip dress needs little more than a sharp jacket and a good pair of heels. Metallic threads and deep jewel tones lean glamorous, while soft neutrals and muted palettes bring a quiet, modern elegance to workwear and weekend looks alike.

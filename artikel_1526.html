Lulli and Mancini to start vs Manchester United

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

After dominating Udinese with a 2-5 victory, Roma are already focusing on the Champions League.

Tomorrow, the Giallorossi will play away to Manchester United, a struggling team that hasn’t picked up three points since September 10th, having lost six and drawn three of its last nine games.

Yesterday, the Roma team held a recovery session in Udine, hosted at the Bianconeri’s training center, without returning to the Eternal City.

A light training session followed before departing for England, where Gasperini will speak at a press conference today at 6:20 PM (Italian time). The Giallorossi coach will still be without Dybala, Svilar, and Koné.

In Roma’s last match, won in Udine, both Lulli and Mancini remained on the bench.

The number 23 only came on in the 79th minute, while the 2007-born player played the entire match alongside Gasperini.

As reported by La Gazzetta dello Sport, the Giallorossi coach intends to start both players against Manchester United. This would be Lulli’s Champions League debut, a unique experience he’s about to experience.

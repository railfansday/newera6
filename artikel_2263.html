Four chances created: 30-year-old justifies new Southampton contract talk with starring role in QPR thrashing

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Southampton continued their rise up the Championship table on Saturday as they defeated QPR 4-1, making it five undefeated. The Saints are now up in 5th, recovering well from the four-point deduction they started the campaign with.

It was a fine display from Tonda Eckert’s side in their routine triumph. The goals were shared across the team with Kuryu Matsuki, Cam Bragg, Keven Schlotterbeck and Welington netting, but one man who had a key role in two of the goals was Ryan Manning.

It was Manning’s pull-back that Bragg converted from for the second, and a deep corner of his found Schlotterbeck completely free at the back post to make it 3-0. In a week where news has emerged of Southampton’s talks to renew his contract, the 30-year-old further strengthened the case for his deal.

Creation was where Manning stood out on Saturday. Alongside his aforementioned pair of assists, the Irishman created the joint-most chances in the game with four – tied with Southampton teammate Finn Azaz.

His set-piece ability was on display as a deep, in-swinging cross to the back stick almost saw centre-back Schlotterbeck score in the first half too. Manning took five corners and delivered two successful crosses from open play.

Manning didn’t have much defending to do as he made only three defensive contributions against his former side QPR. However, he was victorious in four of his six ground duels and made three recoveries as well (all stats via Fotmob).

The Saints star’s contributions now put him on one goal and three assists in 13 Championship games this term. He set up one goal in the 2-0 Carabao Cup victory over Colchester United at the start of the season too.

Southampton fans won’t have had many complaints when news of Manning’s new contract talks emerged. He has grown into an influential player under Eckert with his creative abilities from left-back making him a valuable asset.

His case for a new deal has only grown stronger with his performance from QPR though. The technical ability he offers makes the Saints such a threat from wide areas and set plays.

A new contract would be a justified reward for how Manning has grown at St. Mary’s. Like many, he struggled during their last stay in the Premier League in 2024/25, but he will be determined to earn another shot as Southampton battle for promotion from the Championship this season.

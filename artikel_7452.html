Staff Application Security Engineer at Fingerprint

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Fingerprint empowers enterprises to detect and stop online fraud with the world’s most accurate device intelligence.  We lead our industry with bleeding-edge identification capabilities and work on turning new ideas and discoveries in the fraud detection space into reality. Our customers range from innovative startups to leading enterprise companies, including Plaid, Dropbox, and Booking.com.

Fingerprint is a globally dispersed, 100% remote company. We were named on on the 2026 Forbes Best Startup Employers list and ranked #803 on the 2026 Inc. 5000 list of America’s fastest-growing private companies.

We have raised $77M and are backed by Craft Ventures (Tesla, Facebook, Airbnb ), Nexus Venture Partners ( Postman, Apollo.io, MinIO, Druva) and Uncorrelated Ventures ( Redis, Rollbar,  Gradle).

Fingerprint's device intelligence platform processes billions of identification events for some of the world's most security-conscious companies — financial institutions, fraud prevention operators, and enterprises that rely on us to stop attackers. That makes our own application security a product feature, not a back-office function: our customers' adversaries are, by definition, motivated to probe what we build.

As we scale with large enterprise customers, we're investing in application security as a dedicated, first-class engineering discipline. Security is already part of how our teams build — scanning in the pipeline, a Cloud Platform team that treats security as a partner, and a mature compliance posture — and we're now standing up a focused AppSec function, led by a Senior Manager, Application Security who owns the program and strategy, to take it to the next level. You will be the founding senior engineer of that function — the person who turns strategy into a working secure SDLC, finds and fixes what matters, and raises the security bar across engineering through technical depth, tooling, and influence.

This is a hands-on Staff role. You will design, build, and operate — not just advise.

Threat Modeling, Security Reviews & Vulnerability Management

Incident Response & Detection (application layer)

This is the senior individual-contributor counterpart to the Senior Manager, Application Security. The manager owns the program, the strategy, and the team; you own the technical depth and the execution. The two roles are designed to work as a pair: you'll bring the findings, the engineering judgment, and the ground truth from inside the codebase, and together you'll decide what to prioritize.

As a Staff engineer, you are expected to operate with a high degree of independence — scoping ambiguous problems, setting technical direction for AppSec, and driving multi-quarter initiatives without needing day-to-day direction. As the founding engineer of a dedicated AppSec function, the patterns, tooling, and standards you establish will be the foundation the practice grows on.

This is not a role for someone who wants to primarily write policy or manage a program from a distance. It's for an engineer who finds real bugs, builds real tooling, and earns the trust of other engineers by making their code better.

At Fingerprint, we believe in pay transparency — it's part of how we operate as a distributed, remote-first team.  Salaries are based on function, level, and geographic location, benchmarked against similar stage growth companies.

Opinion: Liverpool’s academy has produced another Rio Ngumoha, he’s unreal but there's a twist

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Liverpool have secured produced another exciting player, but there is a twist.

Rio Ngumoha is quickly becoming one of the most exciting young talents in world football, and his recent performances show exactly why. Back-to-back brilliant Champions League displays and record-breaking dribbling numbers have put the England international firmly in the spotlight.

For Liverpool, signing him from Chelsea could prove to be an absolute masterstroke. Ngumoha wanted a clear pathway into the first team, and the Reds have certainly delivered. At just 18, he's already made 39 appearances for the club and earned his senior England debut.

The scary thing is that he's only going to get better. His confidence, dribbling ability and fearlessness make him incredibly exciting to watch. Liverpool could genuinely have a future superstar on their hands.

Liverpool have an exciting futureNgumoha isn't the only youngster exciting Liverpool fans right now though. There are plenty of others. Trey Nyoni is also starting to make his mark under Andoni Iraola, and there's plenty more talent waiting in the wings. Ifeanyi Ndukwe is impressing on loan at Levante in La Liga, while Mor Talla Ndiaye looks like another brilliant find who could eventually become a regular starter. Joshua Abe, meanwhile, made his first-team debut in pre-season at just 16. Then there's Luca Eden, who's been compared to Steven Gerrard, and Lucca Brughmans, who became the most expensive U23 goalkeeper ever. Samuel Martinez has drawn Kaká comparisons, Erik Farkas is another standout prospect, and Will Wright can't stop scoring for the U21s. The future looks seriously exciting.

Meet Vincent Joseph - the next NgumohaLiverpool have done it again with another one of their academy products. Vincent Joseph came through the club's youth ranks since he was 9-years-old. He worked his way up all the way to the club's U18 side, where he scored three goals in his first two games for the Reds. But then, in a major twist he decided to leave Liverpool for pastures new this summer. Joseph joined Arsenal on a free transfer after rejecting a scholarship offer by the Reds. And he's just gotten off to the perfect start for the Gunners. In his first ever start for Arsenal's U18 side, he scored a hat-trick in just 45 minutes. It means he now has six goals in four games at U18 level as a mere 16-year-old. He truly is the next Ngumoha produced by Liverpool but sadly he will be making his mark at Arsenal.

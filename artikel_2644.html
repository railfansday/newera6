Red Sox Should Consider Predicted $118M Pitcher if Sonny Gray Leaves in Free Agency

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

With the possibility of losing right-hander Sonny Gray in MLB free agency, the Boston Red Sox will have to figure out who they want to replace him with.

The reality is that there aren't many great starting pitchers available in Major League Baseball this offseason, and teams like the Red Sox will have to consider that.

However, one player makes a ton of sense for Boston: right-hander Michael King.

King is a Northeast guy, went to school at Northeastern, and has played for the New York Yankees throughout his career. All of those factors make him a perfect candidate to join a team like the Red Sox, though his contract situation does lead to some questions.

King is expected to land a deal worth over $100 million, with the latest prediction recently suggesting that he could be a four-year, $118 million man. However, it was also noted that he really enjoys his time with the San Diego Padres.

"I expect King to opt out of the two remaining player-option years on his contract so he can receive a $5 million buyout and then look to secure a long-term contract. King had a terrific year with the Padres, posting a 3.21 ERA in 185 innings over 32 starts. He's from the East Coast but has really enjoyed his time with the Padres, considers San Diego his second home and has sincere interest in staying. San Diego desperately needs to re-sign him, as he's clearly the ace of their staff," Jim Bowden said.

With King now a free agent, baseball expects he'll at least listen to other teams around the league.

He's coming off a ridiculous postseason, showing that he has the stuff to be among the best in baseball at any given moment. He was perhaps better than anybody in the playoffs this year, appearing in three games, starting two of them, and posting a 0.64 ERA in 14.0 innings.

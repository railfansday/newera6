Ghost Audiences Are Haunting CTV Measurement

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Streaming is officially an advertising business. According to Antenna, nearly half (48%) of all premium subscription video-on-demand (SVOD) accounts in the US were ad-supported in Q1 2026, and 59% of new subscriptions in the quarter were for ad tiers. US ad-supported Premium SVOD subscriptions, meanwhile, hit 110 million by the same quarter, up from 53 million two years prior.

In digital advertising, there is a growing risk of conflating audience size with audience availability. There are now improved ways to think about metrics:

The closer buying moves to the impression, the more costly it becomes to confuse these audiences. Just because a platform’s audience is large doesn’t mean it’s available. And even if it’s available, it isn’t always addressable. The delta between the audience a platform reports on and the audience an advertiser can actually reach is the key gap to close. Consider new viewers who download an app to stream NFL games during the regular season. Samsung Ads recently found that two of every three of these new viewers had “ghosted” the streaming service after the NFL season ended – nearly twice the churn rate of existing users. These ghost audiences remain part of a platform’s reported audience, yet contribute little to campaign-ready inventory. The result is a widening disconnect between reported audience size and the transactable supply advertisers are actually bidding on.

The industry’s next evolution isn’t happening on the demand side – it’s happening closer to the supply. As selling moves closer to the glass, OEMs and SSPs are evolving from distribution partners into intelligence partners, enriching inventory with real-time viewing signals that help determine the value of every impression before it reaches the auction. In that environment, competitive advantage will no longer come from reporting the largest audience. It will come from identifying the best available audience at the exact moment an impression becomes available. The next currency in streaming will be the strongest combination of reach and availability.

Amazon’s early Black Friday deals have started and these 28 products are at their 2026 lows

Halloween is over, which means it’s time to put away the giant skeletons and start thinking about holiday shopping. Amazon’s early Black Friday deals went live November 1, and new ones are rolling out every day, so we’re keeping track. Most of the bargains run through the end of the month, which leaves time to deal with the shelf that never got hung or the grill that won’t survive another season. We checked every price in this post on Amazon Monday morning, November 2.

The Black+Decker 20V Max brushless drill kit comes with a 2Ah battery, a charger and a two-speed gearbox, and at $59.99 it’s $20 below the lowest price Amazon charged for it earlier this year. Black+Decker rates it at 486 inch-pounds of torque, plenty for furniture assembly, shelf brackets and pilot holes in studs. For more options, see our guide to the best cordless drills.

The Weber Smoque 22-inch pellet smoker hasn’t been this cheap on Amazon all year, and its previous 2026 low was $699. Weber says its PID controller recovers heat quickly after you open the lid, and a SmokeBoost setting holds lower temperatures when you want more smoke flavor. A pellet grill also spares you from standing over a charcoal chimney in the cold. Our guide to the best pellet grills covers how these cookers work.

The Klein Tools 93MCLG laser level projects green horizontal and vertical lines that Klein rates out to 50 feet, so hanging a row of frames or a run of shelves stops being a two-person job with a tape measure. The included magnetic bracket sticks to metal surfaces and mounts on a tripod. At $59.98, it’s at its lowest price of 2026. See our guide to the best laser levels for other picks.

Tied for the biggest dollar drop in this post

Segway rates the Ninebot F3 for up to 44 miles of range in Eco mode, and an optional 460Wh battery extends that further. It rides on 10-inch self-sealing tubeless tires and has a magnesium alloy frame. The $200 cut ties its lowest price of the year.

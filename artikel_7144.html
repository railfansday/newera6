To grow teacher capacity, measure what matters

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

When I was a school principal, I’d put aside several hours every week for one-on-one coaching with teachers. I did it because I’d seen what happened as teachers grew in their professional capacities: they were more satisfied and more likely to stay, students were more engaged, and stakeholder relationships improved. For this reason, I’ve spent most of my career supporting educators’ professional growth.

Effectively cultivating teacher growth requires focusing on behaviors proven to enhance professional practice. Through my work with a school improvement organization, I observed significant alignment between my coaching experiences and the five overarching dimensions of professional practice identified in the organization’s research. Without data, educators will struggle to understand exactly where they’ve improved and by how much. But when schools measure teacher practices within the five dimensions and act on the data, they build a culture of growth for staff and students alike.

Nicole Reeves, Ed.D., serves as vice president of professional learning at Cognia, a global nonprofit organization. Prior to this role, she was a teacher, assistant principal, principal, national trainer, and educational consultant. As an educational expert and facilitator, she has supported administrators, teachers, and board-level personnel at all levels to improve organizational and instructional processes that support student learning.

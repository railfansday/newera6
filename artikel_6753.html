All the Standout Fall/Winter 2026 Fashion Campaigns So Far

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

As fall kicks off, the fashion world is already offering its first look at what we’ll be coveting this season. The biggest houses are ushering in fall/winter 2026 with campaigns that blend hallmarks of the season with transitional staples, from Celine’s sleek vision lensed by Zoë Ghertner to Fendi’s Peekaboo bag taking center stage on Monica Bellucci and Yara Shahidi to Louis Vuitton’s and Chanel’s après-ski wonderlands.

Elsewhere, Hunter taps fashion and festival icon Kate Moss for its global campaign, celebrating the brand’s 170th anniversary; Kendall Jenner leads Frame’s latest offering, set in the Hollywood Hills; and Kate Moss stars in Gucci’s Primavera campaign, lip-syncing to Chris Isaak’s rendition of “Wicked Game.” Also, Prada goes cinematic in a new visual titled Simple Stories starring Hunter Schafer and Troye Sivan; Heated Rivalry star Connor Storrie, Rosé, and Liu Wen take the lead for Saint Laurent; and Chloë Sevigny stars in 7 For All Mankind’s fall campaign.

Notice of Data Event

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

EAST LONGMEADOW, Mass., Nov. 2, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Magnolia Management Company, LLC ("Magnolia") is providing notice of a recent data privacy event that may have affected limited protected health information for certain individuals. On April 3, 2026, Magnolia became aware of unauthorized activity in one employee email account. Upon discovery, Magnolia took immediate action to secure its email environment and investigate the scope of the event, including hiring a third-party forensic investigation firm. Following a thorough review, Magnolia confirmed that a limited amount of information may have been accessed by an unauthorized individual. Magnolia undertook a comprehensive review of the impacted data to identify what type of information was contained therein, and to whom that information belonged. On August 10, 2026, that review was completed.

In response to this event, Magnolia also implemented additional technical safeguards to further enhance the security of information in its possession and with the aim of preventing similar events from happening in the future. While there is no evidence of actual or attempted misuse of any information as a result of this event, Magnolia will be providing impacted individuals with access to complimentary credit monitoring and identity protection services.

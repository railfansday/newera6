Kieran McKenna on West Ham’s three-man managerial shortlist as Nuno doubts grow

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

West Ham slipped to 8th in the Championship after defeat against West Brom on Saturday. WHUFC are currently in the play-off positions, but back-to-back losses to fellow promotion hopefuls have brought concerns.

Now, reporter Alan Nixon states that the Hammers have a three-man shortlist of potential targets if they decide to part ways with the under-fire Nuno Espirito Santo. Nixon says that Kieran McKenna, Rob Edwards and Scott Parker are all on the radar at the London Stadium.

All of the names mentioned are currently available, but it remains to be seen if the interest develops into anything concrete.

West Ham have some favourable fixtures coming up, with Cardiff City, Blackburn Rovers, Preston North End and Portsmouth all to play before the end of November. This run could be crucial for Nuno as pressure on his position grows.

Unlike Championship rivals Burnley, who have recently appointed Fabio Celestini, it appears West Ham are looking for experience in the division as they weigh up potential Nuno replacements.

It may be hard to persuade McKenna to return to the second tier, however, Parker and Edwards may be tempted by the opportunity.

Parker has gained promotion from the Championship with Fulham, Bournemouth and Burnley. He operated with possession-based football and defensive organisation within his ranks. However, his time at each club ended on a sour note in the Premier League with relegation campaigns.

Edwards has also gained promotion from the division with Luton Town, whilst showing positive signs at the likes of Middlesbrough. He deploys his sides with a compact, high-energy system, putting an emphasis on pressing and attacking width.

McKenna meanwhile has only managed Ipswich Town, taking them from League One to the Premier League. He left the club earlier this year, but the Tractor Boys would still be owed a sizeable compensation fee.

Although Parker has multiple promotions on his CV, a move for the Englishman may be a risk. He might take West Ham out of the Championship, but his spells at other clubs has indicated that he may not be able to keep them in the Premier League if they return.

McKenna would be a very exciting appointment for West Ham, however it would be a surprise to see him drop to the Championship again. He is the most attractive target of the three, but the most unlikely.

Edwards, may be a more feasible appointment though. He’s proven his promotion pedigree in the Championship and takes pride two things WHUFC are currently lacking: defensive organisation and battling spirit.

Time will tell if Nuno can turn the tide, but the Hammers are seemingly prepared if they do opt for change.

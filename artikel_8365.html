The Wait Is Over: The First Major Ski Resort in North America Is Open for the 2026-’27 Season

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

(Photo: Courtesy of Arapahoe Basin/Riley Kisser)

The wait is over: Arapahoe Basin opens today, October 28, becoming the first major ski area in North America to start spinning lifts for the 2026–’27 season. Well, spinning a lift, we should say: The Colorado resort will fire up Black Mountain Express at 11 a.m., giving skiers access to High Noon, an intermediate run, as A-Basin begins its 80th anniversary season.

Opening-day festivities include live music in the base area all day, giveaways in the lift lines, and plenty of early-season stoke. The 6th Alley Bar & Grill opens at 9 a.m., with Black Mountain Lodge opening in the afternoon.

Ikon Pass and Ikon Base Pass holders have unlimited access to A-Basin all season, and lift tickets and season passes are available for purchase at the ticket window. No parking reservations needed today; reservations are only required on Saturdays and Sundays from Jan. 2 through May 2, 2027. Check the resort’s blog for added info.

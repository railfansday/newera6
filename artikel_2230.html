Martin O'Neill explains Jota omission before Falkirk clash

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Martin O'Neill has explained his decision to leave Jota out of Celtic's matchday squad against Falkirk.

The Portuguese winger has only recently returned from long-term injury with an ACL problem and he has made four appearances since his comeback against Motherwell last month.

He was not named in the squad at all by the manager for this afternoon's test against the Bairns who are sitting sixth in the Scottish Premiership and in decent form of their own.

O'Neill, though, put all worries about a potential setback to bed as he revealed the playmaker has been left out due to the fact that Falkirk play their games on an astroturf pitch.

Asked on Sky Sports why the wide man is not involved and whether that may be something for punters to worry about, the Northern Irishman calmed those fears.

"No, he's just back from the injury and I think in terms of the pitch, we felt it's best with the type of injury he's recovering from, to leave him," he said.

Celtic found themselves a goal down to Ethan Ross' finish within the first three minutes of action. At the time of writing they remain 1-0 behind.

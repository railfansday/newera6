What makes you think gmail is not encrypted?
Well, mine is.
How about if I answer a slightly different question or two: Why doesn’t Google encrypt Gmail in the first place?

- It is in fact encrypted, but Google holds the keys.

Why isn’t it encrypted like Protonmail, so-called Zero Knowledge encryption?

- There would be no more machine learning.
- There would be no more spam or phishing filters.
- Direct targeting for ads has stopped (2017), but there is an indirect form via machine learning.
- No more Search. That’s a big one.
- Lost your password? No more gmail account. (This is, in fact, how Protonmail works, as Proton really is encrypted).
In short, it’s bad for business.

There is a service you can buy from Google, Workspace Client-Side Encryption (GSE). I’ve only heard about it here and one other place. It may be popular with businesses. I don’t know.

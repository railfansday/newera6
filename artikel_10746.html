Influencer who flaunted private jets indicted over alleged $4 million food stamp fraud scheme

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A social media influencer has been arrested after allegedly stealing millions of dollars from a government food assistance programme.

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) provides financial support to those with low income, allowing eligible households to purchase groceries using electronic benefit transfer (EBT) cards.

Atlanta-based influencer Mercedes Reshard New, also known as Shawty Dred, has been accused of exploiting the scheme to obtain more than $4 million in benefits between January 2020 and July 2026.

According to the US Department of Justice, the 39-year-old allegedly created more than 350 fraudulent SNAP accounts using stolen identities, before arranging for EBT cards to be delivered to addresses across Atlanta.

Prosecutors also claim that New registered his business, Shawty Dred LLC, as an authorized SNAP retailer, allowing him to withdraw more than $660,000 through transactions involving the fraudulent cards.

After his retailer authorization was revoked in November 2025, New allegedly approached Walmart shoppers and offered to pay for their groceries using EBT cards in exchange for cash.

New was indicted on 63 federal charges, including mail fraud, aggravated identity theft and SNAP fraud, before being arrested on October 13.

During a court appearance on October 22, New pleaded not guilty and was denied bond, with prosecutors arguing that he posed a flight risk. He remains in custody awaiting further proceedings.

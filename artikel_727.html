‘Dancing With the Stars’ Disney Night Rescheduled Ahead of Midterms: When and Where to Watch Online

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Cynthia Erivo joins this week's judging panel, while co-hosts Julianne Hough and Alfonso Ribeiro will participate in the evening's highly-anticipated team dances.

Fan-favorite DWTS Disney Night returns with guest judge Cynthia Erivo. Given Tuesday’s live election coverage, this week’s episode instead airs on Monday, Nov. 2 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on ABC, available to stream live via DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV. It will also stream on Disney+, which doesn’t offer a free trial of its own, but is included in any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which do offer the aforementioned free trial.

Disney Night 2026 airs live on Monday, Nov. 2 at 8 p.m. PT/ET on ABC (carried on live TV streamers like DirecTV, Fubo, Sling and Hulu + Live TV) and will be simulcast on Disney+.

Since the competition show airs on ABC, it’s also available to watch live on any streaming service that carries said network, including DirecTV (with a five-day free trial), Fubo (also with a five-day free trial), Sling and Hulu + Live TV.

The special Monday episode will also be available to stream live on Disney+, and while the streamer doesn’t offer a free trial directly, fans can get free access through any of DirecTV’s Signature packages, which include the Disney+ and Hulu bundle and offer a five-day free trial.

Learn more about each plan option, including how to build your own channel lineup (starting at just $20.99 per month), at directv.com.

In select markets, ABC is included in Sling’s budget-friendly Select plan, from $19.99 per month; Sling’s Blue plan, from $45.99 per month; and Sling’s Orange & Blue plan, from $60.99 per month. Visit Sling.com to check station availability.

Watch ABC with Hulu + Live TV. Subscriptions come bundled with Disney+ and ESPN+, and start at $89.99 per month.

Cynthia Erivo will guest judge the Nov. 2 episode alongside Carrie Ann Inaba, Bruno Tonioli and Derek Hough.

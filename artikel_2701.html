See how the Brasileirão table looks after matchweek 34's opening games

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Matchweek 34 of the Brasileirão kicked off on Monday (2) with just one match.

Playing in Bragança Paulista, Santos lost to Bragantino, who leapfrogged Santos in the standings thanks to the three points.

Rodinei opened the scoring with an own goal, Coutinho equalized, and Wallace Yan secured the win for the team from the São Paulo countryside.

Both teams remain in the hunt for a place in next season's Libertadores, relying mainly on an increase in the number of qualifying spots available through the Brasileirão.

The matchweek will unfold throughout the week, with at least one game a day until Friday (6).

Until then, plenty could change in the title race, the battle for Libertadores qualification, and the fight against relegation.

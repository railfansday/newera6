LiveWire set to show off several new electric motorcycles next month

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

LiveWire is preparing to bring its biggest electric two-wheeler lineup yet to EICMA (Milan Motorcycle Show) next month, including a pair of previously unseen models that will offer a preview of where the electric motorcycle company is headed in 2027.

The company will exhibit alongside its majority shareholder Harley-Davidson at the massive Milan motorcycle show, running November 5-8, where its booth will span everything from STACYC electric balance bikes to the lightweight S4 Honcho platform and the more performance-oriented DUST family.

Perhaps the biggest surprise will be the first public appearance of the new S4 Honcho Skate. LiveWire hasn’t revealed much about the bike yet, other than confirming it will expand the Honcho family when it arrives in 2027. We won’t have to wait until EICMA for all the details, though, as LiveWire says preliminary specs and additional information will be released on November 2.

The Skate will join the S4 Honcho Street and S4 Honcho Trail, giving LiveWire a growing family of lightweight electric motorcycles built around its modular S4 platform. The production-ready Honcho Street will also make its European debut at EICMA before going on sale there immediately after the show. Its U.S. launch is scheduled for next week.

LiveWire is also continuing to flesh out its newly acquired DUST lineup. After acquiring the assets of Dust Moto earlier this year, LiveWire will show off a production-intent version of the DUST Hightail, which is targeting a 2027 introduction.

Alongside it will be another new model known as the DUST Spur, also planned for 2027. Like the Honcho Skate, details remain thin for now, with preliminary specifications coming November 2 and final production specs and pricing expected in the first quarter of next year.

The expanding lineup is particularly interesting because it shows just how far LiveWire is moving beyond the premium full-size electric motorcycles that defined the brand’s early years.

Between STACYC, the lightweight Honcho models, and the more dirt-focused DUST machines, LiveWire is increasingly building a ladder of electric two-wheelers aimed at riders across a much wider range of ages, experience levels, and budgets.

We’ll get a much better look at the newest rungs on that ladder in just a couple of weeks.

Tony Mowbray confirms early Blackburn Rovers winter transfer hope with key position targeted

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Blackburn Rovers have had a strong first quarter of the 2026/27 campaign, despite a challenging summer. Many at Ewood Park felt more was needed in the transfer window as only three new players signed, and now eyes are already on January.

Speaking to the Lancashire Telegraph, Tony Mowbray has confirmed one position he wants to strengthen is central midfield. He said: “We’ve talked about positional stuff and I think that’s very much an area where we can create competition for places. I’m sure we’ve got a department looking and I think within the next week or so I’ll be shown some players.”

Sam Morsy was signed on a free transfer and has made a positive impact at Ewood Park, but depth is limited and at 35, the Egyptian isn’t a long-term fix. Kristi Montgomery, Moussa Baradji and Jake Garrett are the other midfield options at present with Adam Forshaw out through a hip issue.

The Blackburn Rovers hierarchy have not got a great reputation. A dismal year of recruitment left them in relegation danger last season, and there have been justified concerns over their fate this term given just how few new arrivals there were in the summer.

Mowbray has made a good start to his latest Rovers stint as he gets a tune out of the group at his disposal, but they can not afford to rest on their laurels. If they do not strengthen in January, the Lancashire outfit are certainly at risk of a slide down the table.

It means Blackburn have to do business in the winter, and the midfield is a good place to start. Identifying targets now will allow them to make their moves early in the next transfer window, rather than scrambling for late additions like they have in recent times.

A new midfielder is clearly a priority. However, goalkeeper is another position of focus for Rovers amid doubts over Balazs Toth’s future as his deal runs down.

Morsy provides experience, leadership and pedigree in the middle of the park for Blackburn Rovers. Someone of a similar profile to him could be ideal, but perhaps a few years younger.

Montgomery is developing well and Baradji is settling into Championship football too, and they will both benefit from an experienced player alongside them. Targeting another central midfielder who has played plenty of football at a similar or higher level will be wise – someone who can guide their partner through the match and offer a bit of bite out of possession.

Forshaw and Morsy have proven to be players of value for Rovers, but at 35, the club will be aware that they are not long-term options. Perhaps a signing like the 2023 swoop for Sondre Tronstad would be ideal to keep the balance of youthful exuberance and experience in the middle.

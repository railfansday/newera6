Here’s What ELLE Editors Are Actually Buying From the Sephora Sale

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

From K-beauty masks to hot tools, these are the editor-approved picks worth adding to cart.

Time waits for no one, so if you’re looking at the calendar and wondering whether October is too early to start holiday shopping, Sephora has the answer. It’s officially a beauty lover’s favorite time of year—our Super Bowl, otherwise known as the annual Sephora Savings Event. For those unfamiliar, each year Sephora runs a tiered discount event for about a week (this year it begins today, October 30, and runs through November 9) that puts the whole site on sale—yes, all your favorite brands, products, and gift sets are up for grabs.

Of course, there’s a tiny caveat: the sale opens at different times throughout the week with different percentages off your purchase depending on your membership tier. If you’re a Rouge member, the highest tier, today is your day to shine with 20 percent off; VIB (15 percent) and Beauty Insider (10 percent) shoppers will join in on the fun starting on November 3. However, if you’re shopping the Sephora Collection, no need to wait, as the entire offering is 30 percent off for everyone from the moment the sale opens. Use code ALLONSALE at checkout to save on everything.

While this time of year is sure to inspire holiday gifting, the sale is also a perfect time to stock up on tried-and-true formulas or finally check off your beauty wishlist. Looking to enhance your “everything” shower? Start with classics from L’Occitane or discover new favorites from Cyklar. If hair or skin tools are calling your name, editor picks from Shark and T3 are worth the investment. We understand parsing through the sprawling pages of Sephora could be quite intimidating if you don’t have a game plan. Luckily, ELLE editors’ Sephora sale picks are too good to ignore. Ahead, scroll through the items our shopping-obsessed editors can’t get enough of.

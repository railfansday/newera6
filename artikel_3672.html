A PUBG TV Show Is Coming To Netflix, Starring Donald Glover

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Krafton and Netflix today announced a live-action TV show based on PUBG, and it will star Emmy-winner Donald Glover. In fact, Glover will not only star, but also direct, write, and executive produce the show, along with his brother, Stephen Glover, as writer and executive producer.

That's all Krafton had to say in its announcement, so it sounds like early days for the PUBG series. A PUBG animated series is on the way, too, from Castlevania's Adi Shankar.

Donald Glover is no stranger to video games, as he voiced Yoshi in The Super Mario Galaxy Movie this year. He also partnered with Ubisoft for a Far Cry 4 trailer.

Krafton has maintained that it sees PUBG not just as a game, but a connected universe, so it's only fitting that the company would get into TV.

PUBG is a battle royale game where players duke it out to be the last person or team standing. The game remains immensely popular, boasting more than 785,000 peak concurrent users on Steam alone today.

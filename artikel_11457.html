Inde Navarrette, Michael B. Jordan and Yerin Ha Front David Yurman’s New York-Set Holiday Campaign

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A peek into the luxury jewelry brand's 2026 holiday campaign, unraveling through a collection of quintessential New York moments.

If you purchase an independently reviewed product or service through a link on our website, The Hollywood Reporter may receive an affiliate commission.

David Yurman is no stranger to a star-studded holiday campaign, so just as the calendar struck November, the luxury label unveiled its latest iteration for the 2026 gifting season. Obsession‘s Inde Navarrette and Bridgerton‘s Yerin Ha join Academy Award-winning actor and David Yurman Global Brand Ambassador Michael B. Jordan in the cinematic seasonal campaign, aptly titled Share the Warmth, which plays out in a series of quintessential New York vignettes shot throughout the snow-dusted city.

Pictured in citrine, pink tourmaline and diamonds; available in four variations.

Pictured in 18k yellow gold with rubies; available in six stone variations.

Of course, the trio is adorned in some of the brand’s most striking designs — a combination of new releases and recognizable DY favorites. The campaign video and imagery, unveiled Monday, Nov. 2 on DavidYurman.com, are accompanied by a collection of curated holiday gift guides, organized by price, style and recipient: gifts for her, gifts for him, symbolic gifts, classic gifts, statement gifts and so on. “Recognized for their distinct creative perspectives and culture-defining performances in 2026, Navarette and Ha embody David Yurman’s unwavering commitment to individuality and self-expression,” reads the accompanying campaign material. “Having captivated audiences with powerful portrayals of complex, culturally resonant characters, all three talents demonstrate a dedication to craft and artistic excellence that reflects David Yurman’s rich heritage and enduring values.”

Sterling silver with 14k yellow gold and diamonds.

David Yurman’s 2026 holiday campaign was directed by Bradford Young and photographed by Drew Vickers. The full campaign debuts Nov. 2 on David Yurman’s website and social channels. Visit DavidYurman.com to shop the full gifting assortment.

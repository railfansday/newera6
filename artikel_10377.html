Pixel Watch 5 matches all-time low price on Amazon

Black Friday is still weeks away, but holiday tech discounts are already arriving early. Joining the recent price drops across Google’s flagship hardware lineup, the Google Pixel Watch 5 has once again hit its lowest price ever on Amazon.

Pulisic comments after renewal and derby success: “The bond I have with this club”

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Christian Pulisic experienced a brilliant 48 hours over the weekend, following up his renewal with another big derby moment.

With a new contract signed on Friday and a goal in the derby on Saturday, it was a weekend to remember for Pulisic. The American expressed his joy on his Instagram, also thanking the fans for their overwhelming support during the difficult months the preceded.

“Renewing my contract with AC Milan has always been my dream. The bond I have with this club, this city, and our incredible fans means everything to me. The club and the fans have always been there for me in difficult times, never ceasing to believe in me.

“I will never forget this support, and I am determined to repay it. I want the next few years to be the best ever, full of unforgettable moments and victories. Let’s make history together. Forza Milan.”

It took the American just four minutes to break the deadlock against the city rivals, ghosting in between two defenders to meet a teasing cross from Diego Moreira and guide it inside the far post.

Pulisic was again at the heart of a lot of the good things that the Rossoneri did in what was an overwhelming first half. With Gerry Cardinale watching on, he will be proud and relieved to have resolved the long-term future of his ‘franchise player’, as he put it.

If you’ve tried every app and trend to lose weight and nothing’s stuck, listen up. This app isn’t about food restriction; it’s about checking under the hood (your brain) to see how things function and what makes it tick.

Noom Weight is more than your average weight loss app. It’s giving ‘therapist, coach, dietitian,’ but also ‘bestie who’s been through this before, and wants to follow you on your journey’.

It’s a mix of psychology, coaching, flexible food tracking, and a community of folks who have been where you are (yup, even there).

Noom’s goal isn’t to fix you. It wants to be the guardrails in the noisy world of wellness, helping you figure out what your body actually needs to live its best life.

The magic touch is its psychology-based approach. It’s less about what you’re eating and more about the why.

A gut check, essentially (pun-intended), to help you understand your habits. All without the side of guilt.

There are no ‘forbidden’ foods here. Noom uses a color-coded system to help you understand which foods offer the most nutrients without the side of guilt.

The psychology-based program helps you understand and unlearn your old habits and helps you build new ones that can last longer than before.

If you’re looking for real-life experience with Noom Weight, have we got one for you.

Kimberly Currin, Kim, tested Noom for over a year. Month after month, she checked in with us and shared her candid, vulnerable experiences so we could understand how she handled the process.

The time came with struggles and roller coasters, but with the additional resources Noom provided, Kim was able to get to the place she is now. At month 11, she celebrated 20 pounds of weight loss with Noom.

Going forward, she’s excited to have the knowledge that she is capable of managing her relationship with food.

“I feel like it’s no longer controlling me; I’m managing it,” Kim said. “I know my body, I know my cycle, I know my hormones, I know my brain. So, the journey continues.”

Lasagna Alla Vodka Skillet

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Lasagna and penne alla vodka are two Italian-American staples that go hand in hand. Because our one-pot lazy lasagna skillet is a weeknight favorite of mine, it only felt right to consolidate these two classics under one roof (or, into one pan). Making this lasagna alla vodka skillet is far easier than you’d think. It’s actually super lazy: You make an all-vodka sauce base by frying tomato paste in oil and burning off the vodka before adding some chicken broth. Lasagna noodles are broken into pieces, cooked in the broth until tender, and folded into heavy cream, Parmesan cheese, and butter for a rich, comforting dinner that looks just as stunning as it tastes.

Top tips:Always grate your own cheese. Saving time is important, but good ingredients are a game-changer. Your lasagna could go from mediocre to absolutely amazing. Also, use double-concentrated tomato paste. It’s double the richness and overall better tasting. It’s also important to cook out the rawness of the tomato paste in the hot oil, so don’t skip that step.

Storage: Many people say lasagna tastes better after it sits in the refrigerator overnight, so I’d recommend saving some leftovers and eating this the next day, especially if you’re like me and you like your pasta on the drier side. The sauce soaks right into the noodles with just enough left for coating the outside. Leftovers can be stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 days.

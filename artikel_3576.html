This Is the Robot Vacuum I Use Every Day, and It's 50% Off Right Now

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

I live in a 1,600-square-foot home, and vacuuming and mopping it by hand takes a considerable chunk of time. Passing that labor off to a robot vacuum was a great decision. I decided to go with the Roborock Qrevo CurvX since it seemed like a great fit for my circumstances. If you also have a dog, a mix of different floor types, and a gym with a workout mat, this robot vacuum might work for you, too. Amazon currently has it for $749.99 (originally $1,499.99), a 50% discount, and the lowest price it has ever been, according to price-tracking tools.

One of the main things I was looking for in a robot vacuum was suction power. The Qrevo CurvX has 22,000 Pa, which is stronger than most options at its price and is powerful enough to pick up anything that needs vacuuming in my house. I like that it has a self-lifting chassis so it can climb over my workout mat, rugs, obstacles like cables, or anything else that might be in the way, so I don't have to babysit it while it's doing its job. I also like the extendable mop pads and side brushes that allow it to reach corners and crevices it might not be slim enough to fit into. (Speaking of which, it's only 3.14 inches tall, so it can fit under most couches and beds without issue.)

My experience with the Roborock app has been great. It's easy to use and gives me enough control to clean my house how I want without a hassle. "No Go Zones" are easy to set up, as is the schedule. The main issue I've had is the size of the internal dust bin: It's small, so it needs to be cleaned after basically every scheduled use (though that shouldn't be as bad if you don't have a pet that sheds a lot).

Take it from me: If you're looking for a reliable hybrid robot vacuum that can clean multiple types of floors, mop, and comes with a great app, the Qrevo CurvX makes a compelling case at the current $750 discount.

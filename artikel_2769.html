Packers to sign QB Cam Miller to practice squad

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Packers are signing free agent quarterback Cam Miller to the practice squad, Jeremy Fowler of ESPN reports.

The Dolphins waived him Aug. 30, and he remained a free agent until Monday.

The Packers have Jordan Love and Tyrod Taylor on the active roster. They still list Kedon Slovis on the practice squad.

The Raiders made Miller a sixth-round pick in 2025, and the Dolphins signed him off of Las Vegas’ practice squad late last season.

Miller, 25, has never appeared in a regular-season game.

86-year-old man and his dog found safe two months after disappearing at sea

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

An 86-year-old sailor and his dog have been found alive more than two months after disappearing during a voyage across the Pacific Ocean.

The pair were rescued roughly 460 miles off the coast of San Diego, California, on October 28, after spending more than two months without contact with the outside world.

According to the US Coast Guard, the man had been sailing aboard his vessel, Willow Wind, from Astoria, Oregon, to Pago Pago, American Samoa, accompanied by his dog.

However, nobody had heard from him since August 20, and authorities were eventually notified that he was overdue on October 24.

Three days later, on October 27, rescuers finally caught a break when an emergency beacon registered to Willow Wind transmitted a distress signal.

A Coast Guard aircraft located the damaged sailboat later that evening, setting the stage for a dramatic rescue.

As reported by PEOPLE, the commercial vessel Grand Orion arrived at the scene around 3:20 AM on October 28, but rough sea conditions initially prevented its crew from reaching the stranded pair.

After more than 14 hours, rescuers finally managed to bring both the man and his dog aboard at approximately 5:36 PM.

The following morning, a Coast Guard helicopter hoisted the pair from the Grand Orion, which was then around 170 miles west of Ventura, California, and transported them to San Diego.

Despite the ordeal, the 86-year-old reportedly suffered no major injuries. However, he was described as weak and having difficulty walking before being taken to Scripps Mercy Hospital for further evaluation.

His dog’s condition was not detailed, but both survived the ordeal. Their damaged sailboat was left drifting at sea.

“We are grateful to the merchant mariners and Coast Guard crews whose coordinated efforts brought this search to a successful conclusion,” Coast Guard Commander Jason Nguyen said.

The remarkable survival story follows another dramatic Pacific Ocean rescue in August, when two fishermen survived five days stranded at sea using a large fishing cooler as a makeshift boat.

Meanwhile, the lucky canine joins Bruce the German Shepherd, who was rescued earlier this year after drifting nearly 2.5 miles out to sea in an inflatable kayak.

As for the 86-year-old sailor and his companion, authorities have not revealed what caused their voyage to go wrong or how they managed to survive for so long without contact.

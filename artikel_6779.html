The Best-Dressed Guest’s Guide to the Holiday Wedding Season

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Every product on this page was chosen by a Harper's BAZAAR editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

Great dresses, fabulous accessories, and plenty of reasons to RSVP yes.

Gorgeous gowns, sleek stilettos, sparkling jewelry—there’s plenty to love about getting glammed up for a wedding. It’s a chance to have a little more fun with your fashion, whether that means wearing a more dramatic gown than you’d typically go for, trying a bold color rather than your usual neutrals, or finally breaking out those festive heels. Whether the invitation calls for a destination, beachfront ceremony or a cosmopolitan, black-tie celebration, there’s an art to dressing for the occasion while embracing your personal style.

And that’s why Bloomingdale’s is your go-to. From romantic florals and sweeping gowns to metallic heels, bold jewelry, and conversation-starting bags, there’s something for every kind of celebration. Ahead, 11 wedding guest finds so good you may even want to wear them for the holidays.

Moody florals feel especially right for a fall wedding, and Simkhai’s draped maxi matches the romance of a countryside estate or garden ceremony. This fluid, body-skimming silhouette is especially elegant; play up the deeper colors with burgundy heels and gold jewelry, or add a tailored blazer if there’s a chill in the air.

Make your bag the focal point with Cult Gaia’s sculptural olive-green clutch, complete with a wavy gold handle. The earthy color feels especially fitting for an outdoor wedding; pick up a floral-print dress in a similar shade, or pair it with butter yellow for a more unexpected color combination.

If there’s one shoe to have in your wedding-guest wardrobe, it’s a great gold sandal. Aqua’s strappy pair adds extra sparkle with crystal-trimmed buckles and a sculptural heel, making them a flawless match for everything from printed midis to black-tie gowns.

An asymmetric, off-the-shoulder neckline feels unexpected. The draped silhouette and uneven hem give it extra movement for a cocktail affair, especially when paired with sculptural earrings and barely-there heels.

A beach wedding calls for something a little more shell-ebratory. Simon Miller’s polished silver clutch has a playful seaside touch thanks to its sculptural shell shape and black tassel. It stands out against a silky slip dress or bold black gown.

Wedding guest dressing is the perfect excuse to bring out the statement earrings. Pair these BaubleBar chunky gold-and-blue studs with a simple one-shoulder dress for a minimalist cocktail look.

A black-tie invitation is your chance to fully send it, and this Amur gown delivers with dusty mauve silk, delicate lace appliqués, and romantic off-the-shoulder detailing. For an estate wedding or grand ballroom reception, add crystal earrings, a satin clutch, and a sleek updo to show off the neckline.

Loeffler Randall’s gathered leather design with gold ring detail keeps things understated but still has plenty of personality. Carry it with an evening gown or tailored suit for the big day, then bring it back out with jeans and a blazer for dinner.

A black-tie wedding invites a little sparkle. Jennifer Behr’s crystal-covered hoops bring the shine with a bold, rounded silhouette. Wear them with a strapless satin dress or a sharply tailored evening look, sweep your hair back, and skip the necklace to let the earrings catch everyone’s eye.

A printed floral slip dress is a stunning choice for a tropical destination wedding—especially one with an ocean view. Farm Rio’s fluid maxi looks beautiful with gold sandals and playful earrings.

Leave it to Jimmy Choo to make black heels feel anything but basic! With sheer floral lace, a pointed peep-toe, and a slender stiletto heel, these satin mules look especially gorgeous with a sleek column gown or silk midi for a formal evening wedding.

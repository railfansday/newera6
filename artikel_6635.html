Apple Cinnamon Bread

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Sorry, banana bread, there’s a new quick bread coming for the crown. Bursting with chunks of cinnamon apples, this easy bread is a MAJOR hit come fall. Gone apple picking? It's the perfect baking project to use up some of those goodies. Don't worry, you'll still have enough for apple pie, too. Our secret weapon? Orange juice! This loaf is perfectly balanced, not too sweet, and tender and fluffy. It pairs well with a cup of tea or coffee, but you can also totally eat it by itself as a festive midday snack.

Start by preheating your oven to 350° and lining a 9x5-inch loaf pan with parchment paper. Now you are ready to make your apple mixture! First, core and slice your apples into chunks, about 1 inch in thickness. In a large bowl, stir together chopped apples, lemon juice, brown sugar, and cinnamon until your apples are evenly coated in the sugar and spice mixture. Set this bowl aside, and get started on your batter.

In another large bowl, using a hand mixer, cream together butter and sugar until light and fluffy—about 2-5 minutes. Scrape the sides of the bowl continually to ensure everything is evenly combined. Then, add in your eggs and vanilla and beat until smooth. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour and baking powder. Then add this dry mixture to your butter mixture, keeping your mixing on low. Be careful not to over mix, which will result in an overly dense cake! Add in your orange juice and mix until just combined.

Now that your batter is done, scoop half of it into a greased loaf pan, spreading it out with a butter knife or the back of a spoon. Then spoon in half the apple mixture. Top this with the remaining batter, then press in the remaining apples on top.

Now your loaf is ready for the oven! Bake until your loaf is golden and a toothpick inserted into the center of the cake comes out clean, about 1 hour and 15 minutes. Let cool completely. Enjoy!

This apple cinnamon bread can be stored, covered, at room temperature for 3 to 4 days. You can also wrap it tightly in plastic wrap, then wrap it with foil or place it in a freezer-safe zip-top bag, and freeze for up to 3 months.

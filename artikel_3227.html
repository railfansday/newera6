The 1980s were a huge decade for Hollywood, delivering some of the most iconic films ever to hit the big screen. “E.T. the Extra-Terrestrial” became the highest-grossing film during that time, “Back to the Future” nearly had a different Marty McFly, and “Top Gun” helped turn Tom Cruise into a global superstar. Meanwhile, “Star Wars” continued its box office dominance, “Indiana Jones” went on another adventure, and Tim Burton brought “Batman” back to cinemas in a big way.

But how much do you actually know about the movies behind these record-breaking box office figures? This quiz will put your movie knowledge to the test with questions about the decade's biggest theatrical hits. From memorable characters to general trivia covering specific plot details, it’s time to see how much you really remember about one of the strongest decades for blockbuster movies.

Norwalk beats Ballard in Class 4A state volleyball quarterfinals

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

CORALVILLE — The Bombers gave the Warriors a scare, but there was no early exit for No. 1-seeded Norwalk.

Norwalk (31-6) gave up one set to Ballard (29-11) and had to come from behind in another, but the top-seeded team in Class 4A stayed alive with a 3-1 win over the Bombers in the state volleyball quarterfinals on Nov. 2.

"All eight teams in the state tournament are fantastic teams, they're here for a reason," Norwalk coach John Fulton said. "Ballard is a really scrappy, defensive team, and they don't make a lot of errors. And to get the first win is big.

"It's always different coming in as the No. 1 seed … so playing in that pressure, playing in that moment was good for our girls."

The Bombers ran into a buzzsaw in Elle Hatlevig, who proved impossible to shut down. She scored the opening point against Ballard, and she put up eight kills on a dozen attacks – and an ace – in the first set alone.

The Norwalk junior wasn't the only one piling on the points early, though. Myla Brown, Mary Bush and Delaynee Steele each recorded a service ace, and five players recorded a kill as the Warriors took the first set, 25-16.

Norwalk didn't run away with the second set. Ballard kept the score close, even building a six-point lead around the midway point of the set. The Warriors managed to close the gap and it took extra points to decide the second set, which Ballard took 28-26.

In the second set, Addison Hosteng and Kendall Ersland each recorded six kills, and Hosteng added an ace. Norah Mudd picked up four kills.

Ballard took the lead earlier in the third set, climbing to a 9-5 advantage before Norwalk called its first timeout. The Warriors scored the next point out of that break, but the Bombers responded by racking up five straight before Norwalk called its second timeout.

The Warriors made it a close game and forced extra points again, rallying to win 28-26.

"Set two and set three were just great volleyball," Fulton said. "It was really good for our girls to be able to play that type of tight volleyball because it's just going to get harder as we move on this week."

After trailing in both the second and third sets, Norwalk made a statement to start the fourth. The Warriors picked up four unanswered points, and they built a double-digit lead that Ballard couldn't overcome.

With a dominant 25-12 win in the fourth set, Norwalk advanced to a Class 4A semifinal matchup on Nov. 4 at 12 p.m.

It was a team win for Norwalk with Bush, Hatlevig and Steele each finishing with three or more aces. Six different players recorded blocks, including Olivia Winslow, who finished with two. Steele recorded 17 kills, and Hatlevig led the way with 32 kills.

"She does a lot for us," Fulton said of Hatlevig. "She helps us out for sure whenever we need somebody to put the ball away. She just sees the court so well, and she's played in a ton of big moments. So I can't say enough about her and the team around her."

The Bombers finished the season with a loss, but that doesn't take away from their history-making year. Ballard advanced to the state tournament for the first time in program history, and the Bombers made winning difficult for the No. 1 seed at Xtream Arena.

"Coming in, being that lower-ranked team, people just kind of expect you to lay down," Ballard coach Erica Dunkin said. "This team has grit and they have fight, and that's what takes them over the edge.

"We've got six seniors – they all play – so next year might be a bit of a rebuilding year. But (this season) shows the growth."

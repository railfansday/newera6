Evernorth going live on Nasdaq is a golden opportunity for XRP holders; industry expert explains why

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The company’s scheduled listing on Nasdaq demonstrates its dedication to redefining the accessibility of XRP and offering greater benefits to investors.

The year 2026 promises a new chapter for XRP, especially with Evernorth’s move to go public. In shared data, the crypto industry expert revealed the XRP treasury company’s plan to list on Nasdaq. According to the analyst, the move is set to unlock major opportunities for XRP investors and expand capital access to the XRPL ecosystem.

Evernorth is a public treasury company that specializes in buying and holding XRP as its main reserve asset. Rather than holding cash, the firm buys XRP and communicates with shareholders about its purchases, with capital preservation and liquidity as its goals. Since 2024, the treasury company has accumulated and held XRP tokens as part of its balance sheet to generate wealth.

Evernorth has committed to revolutionizing access to its digital asset treasury. The company is preparing for its listing on the Nasdaq stock exchange, according to the analyst’s revelation.

The successful definitive agreement, reached last month, paved the way for the firm’s public listing on the prestigious Nasdaq exchange. As per the latest announcements, Evernoth is scheduled to start trading on Nasdaq next week, October 8. With the listing, the firm will become a publicly traded company on the American stock exchange.

The development marks a significant milestone in the company’s journey towards entering the public market as XRPN. This strategic approach aims to further expand Evernorth’s already massive customer base, providing both individual customers and institutional investors with a better-regulated exposure to XRP investment.

As Evernorth prepares to trade on Nasdaq under the ticker name ‘XRPN’, the company is in a privileged position to foster adoption of XRP in North America and worldwide. This achievement reflects a future where XRP is accessible to investors worldwide, setting the stage for a more inclusive financial ecosystem.

With its upcoming listing on Nasdaq, the firm will operate as the first institutional XRP treasury model, offering global investors yield-generation opportunities, liquidity strategies, and DeFi engagement for long-term XRP investment and growth. In short, this approach transforms everything for XRP holders.

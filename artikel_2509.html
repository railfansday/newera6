DFB-Pokal round of 16 draw produces several big clashes

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The draw for the round of 16 of the 2026/27 DFB-Pokal took place on Sunday evening.

Defending champions Bayern Munich will host 2. Bundesliga side Hertha BSC, while last season's runners-up VfB Stuttgart have been drawn against Dynamo Dresden.

Other notable fixtures include a repeat of the 2017 DFB-Pokal final between Eintracht Frankfurt and Borussia Dortmund, as well as an exciting showdown between Bayer Leverkusen and RB Leipzig.

The round of 16 matches will take place on 1 and 2 December 2026.

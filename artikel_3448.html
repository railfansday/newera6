The Biggest Video Game of All-Time Officially Releases This Month

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

2026 has been a great year for video games so far, but the big whale with the capability of trumping them all is finally set to be released later this month. When it comes time to decide the Game of the Year at the Game Awards, there will be plenty of games in contention, including the surprise hit, Blood of the Dawnwalker. The game was developed by the same team behind The Witcher 3, which released a new remaster for next-generation consoles at the end of September. One of the biggest gaming disappointments of the year so far has been Marvel’s Wolverine, the new game from Insomniac, the same studio behind all three critically acclaimed Spider-Man games. Going in with high expectations, the release is not expected to be a contender for Game of the Year like all of its web-headed predecessors.

There have been some wild snubs for Game of the Year at the Game Awards over the years, and one of the most-talked about years in history is still 2018, when God of War won over Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar Games will have a chance to redeem itself in just over two weeks with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, which is widely viewed as one of the most anticipated video games of all time. It was originally thought that GTA 6 would be released in 2024 before the game was delayed to 2025, then May 2026, but it has finally settled on November 19, 2026, as the official launch date for the next Grand Theft Auto game. The 10+ year wait for a new Grand Theft Auto game is almost over.

Four action series. One of them is built around exactly the way you handle pressure, people, and a room that has just gone very quiet. Here is where you would survive the trilogy, and probably headline it.

You are precision with a grudge. You don't posture, you don't escalate, and you don't warn anybody twice. That is exactly why the people who know you are so careful never to be the reason you start.

You understand the thing the whole franchise is secretly about: the cars are spectacular, but the crew is the point. You collect people, you keep them, and you'd drive through a building for any one of them.

You over-prepare and then take the dangerous part yourself anyway. It looks like control, but it's really responsibility. You have never worked out how to ask somebody else to carry the risk.

You are not the most trained person in the building, the best equipped, or the one anybody would have picked. You are simply the one still standing at the end, which turns out to be the only qualification that matters.

There were three playable protagonists in Grand Theft Auto 5, Michael, Trevor, and Franklin, but all reports and released footage allude to there only being two in GTA 6, Jason and Lucia. The dynamic between protagonists will be much more detailed in the new Grand Theft Auto game, though, with players having the choice to decide how much effort to put into the relationship, which will directly affect things like their dialogue and mission availability. Grand Theft Auto 6 is expected to be more of a modern-day successor to Red Dead Redemption 2 than a sequel to GTA 5, which is music to the ears of most fans, who prefer the Western masterpiece to the modern, somewhat repetitive crime thriller.

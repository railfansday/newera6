Strongest DPS and Support units and best traits for each unit in Anime Galaxies!

Anime Galaxies Meta teams are the ones that balance Hybrid DPS units with other power-hitters and supports. I’ll guide you through the best units for each category as well as how to unlock key units. Here’s my Anime Galaxies unit and trait tier list and guide.

⇓ Best Support Units (Buffs/Debuffs/CC-Stalling) ⇓

There’s two categories when it comes to the best units in Anime Galaxies which are DPS and Support. The best DPS units are found in the S and A-Tiers of the tier list while the best supports are currently Tango, Megomu and Itoshi. I’ve marked the Hybrid units but if you’re looking for beginner units that can hit fliers go for Foji and Kizaru which are Legendaries.

Best Trait: Galactic or Chosen (Bounty Hunter for Spedwag)

How to Get Freezo: Farm Portals – They gradually upgrade and you have a 2% chance to get Freezo from the Secret tier Portal with a 100 Pity. You can buy Portals from the Event Shop or get them as a 1% drop from Infinite every 5 Waves.

How to Get Gowko: He drops from capsules at a 0.5% drop rate with a 200 guaranteed Pity. Get capsules from the Event Shop for 100 Rainbow Stars.

How to Get Demon King: Farm Endless to get an Invitation at 1% drop rate every 5 waves. Then, once you have it reach Wave 49 and you’ll get a prompt to use it and fight Demon King on Wave 50 with a chance to unlock him at 20% drop rate +5% per co-ops that also have an Invitation.

How to Farm Stars: Stars are drops from every game mode or from doing event quests. I recommend farming the first Story Act 2 on nightmare to get 10 per stage.

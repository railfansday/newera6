League One table predicted after Huddersfield Town & Barnsley boosts and Doncaster Rovers & Bradford City blows

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Opta have forecasted League One finishes for Huddersfield Town, Sheffield Wednesday, Bradford City, Doncaster Rovers, Barnsley and more

League One served up plenty of pulsating action over the weekend.

There was a West Yorkshire derby at Valley Parade, where Bradford City fell to a 3-0 defeat to Huddersfield Town.

Doncaster Rovers were on the receiving end of a 6-1 mauling away at Milton Keynes Dons, a result for which manager Grant McCann shouldered the blame.

Barnsley went down to 10 men early on against high-flying Oxford United, but battled their way to a 1-1 draw at Oakwell.

Sheffield Wednesday also had to manage with 10 men and shared the spoils in a 1-1 draw with Blackpool, with new manager Dave Challinor watching on from the stands.

Away from Yorkshire, Wycombe Wanderers defeated Burton Albion and struggling Wigan Athletic were 3-2 victors over Bromley.

Stockport County saw off AFC Wimbledon, Peterborough United were beaten by Luton Town and Mansfield Town lost to Notts County.

Leicester City were held to a 2-2 draw by Stevenage and it also ended level between Plymouth Argyle and Reading. Leyton Orient, meanwhile, cruised to a 3-0 win over Cambridge United.

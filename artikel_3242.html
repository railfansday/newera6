Apple includes an excellent built-in trackpad with the new MacBook Pro. But if you prefer a mouse, one of the best options on the market is Logitech’s MX Master 4. The long-awaited successor to the popular MX Master 3 has proven a hit since its release last year.

If you need an external trackpad because you plan to work with the MacBook Pro in clamshell mode, Apple’s first-party Magic Trackpad is the only option I’d recommend.

For raising the new MacBook Pro to eye level, I recommend Twelve South’s excellent Curve aluminum stand.

Twelve South’s Curve looks like the type of accessory Apple would build itself. Its sleek, minimalist aluminum design means you don’t have to choose between form and function—you get both.

The Curve raises your MacBook Pro six inches off your desk and includes anti-slip silicone pads for your Mac to sit on, thus preventing any slips or scratches.

Apple mercifully didn’t remove any of the MacBook Pro’s ports with its latest redesign. However, there are still plenty of reasons you might want a USB-C docking station for your MacBook Pro with PROLED XDR.

My go-to docking station is this 8-port Anker model that adds Ethernet, USB-A, and several more ports for your MacBook Pro.

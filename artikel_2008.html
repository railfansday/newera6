Football legends gather to remember Pompey goalkeeper Alan Knight

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Portsmouth football legends will unite for a special evening honouring the life and legacy of the former Pompey goalkeeper Alan Knight who died in September.

A spokesman said: “The Portsmouth football community was deeply saddened by the passing of Alan Knight, one of the most recognisable and respected figures in the club’s history. With his extraordinary longevity, loyalty and connection to the city, Alan became far more than a footballer. He was a true local legend.

“Alan had been due to be part of the Portsmouth Legends evening at The Kings Theatre. Following his passing, the event will now take on a new significance, becoming a tribute to Alan and an opportunity for fellow players, fans and the wider Pompey family to remember a remarkable man and goalkeeper.

“Joining the evening are Paul Merson, David James, Mark Hateley and Micky Quinn, who will share their own memories of their football careers, their experiences of playing at Fratton Park and, importantly, their memories and stories of Alan.”

Portsmouth Legends at The Kings Theatre Portsmouth will be on Thursday, November 12, with tickets available on https://www.kingsportsmouth.co.uk/whatson-event/portsmouth-legends-live/

“From unforgettable moments on the pitch to the characters, friendships and rivalries that made up life around the dressing room, the evening will celebrate the stories behind the football, with plenty of humour, affection and the occasional bit of mischief along the way.

“There will also be a live audience Q&A, giving supporters the chance to put their questions to the legends on stage and hear some of their own memories of Alan and their time in the game.

“For Portsmouth, Alan Knight’s name is woven into the fabric of the football club. His remarkable career saw him make 801 appearances for Pompey, a club record that reflects not only his ability but an extraordinary commitment to the team and the city he represented.

“Paul, David, Mark and Micky will share their own memories of Alan, alongside the stories, laughter and camaraderie that make football so special. We hope the evening gives Pompey fans the opportunity to come together and remember a truly remarkable man.

“The Kings Theatre Portsmouth is proud to host what will now be a very special tribute to Alan Knight – a night of stories, laughter, memories and, above all, a celebration of a true Pompey legend.”

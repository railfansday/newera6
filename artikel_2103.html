'Ticking bomb': Ex-striker's brutal Celtic warning as tensions threaten to explode

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Former Hibernian striker Brian Graham has branded Celtic a "ticking bomb" amid growing unrest at Parkhead.

Graham believes pressure is mounting on the club, with supporters unhappy with the board, performances below expectations and questions surrounding the team's direction. It comes after the Hoops' latest 2-0 defeat to the Bairns as they've now won just three games from their last 10.

Speaking on Superscoreboard, Graham also defended Martin O'Neill against potential calls for his head, insisting the Celtic boss deserves credit for last season's title success.

"If it wasn't for Martin Celtic wouldn't have won the league last year," Graham said.

He suggested O'Neill may have been frustrated by the club's transfer business, adding that supporters are unaware of exactly what has been happening behind the scenes at Lennoxtown.

Graham admitted Celtic's European campaign had been a "disaster" and questioned the circumstances surrounding Cameron Carter-Vickers' recent absence.

But his strongest criticism was reserved for the club as a whole.

"As us and as fans, we don't know what's going on in the background at Celtic Football Club. Martin might've wanted X, Y and Z in the transfer window and didn't get it. Clearly with a couple of his comments he wasn't particularly happy with how it ended," he added.

"It's not been a fantastic start for Celtic in terms of their standards and how they're playing at the moment but rightly so they're top of the league. Europe has been a disaster, we have to be honest with that one, so there's unrest within the camp, it's clear to see.

"When Carter-Vickers comes out and he doesn't play the next couple of games which was alarming for me and the so-called 'injury'. Was there? We don't know. Then he's back in, makes a mistake for the goal.

"Celtic on the whole as a club right now is a ticking bomb. They're ready to just go off and it's been like that for the last six months, there needs to be some sort of resolve.

"There's been a lot of fans not happy with the board and that's been the case for a long time. Something has to give. Surely."

Racing shoes get all the glory, but you’d never make it to race day without reliable workhorse shoes for training and recovery. If your feet are aching for a cushy, comfortable shoe that’ll help them rest and recover after a hard workout, meet the Brooks Glycerin Max 2: a generously cushioned, supportive trainer ideal for recovery days, easy jogs, and run-walk sessions. Normally it comes with a hefty $200 price tag, but right now Brooks has it on sale, bringing the price in line with typical training shoes—this is your golden opportunity to cop a pair.

The Glycerin Max 2 is on sale at Brooks for $150, a 25% markdown (it normally retails for $200). The sale price applies to all colorways in both men’s and women’s, and plenty of sizes are in stock, so you’ll have lots of choices. While other retailers like Dick’s Sporting Goods and Zappos match this sale price, I couldn’t find a better deal anywhere else, and Brooks has the best size and colorway selection.

To make the Glycerin Max 2, Brooks took its traditional high-cushion trainer, the Glycerin, and puffed up the midsole even more, giving it a towering 45mm heel stack height. This shoe is built around a thick block of DNA Tuned midsole foam, a nitrogen-infused compound that features larger gas bubbles in the heel and smaller bubbles in the forefoot. This “tuned” design makes the foam softer at the heel for a cushioned landing, and firmer and more responsive at the forefoot for springy toe-offs. To make the massive sole more manageable, Brooks gave the Glycerin Max 2 a pronounced rocker shape for smooth transitions. Finally, it’s finished with a thick, well-padded upper for a comfy fit.

Testers really appreciated the Glycerin Max’s plush, supportive ride when they wanted to take things slow. “When my legs were tired and ready for an easy run, these shoes are a go-to,” said tester Craig Wallace. Tester Matthew Fronheiser agreed, and in addition to the underfoot cushioning, he also noted the padded, snug-fitting upper “seriously rocks.” And in her review, RW Editor Jamie Sorcher said the Glycerin Max “motivated me to add that extra mile rather than cut things short and head for home.”

This shoe is selling fast at Brooks, and many size-colorway combos are already sold out. Snag your pair for 25% off now before they’re all gone.

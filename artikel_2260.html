Ryan Naderi jokes he'll ‘stick to the easy ones’ after firing Rangers to final

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Ryan Naderi shared the glory after his winner against Aberdeen booked Rangers a place in the Premier Sports Cup final.

The Light Blues had improved after the break at Barclays Hampden after a mostly lacklustre first half and after several periods of sustained pressure, the 23-year-old striker pounced inside the box to knock in a strike from James Penrice in the 83rd minute – scoring his eight goal of the season.

Naderi said: “It feels amazing. I’m happy, of course, happy to be the one who scores the goals, but happy for the team.

“I think we deserved it. We believed we could score right up until the very end and we did, and I think it’s a deserved win.

“I mean it’s a semi-final. No matter who you play against, they’re going throw in everything.

“They’re going to try to defend with every man. Also a big compliment to them because they did well. They threw themselves into everything and made it difficult for us to score.

“But as I said, we kept going, pushing for that goal and as I said, I think it’s a deserved win.”

It was another close-range strike from Naderi and he joked about this proficiency from distance.

The Germany-born Czechia international said: “I’ve tried to shoot twice today from outside of the box and it didn’t go well.

“So I went back inside the box. Maybe I should just stick to the easy ones!

“As I said, I’m just happy how it is right now and I’m going to try to stick to it.”

Rangers will face Hearts or Hibernian in the December 13 final, with the Edinburgh sides facing each other at the national stadium on Sunday.

Asked if he felt the confidence will be there to go and win the trophy, he said: “Yes, we will definitely come here to win that thing, not to lose it.

“We’ve come this far so we want be there standing with the trophy in our hands, of course.”

Tallulah Willis Reacts to Hateful Comments About Appearance

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Demi Moore and Bruce Willis' daughter Tallulah Willis urged people to "choose kindness" as she shared insight into some of the hurtful comments she receives online about hear appearance.

Tallulah Willis is calling out the haters.

The youngest of Demi Moore and Bruce Willis’ three daughters opened up about the impact that negative comments about her appearance online can have on her.

"People seem shocked -genuinely- when I share my experience being called 'medically disfigured' 'or something to fear,'" Tallulah, 32, captioned a Nov. 2 Instagram post. "I don't often read these things, but when I do I remember why I almost starved to death after a lifetime of disordered eating."

It’s why artist—whose sisters include Rumer Willis, 38, and Scout Willis, 35, as well as Mabel Willis, 14 age, and Evelyn Willis, 12, whom the Die Hard star shares with wife Emma Heming Willis—leads her life with intention. And it’s something she hopes others can do too.

"I take pride in that fact that I choose kindness everyday," she continued, "but I just hope to share with you all the realities that someone who has been unstoppably vulnerable in sharing their ups and downs still gets treated like this on certain corners of the internet. and for the record I am far from what nightmares are made of."

The 32-year-old—who had previously discussed her struggles with disordered eating—added the hashtag "#choosekindness" to her post, which featured a screen recording of the comments section under a post that declared that she was the "mirror image of dad in throwback photo."

Some of the comments featured said things like "Bruce is prettier" and "she's not easy looking on the eyes."

Tallulah commented on the original post herself, too, writing, "Def could have picked a cuter photo but," along with a shrugging emoji.

This isn't the first time that she has put a spotlight on the online bullying she’s been subjected to for the way she looks. Last year, she called out at Perez Hilton for the way that the gossip blogger treated her when she was a teenager.

"The face, the smile, dare I say it the chin (hey @perez remember when you bullied a 13 year old for money?) that made me want to end it all,” Tallulah wrote on Instagram last October, “is now the most precious gift I could be bestowed with.”

"I used to not be able to say the word chin out loud," she added at the time. "It was my dirty sinful malady, center stage for all the world to see. At least that’s what you guys said, over and over and over and over. What a cursed child I was for inheriting this joyful, expressive feature, alive with emotion!"

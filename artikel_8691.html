Skip the Total Visa® Card unless secured card issuers have turned you down. Its $95 program fee and its first-year annual fee of $50 to $125 are charged before you buy anything. Open a no-fee secured card instead, since its deposit comes back to you.

The Bank of Missouri issues it for applicants with poor credit who have a checking account, and the card’s agreements on file with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau set starting credit lines of $200 to $500. The First Access Visa Card and the Revvi Card charge the same fees at each of those lines.

The Total Visa makes sense only after secured card issuers have turned you down, since it reports to all three credit bureaus without a deposit. Anyone who can spare a refundable deposit should open a no-fee secured card instead and avoid $145 to $220 in first-year fees.

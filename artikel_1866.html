Liverpool 0-1 Arsenal: Highlights, man of the match, stats as record Kai Havertz goal sends Gunners top

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

By Ben Knapton | 01 Nov 2026 18:27, Last updated: 01 Nov 2026 18:41

Arsenal are back on top of the Premier League table thanks to their first top-flight win at Liverpool in over 14 years - an incredibly gritty 1-0 success at Anfield.

On the hunt for a first Premier League win at Anfield since Mikel Arteta's playing days in 2012, Arsenal's quest started positively, and the holders deservedly broke the deadlock through a record-breaking Kai Havertz goal in the 16th minute.

The German's tap-in sparked a fierce response from Liverpool, but despite having 28 touches in the Arsenal box in the first half - and having four penalty appeals turned down - Andoni Iraola's men somehow trailed at the break.

The second period followed a largely similar pattern, but for all of the hosts' huffing and puffing, David Raya only had one save to make as the champions held out.

Arteta's side have now opened up a three-point lead over Manchester City at the Premier League summit, while Liverpool stay fifth, nine points adrift of the holders.

An ugly win. Away from home. Against a title rival. The hallmark of champions.

It does not take a football aficionado to deduce that Arsenal arguably did not deserve to come away from Sunday's game with all three points, as after dominating the opening 15, the Gunners were under the cosh for almost all of the remaining 75.

However, thanks to a combination of perpetually diligent defending, Liverpool's wasteful finishing and a few questionable calls from Andy Madley, the champions exorcised their Anfield demons in nail-biting fashion.

Top of the tree once again, the title is mathematically now Arsenal's to lose, and Arteta undoubtedly got the response he was after from Tuesday's horrendous EFL Cup exit.

Liverpool fans can rightly feel frustrated at Madley, but the Reds generated just 0.19 Expected Goals more than Arsenal on the night, and just one shot on target from 17 attempts means that their proposed title challengers label comes with an asterisk.

iPhone sales lifted Apple to record September-quarter revenue of $114.5 billion

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Free daily briefing on global business news.

iPhone revenue set a September-quarter record and Services reached an all-time high, while iPad revenue missed estimates

Apple $AAPL reported record September-quarter revenue of $114.5 billion on Monday, up 12% from a year earlier, thanks to soaring iPhone revenue and an all-time record in Services revenue.

Fiscal fourth-quarter revenue beat analyst expectations of $113.39 billion, according to Reuters. The quarter ended Sept. 26.

Apple stock barely moved in after-hours trading.

Diluted earnings per share were $2.10, up 14%, including a $0.05 benefit from tariff refunds. Gross margin was 47.9%, including a benefit of about 1 percentage point from the refunds. Analysts expected earnings of $1.99 per share and a gross margin of 47.5%, according to Reuters.

iPhone revenue was $57.58 billion, Mac revenue was $10.79 billion, and iPad revenue was $6.23 billion, the company said. Analysts had estimated $56.54 billion, $9.61 billion, and $6.70 billion, respectively, according to Reuters.

The iPhone figure covers eight days of iPhone 18 Pro sales and excludes the iPhone Duo, which reached stores after the quarter ended, according to CNBC.

"Apple delivered September quarter records for net income and EPS," CFO Kevan Parekh said in a statement. Apple generated $28 billion in operating cash flow during the quarter and returned $28 billion to shareholders. Revenue for fiscal 2026 reached $479 billion.

Apple's board declared a cash dividend of $0.27 per share, payable Nov. 16 to shareholders of record as of Nov. 12.

Apple raised prices on some iPhone and MacBook models while memory-chip costs climbed, according to Reuters.

"We did see higher memory costs as compared to the June quarter, which was partially offset by the benefit of carrying inventory and lower costs on certain non-memory components," CEO John Ternus told Reuters. "These price increases provided some offset."

Ternus told Reuters that supply of the iPhone Duo trails demand. "Duo is constrained as of today," he said. He said a "very small percentage" of Duo devices were sent back over screen defects, and that Apple is swapping those out for new ones. He added that Apple is "not able to predict when supply and demand will be in balance."

Apple had reported June-quarter revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16% from a year earlier, in the last quarter under Tim Cook as CEO. Ternus took over on Sept. 1.

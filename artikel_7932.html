Heartleaf Skullcap: A Deer-Resistant Native Perennial

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

When you garden with ecology in mind, choosing plants becomes less about you and more about the local birds, mammals, and invertebrates that could benefit from your latest addition. Here, Amy Galloway Medley shares heartleaf skullcap, one of her favorite underappreciated native plants.

Watch: Super Cool Plants Podcast | Underappreciated Native Plants

One of my favorite native species right now is heartleaf skullcap. The fuzzy, heart-shaped leaves are beautiful, and they produce an oil that makes the plant very deer resistant. It looks fantastic planted in masses in a perennial border, with spikes of blue-purple flowers that are similar to those of salvias (Salvia spp. and cvs., Zones 5–11) and that attract hummingbirds.

Some claim that heartleaf skullcap is aggressive, but I find it doesn’t over-compete or crowd anything if you use it for filling those trouble areas where nothing else will grow. In central Texas, most of our native plants prefer full, scorching sun, but this is one that thrives and blooms in dry shade. It’s also impressively cold hardy and makes a wonderful container plant.

Learn more from Amy about this plant on Fine Gardening’s podcast, Super Cool Plants, or watch the episode on our YouTube channel!

Amy Galloway Medley is the lead horticulturist at Lady Bird Johnson Wildflower Center at the University of Texas at Austin.

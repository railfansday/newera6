Last Chance To Save 20% On Enchanted Lights at Hill Dickinson Stadium

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Anyone looking to enjoy a magical festive experience at Hill Dickinson Stadium is being reminded that they have until Sunday 1 November to take advantage of a special 20% Early Bird discount on Enchanted Lights tickets.

The brand-new festive experience will transform Everton's waterfront home into a spectacular Christmas destination from Friday 11 December to Wednesday 23 December, with dazzling lights, festive entertainment and activities for visitors of all ages.

Visitors will begin their journey on Everton Way, following an illuminated trail filled with festive displays and photo opportunities before entering the stadium through the West Stand Players' Entrance.

Following in the footsteps of Everton's stars, guests will make their way through the Players' Tunnel and into the stadium, where they will take their seats pitchside for a Christmas light show.

The festive experience continues at 'Christmas in The Yard', a specially created Christmas village offering seasonal food, drinks and live entertainment.

Families can also add a visit to Santa's Grotto, giving younger visitors the opportunity to meet Father Christmas.

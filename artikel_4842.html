Lexus announces prices for its first 3-row electric SUV in Europe and the US

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Lexus announced prices for its first fully electric three-row SUV, the 2027 TZ, with sales set to begin in the US later this year and in Europe in early 2027.

The new TZ is the largest, most luxurious Lexus electric SUV to date. It will go on sale by the end of 2026 in the US, and early 2027 in Europe.

In the US, the 2027 Lexus TZ will be available in three trims, starting at $64,050. The base TZ 450e Premium AWD model will be available to order in late 2026, followed by the Premium Touring AWD and Luxury AWD trims in early 2027.

The three-row electric SUV will be available with two battery packs: 76.96 kWh and 95.82 kWh. The former provides an EPA-estimated range of up to 253 miles, while the latter gets up to 315 miles.

All TZ variants come with an evolved version of Lexus’ DIRECT4 AWD system. The dual-motor AWD setup delivers a combined 308 horsepower.

Inside, the three-row electric SUV features the latest Lexus Interface multimedia system. The setup includes a 14″ multimedia touchscreen display with customizable widgets, a Voice Assistant, and full-screen navigation on the digital cluster.

The 2027 Lexus TZ is 200.8″ long, 78.3″ wide, and 67.1″ tall, with a 120.1″ wheelbase, or roughly the same size as the Hyundai IONIQ 9 and the Volvo EX90.

The base 2027 Lexus TZ 450e Premium AWD will be available to order later this year, starting at $64,050 with up to 253 miles of range.

In early 2027, the Touring and Luxury models will go on sale, starting at $72,050 and $80,550, respectively. Both use the extended-range 95.82 kWh battery. The Touring model offers up to 315 miles of range, while the most expensive Luxury trim gets 279 miles.

Lexus said the electric SUV can recharge from 10% to 80% in about 35 minutes at up to 150 kW. It also includes an NACS charge port so you can access Tesla Superchargers without a separate adapter.

Lexus revealed TZ prices in several European markets ahead of its official market launch in early 2027.

In the Netherlands, it will be available in three variants: 450e Standard Range, 450e Long Range Executive Line, and 450e Long Range President Line.

The base Standard Range model starts at 79,995 euros ($90,000), including a limited-time 4,000 euro discount.

It uses the same 77 kWh and 95.8 kWh batteries, offering a WLTP range of up to 280 miles (450 km) and 338 miles (544 km), respectively.

In Spain, the 2027 Lexus TZ will go on sale starting at 88,000 euros ($99,000), with prices ranging up to 105,000 euros ($118,000) for the top-spec Luxury two-tone variant.

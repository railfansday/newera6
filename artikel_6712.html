Ariana Grande Shares Rare Photos With Ricky Alvarez After Their NYC Marathon Outing

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Ariana Grande has been taking a planned break from the spotlight since finishing her Eternal Sunshine Tour in September, but the singer gave fans a rare glimpse into her day-to-day life—and rekindled romance with Ricky Alvarez—on Instagram today. Grande posted a carousel, kicking the post off with a shot of Alvarez embracing her. In another photo, she kisses him.

The post comes hours after the couple was seen supporting runners during the New York City Marathon.

Grande and Alvarez, who originally dated from 2015 to 2016, were first romantically linked again in July.

The singer will return to the public eye this week as she embarks on her press tour for Focker-in-Law, which comes out on November 25. This Sunday, she’ll appear with her co-stars Robert De Niro and Ben Stiller on The Tonight Show.

On September 2, one day after her tour ended, a source told People that the actress remained “committed” to promoting the film. “She is very proud [of it],” the insider said, adding that she will continue to support her brand work during her break, too.

In August, Grande spoke to fans at her Chicago show about her decision to take a break. “It’s a plan that I had quietly made a long time ago, and it’s a decision that was made from a thoughtful and empowered place,” she explained. “And I want you to know that many things can be true at the same time. I want to share this because I heard that my fans were worried that negativity was ruining things for me, but I just have to say that could not be more the fuck opposite.

“I promise you, this is not what that is,” she continued. “And I just want to be very, very clear. Nothing has been ruined. Multiple things can be true at the same time. Yes. Boundaries can need to be set. Human beings can need a break sometimes. And also, this can be and will continue to be the greatest experience of my professional and creative life. Nothing is getting in the way of that or will get in the way of that.”

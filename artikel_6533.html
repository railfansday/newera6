Instant Pot Mashed Potatoes

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Thanksgiving is nothing without mashed potatoes alongside the turkey. But making the classic recipe can take up precious time on an already hectic holiday morning, so it's worth finding a shortcut. This Instant Pot version makes the process easier, cutting down on both cooking time and dishes. With this easy guide, you'll have the creamiest mashed potatoes of your life in only 30 minutes—no stovetop required.

Add the potatoes, water, and a generous pinch of salt to your Instant Pot. Secure the lid, set it to manual and high pressure, then cook for 12 minutes. When the timer goes off, do a quick release. Carefully drain any extra liquid and move the potatoes to a large bowl. Mash them until they're nice and smooth.

While the potatoes are cooking, melt the butter with the milk in a small pot over medium heat until it's warm. Pour this mixture over your mashed potatoes and stir until everything's creamy. Mix in the sour cream and season with salt and pepper to taste.

Spoon the mashed potatoes into your favorite serving bowl and add a pat of butter on top. If you like, sprinkle on some extra pepper before serving.

The full list of ingredients and instructions can be found in the recipe below.

Make-ahead dishes are key during the holidays. You can make these mashed potatoes a few hours in advance and keep them warm using the “Keep Warm” setting on your Instant Pot. Just give them a stir every once in a while to make sure they don’t stick, and consider adding a splash or two of warm milk if they thicken up while they sit.

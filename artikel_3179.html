Palantir Grows Revenue 96% as New Deals Slow: Rapid Earnings Reaction

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Palantir's latest quarter showed a business turning rapid growth into heavy profit. Here's what drove it and where the numbers look softer.

Palantir Technologies (PLTR -3.13%) grew third-quarter revenue 96% to about $2.31 billion and raised its full-year outlook again. Shares were up about 2% in after-hours trading. The business is turning growth into profit at a rare pace. But new contract signings barely grew, so future growth depends on signings picking up.

U.S. commercial revenue passed U.S. government revenue for the first time, with government at $953 million. Government contracts are big and sticky, but budgets move slowly. Commercial customers can expand fast once one deployment pays off. That speeds growth but adds exposure to corporate spending cycles.

Profit outran sales because most of Palantir's costs don't rise with revenue. Operating expenses grew about 46% while revenue nearly doubled. Software costs little to deliver once it's built, so most of each new dollar falls to profit.

The soft spot is new business. Palantir closed $2.92 billion in total contract value, up just 6% from a year ago. Revenue is recognized from contracts already signed. When signings grow far slower than sales, the company works through its backlog faster than it refills it. Big deals are lumpy, so one soft quarter isn't a trend yet.

The key number is U.S. commercial remaining deal value, which rose just 4% from last quarter while U.S. commercial revenue rose 29%. If that backlog doesn't speed up, today's growth rate will be hard to hold.

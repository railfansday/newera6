All Gizmos, effects, and rotation in Fortnite Treasure Hunters

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Treasure Hunters season in Fortnite features plenty of different Gizmos you can find on your runs. While this isn’t the first time we have seen them, I am delighted that they’re back, and some of them are overpowered.

During the Treasure Hunters season, you can find five different Gizmos to add to your collection. All five have unique in-game abilities that activate when you use them.

There are different ways to obtain Gizmos in the Treasure Hunters season. You’ll get Daily Quests to complete that will reward you with Gizmos. As of writing, we only have information about the current one.

We will have to wait for now to find out which Gizmos will be available throughout the course of this week. Other methods involve opening Pirate Treasure chests where they can drop as rare loots. Finally, you can also find Golden Fireworks, Portable Extractor, and Extraction Accelerator from the Sprite Mastery Track.

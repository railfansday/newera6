Brewers stars Jackson Chourio and Brice Turang take in Marquette game

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Milwaukee Brewers have been all over the place since winning the World Series title.

Players have popped up at Raising Cane's, gone trick-or-treating in the suburbs, sat courtside at a Milwaukee Bucks game and even made a cameo appearance on "Saturday Night Live."

On Nov. 2, Brewers players Jackson Chourio and Brice Turang took in the Marquette men's basketball team's season-opening game against Mercyhurst at Fiserv Forum.

Chourio and Turang both were awarded Rawlings Gold Glove Awards the day before, but they didn't bring their hardware to the game.

Instead, they indulged fans with selfies and got a big cheer when shown on the big screen.

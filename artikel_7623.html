Judge advances McDonald’s McRib lawsuit that alleges deceptive marketing

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A federal judge in Illinois on Friday did not dismiss a proposed class action lawsuit accusing McDonald’s McRib of misleading customers about the sandwich’s ingredients. (Photo from Getty Images)

A federal judge in Illinois on Friday did not dismiss a proposed class action lawsuit accusing McDonald’s McRib of misleading customers about the sandwich’s ingredients.

The plaintiffs allege that the McRib, which costs $7, is shaped to look like ribs and misleads customers in ads to think that it contains rib meat instead of cheaper pork cuts.

In Chicago, U.S. District Judge April Perry of the Northern District of Illinois dismissed some claims, but she did not accept the McDonald’s argument that a reasonable customer could expect the McRib to contain rib meat. Perry said ads of the rib-shaped patties could possibly be an attempt to defraud, but more proof was needed, according to coverage by Reuters.

“Plaintiffs’ proffered understanding of ‘McRib’ is not unreasonable or fanciful,” Perry wrote.

McDonald’s contends that it does not use hearts, tripe or stomach as McRib ingredients, which the suit alleges. The fast-food giant, which has sold the sandwich since 1981, said distinctive product names do not fool reasonable consumers.

“This lawsuit distorts the facts, and ⁠the claims are meritless,” McDonald’s said in a statement.

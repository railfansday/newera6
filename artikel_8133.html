The New Family-Friendly Shows & Movies Coming To Disney+ & Hulu In November 2026

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

We're getting a Proud Family holiday special, two stunning new nature documentaries, and surprise: a reboot of Netflix's The Circle.

This time of year is always a whirlwind, with its constant Halloween events, Daylight saving time, and holiday planning. So yeah, when you hit the couch at the end of another long day, it’s nice to know there’s going to be something good to watch (and preferably new, you know?). Here are all the new family-friendly TV shows and movies coming to Disney+ and Hulu in November 2026. And yes, fortunately for us, they’re adding some classic Christmas movies to stream too. Is it even the day after Thanksgiving if you can’t watch A Christmas Story?

Millennial Disney Channel stans, rejoice! While we don’t have a trailer just yet, Disney+ has confirmed we’re getting a Proud Family Christmas special, in a brand new animation style but still starring Kyla Pratt as Penny (omg). The logline reads, “After Penny Proud inadvertently destroys Christmas as we know it, she reluctantly agrees to make things right and save Christmas for the children of Emilyville and the world.” I simply cannot wait to revisit these characters as an adult and share them with my own kid.

A Bluey spin-off? Don’t mind if we do. This sweet series is based on the episode “Fancy Restaurant,” in which Bingo and Bluey do their best to create a romantic dinner date for their parents. In this show, real-life children do the same, cooking and decorating and revealing their idea of a “smoochy-kiss” worthy date to their mums and dads.

That’s right, the former Netflix series The Circle is getting a reboot on Hulu, and viewers can expect some big changes. The streaming platform recently revealed its new host, Tinx, and confirmed that there will now be celebrities in the Circle as well as unknown contestants (aka regular people). It will also be filmed in real time with audience votes affecting gameplay.

The Circle will air three new episodes every week, on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays for five weeks beginning on Nov. 17. While we’ll miss Michelle Buteau’s hilarious commentary, we’re so glad to have this family-friendly gem back in our queues.

Wow, this gets me so excited for Christmas movie season...

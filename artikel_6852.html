Beyoncé Looks Incredible in a Green Tinsel Skirt and Tropical Blouse at Jay-Z’s Show in Los Angeles

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Beyoncé doesn’t dress for events like other people. So, when she made a surprise appearance on night one of Jay-Z’s back-to-back shows at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, last weekend, she did so in a fresh-off-the-runway look, naturally.

Last Friday, the record-breaking Grammy winner showed up to support her husband as he closed out his Jay-Z 30 Tour, which began with a series of performances in New York City this past July and was followed by sold-out sets in London and France.

Queen Bey took in the concert with her former Destiny’s Child bandmates, Kelly Rowland and Michelle Williams, as well as her mom, Tina, and eldest daughter, Blue Ivy. Of course, fans quickly shared clips of the appearances to social media.

Now, we’ve got a full look at Bey’s ensemble, thanks to new photos uploaded by her stylist, Ty Hunter.

The tropical-inspired ensemble was pulled from the Roberto Cavalli Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection, and it certainly brought a summery sensibility to these increasingly colder days. The top was printed with renderings of pink-and-white flowers with bright-green stems, while the plunging neckline and flowy sleeves were decorated with tattered fringe.

Just as it appeared on the runway, the top was paired with a seaweed-green midi skirt covered in strands of matching tinsel. But, Beyoncé and her stylist added their own accessories, including a diamond necklace from the Messika High Jewelry 2026 collection, a pair of oversize sunglasses, and pointy leopard-print pumps.

One thing about Beyoncé is she’ll always show up for the ones who are important to her—and she’ll always do it in style.

Racial comments to attorney sparked investigation of federal judge

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

U.S. District Judge Ada Brown of the Northern District of Texas is accused of making racial comments to an attorney. (Photo from Facebook/Judge Ada Brown for SCOTUS)

A lawyer who filed a misconduct complaint against a federal judge in Dallas did so after a hearing during which she repeatedly directed racial insults at him, Bloomberg Law reports.

Documents show that attorney Ezra Young, who represented a Black woman professor suing over a tenure denial, filed the complaint following the April hearing in Dallas. His complaint and the response from U.S. District Judge Ada Brown of the Northern District of Texas were among the documents that Brown circulated in an Oct. 17 email to lawyers, judges and members of the media, Bloomberg Law reports.

Throughout the hearing, Brown, a Black woman with Native American ancestry, accused Young of mistreating Black attorneys, took “judicial notice” of her own background in making findings against Young, and repeatedly referred to Young, who is Latino, as being white.

“I think what’s happening here, quite candidly, is you are very intimidated for some reason, Mr. Young, with Black excellence, in light of your white mediocrity. Yeah. Right?,” Brown said, according to the transcript. “You should write that down. I mean, you—I’m—you look like a white man, right?”

“I’m not white, your honor,” Young replied, adding that he’s Latino.

Young filed the complaint, saying he thought that the judge may be suffering from a mental health condition. A judicial investigation resulted in her temporarily losing her docket earlier this month. A special judicial committee has ordered Brown to undergo a psychiatric examination as part of that investigation.

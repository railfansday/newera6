Judge accused of making racial remarks, skipping court is no-show at her own hearing

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

U.S. District Judge Ada Brown of the Northern District of Texas failed to show up to a hearing Friday to address misconduct allegations. (Photo from Facebook/Judge Ada Brown for SCOTUS)

A federal judge in Dallas—accused of refusing to take cases, making racial insults and abandoning the bench—has failed to appear at an investigatory hearing about the allegations.

U.S. District Judge Ada Brown of the Northern District of Texas did not show up Friday for a judicial committee hearing regarding concerns that a mental condition may be interfering with her ability to perform her duties, Law360 reports.

A three-judge special committee of the judicial council of the 5th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals at New Orleans met Friday morning to address two complaints against Brown. When she did not arrive, the committee paused for 30 minutes to give her more time but ended the proceedings without her.

Brown is accused of delaying rulings on motions, closing cases with pending motions, recusing herself from cases for unusual reasons, making “racial insults,” sending “bizarre” emails to colleagues in the middle of the night, and failing to come to work, according to documents released by the 5th Circuit’s judicial council.

Law360 attempted Friday to reach an assistant in Brown’s office but had not received a response.

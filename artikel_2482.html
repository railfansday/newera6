NWSL playoff schedule set

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

After all eight Decision Day games had the potential to impact the standings, we now know the seeding and matchups for the 2026 National Women’s Soccer League playoffs.

At the top, NJ/NY Gotham FC locked in the No. 1 seed by defeating Utah Royals 2-0. They will host No. 8 Seattle Reign at 12:30 pm ET on Sunday, Nov. 8, on ABC. The Reign secured their spot by defeating the Orlando Pride 1-0 on Decision Day, making North Carolina’s 1-0 win over the Denver Summit moot, as those were the two other teams vying for the last playoff spot that Seattle took.

Also hosting, the No. 2 seed San Diego Wave won their final game of the season 1-0 against Bay FC, but they didn’t get the Gotham loss they needed to take the Shield. Utah Royals were knocked further down the table by their loss to Gotham and will enter the playoffs as the No. 7 seed, playing in San Diego on Friday, Nov. 6, at 10 pm ET on Prime Video.

The third hosts will be the Washington Spirit, who defeated the Chicago Stars 2-0 to keep their spot in the standings. As the No. 3 seed, they will host the No. 6 Portland Thorns on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 3 pm ET on ABC. The Thorns won their Decision Day matchup against the Houston Dash 2-0.

The last playoff quarterfinal will feature No. 4 Kansas City Current hosting No. 5 Angel City FC on Saturday, Nov. 7 at 12 pm ET on CBS. Kansas City ensured their hosting rights with a 2-0 win over Racing Louisville, while Angel City ran rampant with a 4-1 result over the Boston Legacy.

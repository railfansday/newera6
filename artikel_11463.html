Harry Kane offers major update on his new Bayern Munich deal

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Crowned as the new Ballon d’Or winner during an emotional ceremony in London last week, things couldn’t be going any better for Harry Kane.

Becoming the first Englishmen since Michael Owen way back in 2001 to win football’s most lucrative accolade, the England skipper has embarked on an astonishing run since completing a blockbuster move to Bayern Munich three years ago.

Getting his hands on consecutive Bundesliga crowns and shattering a string of long-standing records, Kane also became the first Bayern player since 1981 to lift the Ballon d’Or.

Remarkably notching 156 goals across all competitions for the German giants, it is no secret that both parties have been in discussions over a bumper new deal at the Allianz.

While there might have been tentative links over potential moves to both Barcelona and Real Madrid this year, the 33-year-old has made it abundantly clear that his sole focus is on extending his record-breaking tenure in Munich.

In what looks set to be a famous announcement, Kane confirmed on Tuesday afternoon that he is about to sign a new contract and has speculated that an announcement could come in a “matter of days”.

Speaking ahead of Bayern’s Champions League showdown against Atletico Madrid on Tuesday night, Kane said:

“There’s been good conversations over this weekend and today as well. I know I keep saying we’re close, but we are extremely close now”.

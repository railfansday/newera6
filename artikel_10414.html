Don’t get confused by the names. The DH series is meant primarily for home use, while the DX series is meant for either home offices or small offices. The main difference is in storage capacity and capability.

The home-use DH series works as a great way to cut down on all those annoying cloud storage subscription costs and ensure that your data stays in your home instead of some data center. If you’ve got a sizable movie collection, you can also digitize it and turn it into a streaming media server. It’s super cool and a great addition to simply storing all those files that no longer fit on your computer.

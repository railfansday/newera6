Introducing the 2026 Tech Trendsetters

HousingWire’s 2026 Tech Trendsetters award recognizes 75 technology leaders advancing the housing industry through artificial intelligence, automation, data intelligence and digital innovation. Today, HousingWire announced the 2026 Tech Trendsetters, recognizing 75 technology leaders whose innovation, leadership and execution are advancing the housing industry. Now in its eighth year, the Tech Trendsetters award honors the people developing, improving and implementing the technology transforming how housing professionals work and serve their customers.

“The 2026 Tech Trendsetters are turning today’s most promising technologies into practical solutions for the housing industry,” said Sarah Wheeler, Editor in Chief at HousingWire. “Their leadership is helping organizations work more efficiently, make smarter decisions and deliver better experiences for housing professionals and consumers. We’re proud to recognize the people setting a new standard for technology and innovation across housing.”

This year’s honorees represent organizations across mortgage lending, servicing, real estate, capital markets and housing technology. Their work spans artificial intelligence, automation, data intelligence, digital lending, property technology, risk management and customer experience.

These leaders are turning emerging technologies into practical solutions that deliver measurable results. Through new platforms, smarter workflows and more connected systems, they are helping housing organizations operate more efficiently, make better-informed decisions and create stronger experiences for professionals and consumers.

“This year’s Tech Trendsetters represent the very best of housing innovation,” said Clayton Collins, CEO of HousingWire. “In a period of economic headwinds and rapid technology evolution, these leaders are turning AI, data and digital innovation into real business and consumer outcomes. Their work is making the housing industry more efficient, more connected and better prepared for what’s next, and we’re proud to recognize them.”

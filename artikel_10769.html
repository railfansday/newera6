Ambulance driver accused of leaving chest pain patient to Uber home after getting lost

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A private ambulance driver in Cleveland, Ohio, has been accused of abandoning a patient after getting lost, telling her he was “done driving for the day” before calling an Uber home.

The incident happened on Wednesday, October 28, while the woman was being taken to Cleveland Clinic with chest pain, according to Arizona’s Family.

As the story spread online, the Cleveland Association of Rescue Employees (CARE) Local 1975 stepped in to clarify that the driver did not work for Cleveland EMS.

“We want to clarify it was NOT a Cleveland EMS ambulance, but rather a provider working on a private ambulance,” the union wrote. “We stand by our EMTs, Paramedics, and Dispatchers who strive to provide the best care to anyone who is in need of it.”

The union also urged people to “research the facts before blindly sharing AI-generated images and articles,” pointing to an image that had been circulating alongside the story.

The driver worked for Lynx EMS, a private ambulance company. He reportedly got lost on the way to the hospital before allegedly telling the patient he was done for the day and leaving in an Uber.

A Cleveland EMS unit was sent to the scene, where paramedics treated the woman and took her to the hospital.

Lynx said it had taken immediate steps to address the situation and is reviewing the incident, but declined to comment further as it involves personnel and patient matters.

Butterball unveils free Thanksgiving gadget that makes guests do chores

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Butterball has unveiled a new Thanksgiving gadget designed to get dinner guests involved in holiday preparations by assigning them chores throughout the day.

The turkey company is giving away the Butterball Buddy, a turkey-shaped device that helps hosts delegate tasks instead of handling everything themselves.

The limited-edition gadget arrives ahead of Thanksgiving 2026, with Butterball planning three separate giveaways throughout November.

The Butterball Buddy is designed to make assigning Thanksgiving chores easier by giving guests tasks to complete during holiday preparations.

Rather than leaving all the cooking, cleaning, and other responsibilities to the host, the device encourages everyone to contribute.

The gadget features a turkey-inspired design and takes a playful approach to managing the work involved in preparing Thanksgiving dinner.

Guests pass the Butterball Buddy around during the gathering. When it chimes and vibrates, the person holding it receives a preprogrammed task, such as setting the table, helping prepare food, or washing dishes.

Once the task is complete, the guest passes the device to someone else, allowing chores to be distributed before, during, and after the meal.

The concept was inspired by Butterball’s 2026 Togetherness Report, which found that 85% of Thanksgiving hosts would prefer guests to offer help, but only 38% actually ask them to pitch in.

According to the company, one-third of hosts hesitate to ask for assistance because they don’t want to inconvenience their guests.

The Butterball Buddy also has a second use. After Thanksgiving, it can function as a kitchen timer, while the back of the device includes contact information for Butterball’s Turkey Talk-Line.

Butterball is giving away the gadget through its dedicated Butterball Buddy website, with the first batch available beginning November 2 at 1 p.m. ET.

Additional quantities will be released on November 9 and November 16 at the same time. All three giveaways are limited to available supplies.

Butterball is also reopening its Turkey Talk-Line on November 2, marking the service’s 45th anniversary.

More than 50 experts will be available through December 24 to answer questions about preparing, thawing, cooking, and serving turkey.

Hosts can reach the service by calling 1-800-BUTTERBALL, texting 844-877-3456, or contacting the company through its website and social media channels.

Butterball isn’t the only company looking to make Thanksgiving preparations easier this year. Costco is also offering a massive 30-pound Thanksgiving dinner for $199.99, complete with turkey, sides, and desserts for eight people.

The meal is available for preorder through November 13, with deliveries scheduled between November 4 and November 20. Unlike Butterball’s gadget, Costco’s offering handles much of the meal preparation, although customers will still need to cook the turkey and heat up the sides.

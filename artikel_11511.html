3 teams emerge as landing spots for potential Josh Downs trade

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

With Josh Downs another potential trade candidate for the Indianapolis Colts, NFL insider Jordan Schultz says there are three teams to keep an eye on.

"I would say keep an eye on the Steelers, Chiefs, and Commanders, among others, and then don't be surprised if other teams we haven't even considered yet, because he's valuable and he's set to become a free agent," Schultz said.

Reports suggest that the Colts are looking for a third-round pick in return for Downs, but Schultz doesn't think Indianapolis lands that selection.

"The Colts want a third-round pick for him, but I think ultimately they'll probably get a fourth," said Schultz.

Downs reportedly requested a trade last week. He is currently in the final year of his rookie deal and is set to be a free agent this upcoming offseason.

A third-round pick by the Colts in 2023, Downs has caught 30 passes this season for 330 yards with one touchdown.

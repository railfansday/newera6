Disney Night Returns on ‘Dancing With the Stars’ With a Broadway Twist: See the Scores, Who Went Home (Updating Live)

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Cynthia Erivo joined as the night's special guest judge in a night meant to honor Disney stories and their stage adaptations.

[This story contains spoilers for episode nine of Dancing With the Stars season 35.]

It’s time for Disney Night on Dancing With the Stars.

This year’s Disney-themed episode sports a new Broadway twist, with “performances inspired by beloved Disney stories and their iconic stage adaptations,” per ABC. Couples are paying homage to classics from Mary Poppins, The Little Mermaid, Tangled, Beauty and the Beast and many more.

Tuesday night’s episode also saw the return of the team dances, as co-hosts Alfonso Ribeiro and Julianne Hough each joined one of the group performances. Cynthia Erivo was also in the ballroom, joining as the night’s guest judge. Celebrities still competing on season 35 include Tatyana Ali, Jenna Dewan, Ezra Frech, Amber Glenn, Maura Higgins, Harry Shum Jr., Julia Stiles and Connor Wood.

During last week’s Horror Movie Night, Jackson Olson and Tyler Cameron were kicked off the competition in a double elimination.

The couple with the lowest combined total of judges’ scores and viewer votes is eliminated at the end of each DWTS episode. Each viewer is allowed up to 10 votes per couple, per voting method (via ABC’s website and by text).

Live voting takes place during the East Coast airing of each show and ends shortly after the final performance; that means if you’re a West Coast viewer and want to vote, you must do so between 5 p.m. PT and roughly 6:45 p.m. PT, as voting ends after the final performance.

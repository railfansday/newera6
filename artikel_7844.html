Pamela Anderson’s Best Garden & Landscaping Tips, Straight From Her Backyard

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The actress and activist shows how a thoughtfully designed landscape can create a memorable first impression.

In Pamela’s Garden of Eden, Pamela Anderson transforms her grandparents’ former property into a peaceful, sustainable retreat. In the second episode, Anderson and her team reimagine the landscape surrounding the “roadhouse,” a tree-shaded home with plenty of character but an undefined entrance and overgrown gardens.

By rethinking how visitors move through the space—from the first step onto the property to the front door—they create a lush landscape that’s both welcoming and romantic. Here are a few ideas worth borrowing for your own yard.

A latticed wooden arch sets a clear threshold, making the transition into the garden feel inviting and purposeful rather than purely decorative.

Flagstone pavers set in an irregular pattern create a path that feels naturally worn rather than newly laid.

A landscape refresh doesn’t have to mean starting over. Anderson and her team transplanted existing plants with their roots intact, preserving established growth while reshaping the layout.

What could have been an all-white patio setup is elevated with subtle patterns and soft colors, adding personality without overpowering the space.

Swapping gray trim for white and introducing black window frames adds contrast and gives the exterior an updated look without structural changes or a major investment.

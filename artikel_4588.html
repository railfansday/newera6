Exactly solvable model reveals how trapped light can create magnetic order in one-dimensional spin chains

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A research team wanted to better understand how the forces between particles inside a material change. They discovered that by confining one-dimensional classical spin chains inside a mirror box cavity with light, they could force these magnetic particles to organize and undergo a phase transition. Their work provides the simplest exactly solvable examples showing that light can create new phase transitions with materials.

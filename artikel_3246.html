Here’s how to listen live to Apple’s Q4 2026 earnings call

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Later today, Apple will release its Q4 2026 earnings, with hardware price increases and ongoing component shortages expected to play a big part as the company heads into its first holiday season under new CEO John Ternus. Here’s how you can listen in live.

Half an hour after today’s closing bell, Apple will report its earnings for the fourth fiscal quarter of 2026, followed by a conference call where Apple CEO John Ternus and CFO Kevan Parekh will discuss the results.

This will be Ternus’s first earnings call since taking over from Tim Cook in September, and the conversation will likely center on how Apple navigated another quarter of memory shortages and rising component costs, as well as how it expects those challenges to affect its performance going into the holiday season.

During its previous earnings call, Apple projected revenue growth of between 9% and 11% year over year, despite significantly greater supply constraints affecting the iPhone, Mac, and iPad. If that forecast holds, quarterly revenue should come in between $111.69 billion and $113.74 billion.

The results will include just a few days of sales of the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, which launched on September 18. However, analysts will likely be particularly interested in Apple’s outlook for the current quarter, which includes the launch of the iPhone Duo, the new touchscreen MacBook Pro, and the HomeView smart home hub.

Apple’s conference call is scheduled to start at 2:00 p.m. PT / 5:00 p.m. ET, with the executives delivering prepared remarks before turning to analyst questions.

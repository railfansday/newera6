9 Best Scalp Shampoos for Your Scalp to Help Dryness, Flakiness, and Sensitivity

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

By now, we know that scalp care is haircare. As excepted, then, the days I let my routine slip and I don’t tend to my scalp are when my hair will look dull, dry, flaky, and overall just “meh.” Before I reach for a serum or some sort of scalp mask, I look to the one product that really is the foundation of my hair routine: a good scalp shampoo

Over time, I’ve learned that picking a shampoo for your scalp really depends on what you’re looking to treat. Is your scalp super oily? Are you dealing with dandruff? Have you gone too product heavy and need a detox? Or perhaps you’re scalp has been close to one too many hot tool, and could use some soothing ASAP? For every scalp concern, we tapped the experts to break down the best shampoos for the scalp that they’d actually recommend. Read on below and get ready to click *add to cart.*

What do dermatologists recommend for scalp?

Dermatologists always recommend a good shampoo for the scalp to give it a thorough cleanse. But which one depends on your specific needs and how well your skin will tolerate certain ingredients. “I wouldn't choose a shampoo simply because it says, ‘sulfate-free,’” adds Dr. King. “The important question is whether the overall surfactant system effectively cleans without irritating your particular scalp.”

“Shampoos with effective surfactants are best for cleaning the scalp,” says Dr. King. Some of those surfactants she says to look for include sulfates like sodium lauryl sulfate (SLS) and sodium laureth sulfate (SLES), but you can look for gentler ones like sodium methyl cocoyl taurate.

Dr. Erickson says that if you’re dealing with a dry scalp, you’ll want to skip harsher surfactants like sulfates, and fragrance to minimize irritation. Instead, look for hydrating, barrier repairing ingredients like ceramides, niacinamide, glycerin, and panthenol to pair with gentle surfactants or exfoliators.

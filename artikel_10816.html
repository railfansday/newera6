YouTuber JaackMaate dropped from Christmas event after single complaint over “edgy” 2014 videos

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

YouTuber JaackMaate has been dropped from a Christmas lights event in his hometown after someone complained about his “edgy” videos from 2014.

British YouTuber and podcaster Jack Dean, better known as JaackMaate, was originally selected to switch on Norwich’s Christmas lights on November 19 alongside his Happy Hour co-hosts Stevie White and Robbie Knox.

The invitation was particularly special for the 33-year-old, who grew up in Norwich and described the opportunity as a “bucket list dream.”

However, just two weeks after announcing his involvement, Norwich City Council has withdrawn his invitation over concerns about his previous content.

According to the BBC, Dean revealed that the decision came after “one person complained to the council about my old ‘edgy’ online videos from 2014.”

“I’ll no longer be doing the Christmas lights,” he said.

“Gutted. Not because I won’t be pressing a button, but because someone out there still thinks the teenager version of me is a reflection of who I am today, as a 33-year-old man.

“Sorry to anyone that was going to come… I don’t know how many times I can apologize for the same thing. Ultimately, I did say stupid stuff online when I was 19, but I promise you guys, I like to think I’m a decent bloke.”

In a statement reported by the Eastern Daily Press, Norwich City Council confirmed it had reconsidered its decision to feature the YouTuber.

“Following the council’s announcement to confirm the selection of JaackMaate as the personality to switch on Norwich’s festive lights, we have reviewed our decision,” a spokesperson said.

“As a result, the council has decided to make a change to its plans for the Festive Lights Switch-On and JaackMaate will no longer be appearing.”

The council also admitted it had failed to properly review Dean’s previous content before extending the invitation.

“In reviewing our arrangements, we failed to identify issues from previous published content before announcing him. We apologize for this and will be changing our procedures.”

However, officials have not revealed which specific videos prompted the decision, meaning it’s unclear exactly what content the council found objectionable.

The cancelation is particularly awkward given that Norwich’s Lord Mayor personally invited Dean and his co-hosts to participate after learning it was one of their dreams.

In the original announcement, Dean said he had attended the event since childhood and called being chosen a “pinch yourself moment.”

For now, the council says it will announce revised plans for the November 19 celebration shortly.

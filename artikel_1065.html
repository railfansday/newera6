Hull City and Ipswich Town have faced each other 43 times over the years, but never in English football's top division - until now.

Both clubs won promotion from the Championship last season and made promising starts to the 2026/27 campaign, but the hosts have begun to find the going a little tougher since winning their opening two games.

The Tigers have the joint fourth-best defence in the league - with just eight goals conceded in eight matches - and this explains why they have been able to continue picking up points despite their six-game winless run, drawing four of those fixtures.

Sergej Jakirovic's side remain unbeaten at home but they will feel this is a great opportunity to claim their first victory at the MKM Stadium since the opening day of the season. Defender Nobel Mendy returns from injury to bolster the manager's options.

Ipswich start the weekend level on points with Hull, albeit with an inferior goal difference, and the Tractor Boys will also see this as a big chance to claim three points.

Gary O'Neil's side have been inconsistent and are yet to put together even a two-game unbeaten run since returning to the top flight, but they have also amassed a steady stream of points, adding one more to the total in last Friday's 1-1 home draw with Nottingham Forest.

The Ipswich boss could hand a start to Jack Clarke after the winger's lively cameo off the bench against Forest, with Jaden Philogene potentially making way.

O'Neil has a full squad to pick from after Sasa Lukic recovered from a groin injury to join the squad for the trip to East Yorkshire.

We're backing the Tigers to continue their unbeaten start at home, but will they be satisfied with that?

Confirmed lineups: Eintracht Frankfurt vs. Hamburger SV

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

After Eintracht Frankfurt knocked Hamburger SV out of the DFB-Pokal with a 3-1 away win on Wednesday, the two teams meet again in the Bundesliga on Sunday, this time at Deutsche Bank Park.

With kick-off just around an hour away, both teams have confirmed their starting lineups.

See the confirmed starting XIs for Eintracht Frankfurt vs. Hamburger SV below.

Eintracht Frankfurt XI: Atubolu - Baum, Koch, Brassier, Kosugi - Onyedika, Aseko - Uzun, Doan, Chaibi - Ebnoutalib.

Hamburger SV XI: Heuer Fernandes - Nandja, Torunarigha, Omari - Lemke, Sambi Lokonga, Muheim, Wolfe - Adeline, Grønbæk - Moffi.

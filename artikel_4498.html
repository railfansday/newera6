The 2027 Nissan Rogue Hybrid launches the crossover’s fourth generation and serves as the North American debut of the brand’s e-Power hybrid powertrain.

The inclusion of physical HVAC controls is a decision we’ll always applaud.

There are also physical buttons for the brake regeneration, as well as gear selection.

There’s a shocking amount of faux leather in the Rogue Hybrid’s interior, with the material coating the doors, dashboard, center console, and even the central tunnel.

Celtic handed fresh agony as star who got away rewrites Eredivisie record books

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Sizzling Sondre Orjaester tore up the Eredivisie record books on Sunday and handed Celtic an unwanted reminder of the winger who slipped through their grasp.

The Norwegian wide man was the star of the show as FC Twente demolished Excelsior 9-0, chalking up an astonishing FIVE assists in the rout. The remarkable haul is the highest assist tally recorded by a player in a single Eredivisie match this century and underlines the stunning form that has made Orjaester one of the hottest properties in Dutch football.

It's another painful "what if?" moment for Celtic as the Premiership champions continue to search for answers in a difficult campaign.

The Parkhead club kept close tabs on the former Sarpsborg flyer and pushed to land him during the January 2025 transfer window. But despite significant interest, a deal could not be agreed and Orjaester remained in Norway before eventually making the move to Twente.

Celtic instead turned to familiar ground, bringing Jota back to Glasgow in a £10 million deal. However, the Portuguese winger suffered a serious setback when he picked up a long-term injury against Dundee United in April 2025.

That left the Hoops back in the market for reinforcements and, by the close of the summer transfer window, Michel-Ange Balikwisha and Sebastien Tounekti had arrived in a double deal worth around £10m.

Orjaester, meanwhile, was flourishing in the Netherlands.

The 22-year-old previously opened up on why he decided against a switch to the Scottish champions, insisting the style of football on offer elsewhere was a better fit for his development.

Speaking to De Twaalfde Man, he said: "I don't think the type of football played there suits me best.

"The Eredivisie has proven to be a very good platform for young players like me, and there are a lot of technically skilled players there.

"I didn't have much time to think about whether I wanted to go to FC Twente.

"Of course, I knew they were narrowly eliminated in the Europa League last season against Bodø/Glimt. Everyone I spoke to about the club talked about the fans and how Twente played."

While Celtic continue to wrestle with their current issues, Orjaester is producing the sort of numbers that justify the hype which surrounded him. And after rewriting the Eredivisie record books with a five-assist masterclass, the Norwegian has shown exactly why so many believed he could have been a star in green and white.

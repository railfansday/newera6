Burgundy Ballet Flats Are Trending—5 Ways to Style Them for Fall

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Burgundy has been flexing its might on the fashion scene for quite a few seasons now. We’ve seen the shade crop up on leather jackets and dominate the handbag conversation. Lately, it’s making its way south to ballet flats. Seen at Phoebe Philo, The Row, and Alaïa, burgundy footwear hits a useful sweet spot: It’s not quite a neutral, but it’s nowhere near as outfit dominating as a punchier shade like red or cobalt.

Caroline Trentini (Photo by Claudio Lavenia/Getty Images)

Clara Kong Vila (Photo by Edward Berthelot/Getty Images)

The color feels especially well suited to the ballet flat, a shoe that has become a bona fide wardrobe staple. After seasons of black mesh, metallics, and classic leather styles, burgundy offers a way to make the familiar silhouette feel fresh. It’s also an easy way to introduce a little color into your fall wardrobe. Burgundy flats look just as good with denim, black, brown, and gray as they do with some of the season’s less expected shades, like pale pink or chartreuse.

Below, shop some of our favorites and take a look at the five ways we’re wearing them now.

The Row’s Stella slipper in burgundy lends a fashion-forward finish to classic blue jeans and an everyday wool jacket.

Burgundy pairs surprisingly well with pale pink, lending the sweeter shade a more sophisticated edge.

A graphic black-and-white look benefits from the subtle addition of a burgundy ballet flat, which keeps the combination from feeling too severe.

Burgundy fits easily into an off-duty wardrobe too. Pair it with army green cargos and a gray knit for an easy yet polished combination.

Proenza Schouler’s chartreuse dress may be the star of the show here, but burgundy is an ideal complement, keeping the look dynamic and colorful.

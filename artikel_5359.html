Prolonged moderation in the App Store, lasting more than a month and a half, has forced the onXRP team to adjust its original plans. Faced with bureaucratic delays on Apple’s side, Dutch entrepreneur Kaj Leroy (@BradPunkhouse) and his colleague Henk decided to launch their new product in a browser format.

Apparently, the release of a progressive web app (PWA) next week is intended to bypass the technical restrictions of mobile platforms and provide prompt access to a unique trading method for users of all devices, including Android and PC.

Based on Leroy’s public statements, the new product was not conceived as a direct competitor to existing decentralized exchanges or AMM pools on the XRP Ledger (XRPL). On the contrary, the platform is positioned as a complementary tool that supplements solutions already operating in the ecosystem.

The exact algorithms of the system are still being kept secret, but the key emphasis has shifted to integration with social platforms. The developer claims that the product will allow users to earn income from their usual activity on the social network X (Twitter), without having to make classic trades themselves.

@Iknowhim589 regardless, it really is unique there is nothing like it on the xrpl i think u will love it because u can earn with what u already do on x, without trading

It can be assumed that in this way the team expects to involve a broader internet audience in on-chain activity, monetizing their social attention and converting it into transaction volume for the entire blockchain.

In parallel, Leroy is actively developing the trading card game (TCG) sector, promoting the thesis that a global bull market has begun in this niche. His Fuzzycards project has already released about 100,000 digital booster packs on XRPL, which implement a mechanic of burning NFTs to receive real physical cards.

According to the entrepreneur, such a hybrid format today attracts a mass audience much more effectively than standard digital collections. To date, Leroy has not officially announced a direct connection between Fuzzycards and the upcoming web app.

Nevertheless, based on the general context of the ecosystem’s development, one can assume potential synergy in the future: a social earning tool on X technically looks like an effective add-on for attracting traffic and scaling gaming communities.

How justified these assumptions are will become clear after the release scheduled for next week.

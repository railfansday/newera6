3 Ways to Extinguish Emotional Reactivity

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Smoldering flames may reignite. Rather than burying your emotions, try these strategies to minimize and outsmart them.

It starts with a spark — a critical comment, a goal unrealized — and our emotions quickly catch fire. This blaze can be public and palpable, or private and hidden. Spiraling thoughts, avoidance, self-criticism, and other reactive patterns may create a temporary sense of control, but they backfire over time, trapping us in reactivity loops that increase rather than reduce distress. They can also paralyze us.

When distressing emotions threaten to consume you, try the strategies below to neutralize or dampen them.

Observation, or mindful attention, is a foundational skill for emotional regulation because it creates a pause between stimulus and response.

To build this skill, alternate between focusing on your breath and on how your emotions live in your body – rapid heartbeat, knots in your stomach, tight fists, etc. Then expand your focus: What sights and sounds near and far do you notice? Now bring your attention back to your breath.

Fight the impulse to stuff down your emotions, as unpleasant or distressing as they may feel. For helpful cues, try saying “allow” aloud or picturing a green light through which your emotions can pass.

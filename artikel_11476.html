Randy Orton Verbally Incinerates Cody Rhodes as SmackDown Stars Make Special Appearance on 11/2 Raw

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Randy Orton crashed Raw to give Cody Rhodes a scathing history lesson.

When it was announced that WWE was taping SmackDown from the same building that Raw was airing live from on November 2, it felt like a night where anything could happen. WWE had access to all of its superstar roster, and they certainly made the most of that, as Raw served as a vehicle for anyone and everyone from SmackDown to show up and juice the stakes for this Saturday at Crown Jewel. Two such stars were Cody Rhodes and Randy Orton.

Credit: USA TODAY Network via Reuters Connect

John Cena made his triumphant return to WWE Raw to provide additional details for the upcoming John Cena Classic. In fact, the retired star had three announcements to make as he gave fans a date, location, and first invitee to the exclusive tournament.

The John Cena Classic is headed to Disney World on December 14, and so far, we know one man who will be there. Cena brought out Rhodes, saying there was only one man who deserved to be the first-ever invitee to the classic. Cena praised Rhodes and acknowledged he was wrong when he thought that Rhodes couldn't handle the mantle of being one of the faces of the company. Cena told Rhodes he loved him, and the two shook hands before they were unceremoniously interrupted.

Randy Orton's music hit, and the Viper came out of the back to confront his Crown Jewel opponent. He and Cena exchanged a bit of a moment before Orton made his way to the ring. He said he was there to get Rhodes' undivided attention. Orton was there because he wanted to talk about what the Last Man Standing match meant to both of them. Orton said to Rhodes this was just a stipulation, albeit one the man had no experience in. But to Orton, this match means more because after all these years, he is WWE's last man standing.

Orton said 25 years ago he was breaking into the business and putting it all on the line against John Cena, Brock Lesnar, and Dave Batista. But those men are all gone now, whereas he is very much still here. Orton was impassioned as he said he has no aspirations to be a movie star or have his own celebrity alcohol brand. Plain and simply put, he's a professional wrestler, a third-generation one at that. This is in his blood, in his veins, what he was born to do.

Randy Orton reminded Cody Rhodes that a few years ago he broke his back and found the best surgeon money could buy to fix it. If Rhodes breaks his back on Saturday, he'll have WWE pay that surgeon again because there is nothing he wants more than to be in the ring. Orton told Rhodes the two of them have been going at it now for about six months, but in that time, there is something that never got brought up until today.

In 2007, Randy Orton wrestled the late great Dusty Rhodes in a Texas Bullrope match, which, as it turned out, was Rhodes' final match of his career. Orton reminded Rhodes he rang his dad's bell that night and then kicked him in the head. He retired him and truly was the last man standing. Orton told Rhodes that he's been called many things: the Viper, Apex Predator, Legend Killer, Rhodes Killer…but on Saturday he'll be called the last man standing. And if Rhodes' American dream was to be just like his daddy, Orton said on Saturday, he was going to make sure that dream came true.

Randy Orton left the ring before Cody Rhodes could say a word, but the camera angles showed that the Viper had gotten to him.

Mandatory Credit: Joe Camporeale-USA TODAY Sports for Reuters Connect

All of this seems to be setting up to one moment: Cody Rhodes punting Randy Orton in the head.

The entire story of their feud has been focused on Orton thinking Rhodes doesn't have what it takes. He has tormented his former mentee, believing the man would never go there. And up until a few weeks ago, Orton was right. Rhodes has had several opportunities to punt Orton, but every time, he's hesitated. And while Rhodes still has yet to punt Orton, he showed that maybe that hesitancy is gone. There is only so much a man can take, and it seems Rhodes has hit his breaking point. Cody Rhodes shocked Randy Orton a few weeks ago when he punted Baron Corbin in the head after the man stuck his nose in the wrong business on the wrong day.

Cody Rhodes punted Baron Corbin as Randy Orton looked on, showing perhaps for the first time in their feud, a little concern of his own. If Rhodes is willing to go to that dark place that Orton never thought he would, that changes the trajectory of things for the Apex Predator. Rhodes has even said since then that "the last man standing will be the only one punting. That's me."

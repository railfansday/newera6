Popeye’s Is Bringing Back This Side Dish for the First Time Since the ’90s

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Popeyes is bringing back a nostalgic side dish on Nov. 2, and fans will have plenty to celebrate.

There are two kinds of people in this world: those who go to Popeyes for the chicken and those who will make a special visit just to eat the Cajun fries. Personally, I’m in the second camp. Granted, I have nothing against Popeyes chicken! But the Cajun fries are so deliciously seasoned and perfectly crispy that sometimes I will drive-thru and get them as a snack.

And if you’re also a fan of Popeyes’ side dishes, I have some particularly good news. The Louisiana chicken chain is reaching into its menu archives to bring back a nostalgic favorite that hasn’t been widely available since the ’90s. Let’s just say your next Popeyes order might look a little different!

Popeyes has a long history of serving up more than just fried chicken. When the chain first opened in 1976, its menu featured everything from fried clams to chicken liver. Among those early offerings was a beloved side that eventually disappeared from menus in most markets. But it’s about to make its triumphant return because Popeyes is bringing back Cajun Onion Rings!

The hand-sliced, hand-battered onion rings were slathered with Cajun seasoning. With a distinctive crispy bite, they became a fan favorite almost immediately. In fact, I have family members who constantly compare other fast-food onion ring offerings to the Popeyes version they remember from childhood. In their memories, the Popeyes onion rings were always better!

But the onion rings slowly disappeared from menus. Some locations served them until the 1990s, but by then it was mostly a thing of the past. Now, nearly three decades later, the side dish is finally making its way back.

Onion rings are officially back on the Popeyes lineup now. Availability may vary by location, but this is when you should expect to see them on menus at your local Popeyes.

Priced at $3.99, the Cajun Onion Rings are designed for sharing, although I certainly wouldn’t blame anyone for keeping the whole order to themselves. After all, crispy onion rings are pretty hard to resist. I might even order the onion rings and fries and mix ’em together.

Will you be ordering the new Cajun Onion Rings? Let us know in the comments below!

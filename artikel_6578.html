Pop-Tarts Just Put A Citrusy Spin On Breakfast &amp; Fans Are Going All In: 'I Need 10 Boxes Now'

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Your morning routine might never taste quite the same.

Growing up, few breakfast combinations were more satisfying than a frosted Pop-Tart and a glass of orange juice. I wasn't particularly picky about flavor, as long as there was frosting involved. And apparently, someone at Pop-Tarts has decided that in 2026, we should be able to enjoy the entire experience without dirtying a glass.

The brand has already been busy this year, introducing Super Stuffed Pop-Tarts for anyone who felt the originals were skimping on filling. Now it's joining forces with Liquid Death to bring another unusual flavor to the breakfast aisle.

Kim Holowiak, Pop-Tarts' director of growth and marketing, said the partnership gives the brand a chance to experiment with flavors outside its usual lineup.

And if you're wondering how these two brands ended up sharing breakfast duties, this isn't actually their first go-round.

"First we made Liquid Death iced tea taste like Pop-Tarts Frosted Strawberry. Now Pop-Tarts has returned the favor with a toaster pastry that tastes like our orange soda-flavored sparkling water. They're only around for a limited time, so grab a box while you can," said Greg Fass, Liquid Death's vice president of marketing.

And judging by the response online, people aren't exactly struggling to get behind the idea of orange-flavored Pop-Tarts.

"Oh this flavor sounds delicious!" one person wrote, while a few other people clearly aren't planning to exercise much restraint. "Incredible, I will need 10 boxes now please," a different fan commented, with another a little more measured, offering a simple "Super into this."

If you're tempted to give them a shot, 12-count boxes will have a suggested price of $4.69, while 16-count boxes will go for $4.97. Pop-Tarts hasn't announced an end date for the limited release, so don’t drag your feet, as there's no telling how long you'll have to track down a juiced-up box.

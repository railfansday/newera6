New MacBook Pro, iPad mini, and more now available for pre-order on Amazon

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Apple this week unveiled a range of new products, including a new iPad mini, M6 updates for the iMac and MacBook Pro, and the first-ever MacBook Pro with a touchscreen.

Those products are now available to pre-order on Amazon:

The new iPad mini is Apple’s “biggest update ever” for its smallest iPad. The refresh brings a new OLED display, the A20 Pro chip, a nano-texture display option, and a revamped speaker system. It’s also Apple’s first-ever iPad that is IP67 rated for water and dust resistance.

The new M6 MacBook Pro is largely the same as its predecessor, but with the latest-generation silicon inside. You can expect better performance along with faster Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 7 connectivity. It’s available in silver and space black, just like before.

The M6 iMac is the same story. It brings faster performance along with Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. It’s available in the same colors as before: green, yellow, orange, pink, purple, blue, and silver.

Finally, the star of the show this week is the new high-end MacBook with an OLED touchscreen. It’s a trio of firsts for Apple: the first touchscreen Mac, the first OLED Mac, and the first Mac with a Dynamic Island. It’s powered by the same M5 Pro and M5 Max chips as before.

It also features a glorious glowing Apple logo on the back, the first since nearly a decade ago.

The new Macs and iPad mini will all start arriving to customers and Apple Stores on November 6. Are you planning to buy any of Apple’s new gear? Let us know down in the comments.

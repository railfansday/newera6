101 Rapid-Fire Questions With The Exodus Team

Exodus is set in a far-flung future, with humankind stretching out to the stars and exploring a whole new realm of possibilities. This, of course, means we have quite a few questions about what's happening in the universe surrounding Archetype Entertainment's RPG.

As part of our cover story coverage, we sat down with three members of Exodus' leadership team to ask them some rapid-fire questions. Will there be romance? Will there be cool weapons? Will there be cheesy glove-based puns? Does Sega's incredibly influential role-playing saga Phantasy Star get a shout-out? All this and more in the latest Game Informer Rapid-Fire interview with the leadership of Exodus.

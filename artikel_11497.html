Apple's pricey and intricate folding phone could lead to a costly repair if something happens to it.

The first iPhone Duos are now in customers’ hands, and we’re seeing in real time what happens when years of confidential Apple design meets the real world for the first time. Although reports still seem limited, several just-unboxed units are suffering from dramatic cracks on the inner screen. CNET is doing our own testing of the phone’s hinge by continuously opening and closing an iPhone Duo — which is now up to more than 200,000 times.

Apple has already promised to replace cracked units, a repair that also falls under the phone’s one-year warranty, but what if a crack or some other defect appears outside that window of time? Should owners and potential buyers add AppleCare Plus for their new Duo? (Or at the very least, an iPhone Duo case?) Based on Apple’s repair prices, it might not be a bad investment — unless you’re JerryRigEverything, whose warranty is most definitely voided.

After all, the iPhone Duo starts at expensive and goes up to exorbitant for its higher-capacity configurations. If something happens and you need a repair, the costs could be substantial without AppleCare coverage.

The base 256GB storage option is priced at $1,999, but if you want to max out Apple’s new foldable phone with 2TB of space, it’ll cost $3,199. That’s pricey by iPhone standards, compared to the iPhone 18 Pro that begins at $1,199, though in line with the retail prices of Android folding phones like the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 8 Ultra and Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold.

Adding AppleCare coverage on top of the iPhone Duo’s price is an ongoing cost in addition to the price of the phone, but in terms of potential repairs, it does make sense. Because if you don’t fork out for a plan, and the inner display of your iPhone Duo gets crunched, expect to pay $999 to get it replaced, according to Apple’s support documentation. These are prices for repairs made directly by Apple, not third-party services.

AppleCare Plus with Theft and Loss is priced at $20 per month or $200 per year until canceled and includes “unlimited repairs for accidents like drops and spills” and theft and loss coverage for up to two claims every 12 months. It also features Express Replacement Service, which gets you a new iPhone Duo instead of waiting for a repair.

Alternatively, the AppleCare One service costs $20 a month until canceled and applies AppleCare Plus to up to three devices. It also adds a third theft-and-loss coverage event in the 12-month span.

Best starting eleven this season: Fan player ratings for Milan 3-1 Inter

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

It ended up being a fun Saturday evening at San Siro thanks to the 3-1 win against Inter, as was also reflected in the fan ratings for the AC Milan players.

Eight derbies unbeaten. That is Milan’s current run in the fixture and given just how good last night’s win was, they are certainly on cloud nine. Several players stepped up to the occasion and helped get them over the line, bouncing back from the defeat to Bologna.

We handed out very high ratings, with the highest going to Diego Moreira at 9.5/10, and the same can be said for the newspapers this morning. After all, a derby is a derby and performing in such a big game is always going to be praised.

Taking a look at the ratings that you, our readers, provided via our player ratings tool, the consensus was also that all players did very well. Of course, some did much better than others, like the aforementioned Moreira who got a 9.3/10 in the fan ratings.

Mike Maignan was the closest contender at 8.1, while the likes of Adrien Rabiot (8.0), Luka Modric (7.9) and Christian Pulisic (7.9) also came close.

The ‘worst’ of the starters was Goncalo Ramos at 6.5, while Sankhoun Diawara wasn’t too far ahead at 6.8. In total, 325 fans submitted their player ratings via our tool, so the ratings were certainly based on the views of a lot of Milan fans.

Beyoncé Shares Rare Photos With Blue Ivy Carter Backstage at Jay-Z’s L.A. Concert

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

On October 24, Blue Ivy Carter joined her father, Jay-Z, onstage for the first night of his Jay-Z 30 L.A. concert, playing the piano as he performed. Her mother, Beyoncé, watched in the crowd—and posted her own personal photos from the evening on her site this week. In one shot, Beyoncé and Blue appear together with Beyoncé’s mom, Tina Knowles, backstage.

Beyoncé also posted plenty of outfit shots, putting the spotlight on her Roberto Cavalli look and Messika jewelry. The ensemble featured a white fitted froissé silk satin “Orchidea” print top and an emerald tulle pareo skirt. The bottom was fully embroidered with matching sequins and jet-bead fringes, per the fashion house. See all the photos on Beyoncé’s site.

Jay-Z spoke earlier this year about Blue’s musical talent, revealing she is a very gifted keyboardist. “Blue is a crazy pianist, but she won’t let us get her a teacher,” he told GQ in March. “She doesn’t want it to be a job. But she has perfect pitch. If she hears a song, she’ll be like, ‘Play it again,’ and then she’ll teach herself. That’s just talent; she doesn’t work at that. She worked at this [performing as a dancer during Beyoncé’s Cowboy Carter Tour], and it makes me proud that she fought for something that she really wanted to do. I don’t think we’re going to be able to get her off that stage now.”

Two weeks ago, Blue joined Beyoncé onstage for the opening of the Knowles-Rowland House in Houston. She cut the ribbon with her mom and younger sister Rumi.

Beyoncé’s children are rarely photographed out by design. As Beyoncé told GQ in September 2024, “One thing I’ve worked extremely hard on is making sure my kids can have as much normalcy and privacy as possible, ensuring my personal life isn’t turned into a brand. It’s very easy for celebrities to turn our lives into performance art. I have made an extreme effort to stay true to my boundaries and protect myself and my family. No amount of money is worth my peace.”

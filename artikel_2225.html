Chris Sutton skewers 'pathetic' Celtic star as big money signing called out

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Unimpressed Chris Sutton branded Haissem Hassan's defending "pathetic" after the Premiership champions fell apart in a 2-0 defeat to Falkirk.

The Parkhead icon has long been a supporter of the Egypt international's attacking threat, but he could not hide his frustration at the winger's lack of effort tracking back.

Hassan, bought for £6m in a deal which includes £3m in add-ons, was hooked in the 84th minute after an afternoon full of near misses. Goals from Ethan Ross and Scott Tanser in the first half secured a famous victory for the Bairns over the Premiership champions.

The latest setback means Martin O'Neill's struggling side have managed just three wins from their last 10 games.

And it was Hassan who found himself firmly in Sutton's firing line, with the former Celtic striker making his feelings clear while on commentary duty for Sky Sports. O'Neill admitted on Friday that Hassan's defensive game needed added work.

Sutton said: "Absolutely pathetic from Hassan on a couple of occasions. He doesn't want to defend.

"You thought Celtic would come out in the second half and play with purpose, but they're being bullied by Falkirk."

In pictures, it’s pretty easy to think of the i3 as a carbon-copy 3-series, just with an electric powertrain. But there’s way more to the i3 than that.

To avoid the compromises BMW made when fitting the i4 model’s electric powertrain in the gas-powered 430i’s body, the i3 shares very little with its gas-fed sibling in terms of body.

The i3 has a 1.5-inch-longer wheelbase, and it’s slight taller and wider, as well.

That gave BMW the space it needed to fit two electric motors and a 112-kWh battery, which is way larger than the i4’s 84-kWh pack.

In the i3 50 xDrive, those two electric motors combine to produce 463 horsepower and 476 pound-feet of torque.

We reckon this little rocket will make it to 60 in about 4.2 seconds.

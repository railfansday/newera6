When will Penn State football play Minnesota, which TV channel?

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Penn State football will play in prime time, once more, in Beaver Stadium in 2026.

The Nittany Lions' Nov. 14 game against Minnesota will start at either 7 p.m. or 7:30 p.m. and will be televised by FOX Sports 1 or NBC, Big Ten officials announced on Monday. The exact timing and TV channel will be determined this weekend.

Penn State (4-4, 1-4 Big Ten) and coach Matt Campbell will be trying to finish strong after finally breaking through last weekend against Purdue. It's now preparing for its cross-country showdown with Washington Saturday afternoon.

The Purdue victory was fueled by the Lions' first, true starring running back performance, their most efficient quarterback play in weeks and another surge from their growing defense.

They now await word on whether they'll be further bolstered by the return of their top two playmakers on offense, injured receivers Koby Howard and Chase Sowell.

The Golden Gophers (5-3, 3-2), meanwhile, have played well most of the season under PJ Fleck and own victories over Washington, Michigan and Iowa. But they did just suffer a heartbreaking, overtime loss to Top 10 Indiana in Bloomington and now must play undefeated UCLA.

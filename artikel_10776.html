Fortnite adds rare reward that makes entire Item Shop free for 14 days, but there’s limits

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Fortnite players can now win a prize from chests that makes everything in the Item Shop free for 14 days.

The prize, called the Pirate Shop, arrived with the Treasure Hunters update on November 1, alongside the game’s One Piece crossover.

Opening chests in Battle Royale can roll a Treasure Hunt prize. Dataminer HYPEX found that the Pirate Shop is in the prize pool every week.

According to HYPEX, winners get two activations, one for 2026 and one for 2027. Once activated, everything sold for V-Bucks in the Item Shop can be claimed for free for 14 days.

FIRST LOOK AT THE PIRATE SHOP PASS@snowslept activated his token and he will be able to claim the entire next 14 item shops for FREE, and then activate another token in 2026 pic.twitter.com/mGNb2BS1rS

The timer only starts when you press activate. The 2026 activation can be used until December 15, 2026, and the 2027 one runs from January 1 to December 15, 2027.

There are some limits. Real-money offers, gifting, and Battle Pass levels and rewards aren’t included, and the reward can’t be transferred to another account.

Players can also only claim one free Sidekick they don’t already own.

THIS IS HOW IT LOOKS ONCE YOU LEAVE THE MATCH (IDK HOW TO USE IT) pic.twitter.com/JcQvlr8yyG

Epic Games hasn’t shared the odds of landing the Pirate Shop, and HYPEX said it’s not yet clear if there’s a limit on how often it drops.

At least one player has already landed the prize. X user snowslept said it came from a chest on their third match, in a normal Solos lobby, after opening around 150 chests.

“Some people asked me to show on replays how the chest looked and it looked like any other chest, so I guess it’s really random,” they wrote.

HERE IT IS YALL!! ITS ALLLLL OF THE SHOP FR IM SO HAPPY 🥹😭 pic.twitter.com/qo3hzhEL0D

In a follow-up, they confirmed that items claimed through the Pirate Shop are permanent and that Jam Tracks are included.

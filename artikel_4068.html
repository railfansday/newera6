Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds - Year Two 2.0.0 Patch Notes Preview - New Additions, Changes & Improvements

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Paid and free character updates are also on the way

It's another huge week in gaming, and Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds is joining in on the fun with its launch of "Year Two".

This includes a major title update (Version 2.0.0), and ahead of the 3rd November 2026 rollout, Sonic Global Community Director Aaron Webber has outlined what you can expect. It will include "new additions, changes, and improvements".

On this page: Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds - Year Two

Of course, there's also the "Year Two" free and paid characters and machines. The free characters announced will include Bayonetta (arriving 3rd November), and she'll be followed by McLaren Racing (Sonic) later this month, and Aigis in early 2027.

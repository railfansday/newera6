Cranberry Hugo Spritz

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A classic Hugo spritz is decidedly a summer cocktail, but if you’re looking for an excuse to pop a bottle of Prosecco in colder weather, look no further than this cranberry Hugo spritz. St-Germain is already my favorite liqueur for its ability to add floral sweetness to a cocktail without being too cloying or overwhelming, but this upgrade to the original Italian spritz gives the elderflower notes additional tartness to play with, creating the perfectly balanced drink for all of your fall and winter parties.

Batch it:Including instructions to make a big batch felt like a no-brainer to make this the easily shareable Friendsgiving centerpiece cocktail that it deserves to be. Just combine the non-bubbly ingredients (mint, lime juice, St-Germain, and cranberry juice) in a pitcher and let your friends finish their glasses with a Prosecco and club soda topper. Keeping the fizzy stuff separate will ensure everyone’s drink feels fresh and properly carbonated. If you mix the bubbly ingredients too soon, they’ll lose their carbonation over time, resulting in a flat, underwhelming sip for those who want a second serving. Leave out a plate of fresh mint and lime slices to let your guests garnish their glasses.

Pro tip:I used unsweetened cranberry juice to develop this, but if you prefer your drinks to be a little on the sweet side, opt for a cranberry juice cocktail. A cranberry juice cocktail is usually blended with other juices and sweetener to tame some of the tartness.

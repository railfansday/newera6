OpenAI is making a major change to ChatGPT interface

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

ChatGPT is preparing to give users one fewer tab to think about.

OpenAI plans to merge ChatGPT's Chat and Work tabs into a single experience, bringing everyday conversations and more complex tasks into the same place. The company outlined the change in an email to users, according to TechCrunch, with a limited test planned before a wider rollout.

Currently, OpenAI describes Chat as the place for everyday questions and conversational help, while Work handles longer tasks, including research, analyzing information, and creating documents, presentations, and websites. The desktop app also includes a separate Codex experience for coding, which will remain separate after the merger.

"This will enable you to move from exploring an idea to having ChatGPT carry it through, all in one place," OpenAI reportedly said in the email. Users will be able to bring in files and plugins while reviewing and guiding its work.

Cyberpunk 2 might include multiplayer after CD Projekt Red scrapped the feature in Cyberpunk 2077

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Cyberpunk 2 might try to revive Cyberpunk 2077's online ambitions, as a new CD Projekt Red job listing is looking for someone familiar with "multiplayer network architecture."

As spotted by Cheap Happens, the role-playing powerhouse is currently looking to hire a lead quest programmer for its upcoming open-world sequel. Buried away at the bottom of the job listing, under the "Nice To Haves" section, the studio asks for an "understanding of multiplayer network architecture and client-server synchronization for game state/ quests."

The multiplayer mention is by no means confirmation that Cyberpunk 2 - or whatever it ends up being called - will have co-op and other online modes, but it's definitely something CD Projekt Red is looking into. And it certainly makes sense, too.

Once upon a time, Cyberpunk 2077 was intended to receive a multiplayer portion sometime after its initial launch. Of course, at the time, the shaky state of the game's development meant that was no longer feasible and CD Projekt Red instead said multiplayer would come as its own "triple-A release." Eventually, the famed studio realised it simply had to focus on ensuring the base game was stable enough on its own, and shelved those online ambitions.

A few years back, Cyberpunk 2077's senior quest designer, Philipp Weber, told Eurogamer the company was "focusing on bringing online into all of our franchises one day" by "building an online technology that can be seamlessly integrated into development of our future games."

More recently, in 2024, CD Projekt CEO Adam Badowski said the team were "considering" multiplayer elements for the Cyberpunk sequel back when the game was still in its early concept phases.

We don't know much else about the neon-drenched follow-up, but CDPR has been keeping series fans full and fed with continual updates to Cyberpunk 2077. Earlier this week, the game got an update celebrating the debut of Cyberpunk Edgerunners Season 2 on Netflix - everything from dual-wielding to cross-platform mods were added to the six-year-old game.

First new Nuno replacement name emerges as disgruntled Hammers point the finger | West Ham News

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

I was going to start this article with ‘it hasn’t taken long‘ but then I had to back track and begin again. In truth, West Ham have been waiting for Nuno Espirito Santo’s method to catch fire at West Ham ever since his arrival in September 2025. Thirteen months is, by recent standards, an over ‘average’ length of stay for an Irons manager.

Neither of his two predecessors, Julen Lopetegui – less than eight months – and Graham Potter just over eight months, made it to a year in charge before getting the boot.

A manager cannot survive when the fans turn against him and yet Nuno would seem to be on a rock solid foundation thanks to his being Kretinsky’s “continuity candidate’, appointed to see the club through the murky waters post-relegation and while the boardroom was distracted over the Sullivan allegations and then the Staveley takeover.

Plus of course the estimated ten million pounds that’d be required to send the head coach, along with his whole entourage packing.

Ghosts of West Ham future and West Ham past?

That hasn’t stopped names being thrown into the ether – Thomas Frank being the first to appear thanks to talkSPORT yesterday with Jamie O’Hara admitting that the former Spurs, Brentford and Brondby manager would be ‘not a bad shout‘ to replace Nuno.

This, remember, on the back of a story already running that the Hammers leadership have been in discussions with former Wolves manager Rob Edwards.

Will the board act now? Sadly, certainly not.

Six points at this stage of the season is, on paper, just a week’s worth of favourable results and nowhere near enough for the Czech Sphinx to divert his attention from other projects onto the thorny issue of first admitting he was wrong to re-appoint Nuno and then finding a successor.

What West Ham fans will know is that there’s already been a year of fumbling, stuttering management leading up to the Irons’ predicament and another three or six months won’t see any change. Except the chance of automatic promotion will have sailed off down the Swanee in the meantime.

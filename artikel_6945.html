When Is the ‘Love Is Blind’ Season 11 Reunion? Here’s How You Can Watch a Full Day Early With a Simple RSVP

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

We’re just a few days away from the final episodes of Love Is Blind Season 11, which means we’ll find out which of our Boston-based couples will say “I do” and which will awkwardly say “I don’t” in front of...literally everyone they know.

The final episodes of Love Is Blind drop Wednesday, November 4, but considering this show was filmed in 2025 (according to fan sleuthing), we’re in desperate need of an update on where the cast stands circa right now. After all, a lot can happen in a year: the couples who said “I do” could have gotten divorced already, or even had kids. Which brings us to a very important question...

The Love Is Blind reunion airs Sunday, November 8 at 9 p.m. ET on Netflix. In other words, you don’t have to wait an entire week after the final episodes drop to find out where everyone stands.

Netflix’s fan site, Tudum, is hosting a special early screening of the reunion one day early on November 7 at 4 p.m. ET. You have to RSVP to attend the event, which will include a “pregame” where fans can chat with pod members, the reunion itself, as well as an “after party” with “never-before-seen pod dates, footage from the romantic getaways, and plenty of Bostonian accents.”

Nope! But it was filmed relatively recently!

Tudum’s event language does include the word “live” with “Expect deleted scenes, surprise guests, and a live chat where you can react to every twist, turn, and receipt in real time.” But the reunion itself was filmed ahead of time.

Per The Tab, “the reunion was filmed before the last batch of episodes premiered,” so the info will be new. Just not right-this-second new.

For what it’s worth, Love Is Blind tried to air its reunion live back in 2023, but there were technical difficulties that led to them scrapping the entire thing. But as long as the reunion was filmed recently (which it seemingly was!) we’ll take it.

See you early birds on November 7 at 4 p.m. ET!

Get the Wells Fargo Reflect® Card if you need more than a year to pay off a large purchase or a balance moved from another card. Its interest rate on both is 0% for 21 months, and each transfer costs a 5% fee. Skip it if you pay in full every month, because it has no rewards program.

The Reflect is a no-annual-fee credit card for carrying debt at 0% while you pay it down. It suits someone with a fixed amount to repay and a plan to clear the balance before the 0% rate ends.

Anyone who can repay within a year, or who pays in full every month, does better with the Wells Fargo Active Cash, which adds 2% cash rewards and still gives 0% for 12 months on purchases and transfers. Borrowers who need more than a year should get the Reflect and request any transfer with the application, since its 0% period starts the day the account opens.

Until Dawn 2 to Showcase New PS5 Gameplay and More in Alamo Drafthouse Livestream

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Sony is getting the ball rolling on Until Dawn 2, its narrative horror adventure game making its debut in a few short months.

The PS5 exclusive title, developed by Firesprite, launches on 28th January 2027, but you can get a good look at the game before then, as the company plans to show it off in a fancy live event this December.

Until Dawn 2 will be making a special appearance at American cinema chain Alamo Drafthouse (which Sony Pictures owns, by the way) on 11th December, 2026, where attendees will be able to see "an exclusive gameplay showcase" as well as enjoy a horror movie marathon.

The event will also include "special talent appearances", presumably meaning that actors from the game will be showing up live.

If you're unable to get to an Alamo Drafthouse, the gameplay showcase will be available to watch via livestream so you can catch a glimpse of the new game in action. More details are coming soon.

While it's a sequel to the very successful original game, Until Dawn 2 focuses on a brand new cast and entirely different story.

It features a group of ghost hunters that travel to a remote island, having been offered a TV deal to document its mysterious abandoned resort.

The new premise and characters aside, this is very much in the same mould as the first Until Dawn; you follow this group of people and must make choices along the way that can have a serious impact on the story.

Heavily inspired by horror cinema, this event makes a lot of sense.

Sony has been quietly building interest in Until Dawn 2 by introducing each character one at a time. There's now a little under three months to go before this is in our hands, so I expect we'll be seeing plenty more as we get closer.

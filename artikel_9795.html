Why Every Backend Needs Multi-Tier Caching (L1 RAM + L2 Redis)

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Redis is the undisputed king of distributed caching. But if your application backend is querying Redis over the network on every single incoming request, you are leaving massive performance on the table.

Even inside the same cloud availability zone (AWS VPC or Google Cloud), a roundtrip network hop to Redis costs 1.0 to 4.5 milliseconds.

Here is why Multi-Tier Caching (L1 RAM + L2 Persistent) is the modern architecture standard—and how it slashes latency by up to 95%.

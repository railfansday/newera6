Duck Side of the Moon coming to PS5 on November 17 alongside ‘Homegrown’ update

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Starbrew Games will release a PlayStation 5 version of cozy adventure game Duck Side of the Moon on November 7 alongside the free “Homegrown” update, the developer announced.

Duck Side of the Moon first launched for Switch and PC via Steam and Epic Games Store on May 7.

“Ever since launch, we couldn’t wait to expand on the cozy universe we created,” said Starbrew Games designer Joost Rol in a press release. “We had so many ideas floating around, and ‘Homegrown’ gives players more to do and more ways to make Doug’s spaceship their own. After all, life among the stars is a lot less stressful with a garden!”

Here is an overview of the game and update, via Starbrew Games:

Duck Side of the Moon is a cozy open-world crafting and exploration adventure where curiosity leads the way and relaxation is part of the mission plan. Players drift through a whimsical galaxy as Doug the duck, gathering materials, crafting gadgets, upgrading their crashed spaceship into a place that finally feels like home again.

Across colorful little planets, the journey unfolds at a gentle pace and while you meet and help the unusual locals, including some surprisingly chatty rocks.

Even in space, a little winging it goes a long way!

The “Homegrown” content unlocks around halfway through the game. Players who have already finished Duck Side of the Moon can load their existing save and find the new content waiting for them.

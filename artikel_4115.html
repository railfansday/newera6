Meet Marvel Tokon's Newest Fighting Soul in Full Phoenix Cyclops Character Guide

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Ahead of his release next week on 2nd November, Sony’s dropped a full character guide for the upcoming Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls addition, Phoenix Cyclops.

Like similar videos prior to the game’s launch, this runs through the combatant’s full roster of moves, including his ability to transform into Dark Phoenix state by assembling Solar Shards.

In this secondary form, he unlocks some additional moves and abilities, adding a few layers to his gameplay if you’re hoping to master him.

Phoenix Cyclops is the first of four new characters for Marvel Tokon, with Jane Foster, Venom, and Jubilee all due over the course of the game’s first year.

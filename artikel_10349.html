Motorola Signature 27 specs detailed in new video, including Tandem OLED display

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Ahead of its launch next week, a new video offers a closer look at the Motorola Signature 27 as well as confirming some key specs.

A video hands-on from Danny Winget on YouTube offers a closer look at Motorola’s upcoming flagship, and only makes its arrival all the more enticing.

There are several key specs mentioned in the video, with the most interesting being the “Extreme AMOLED Duo” display which makes use of Tandem OLED technology. Tandem OLED isn’t a new idea, with the tech stacking multiple layers of diodes on top of each other to improve contrast and brightness. Tandem OLED can be found on Apple’s new “PROLED” MacBook Pro, as well as 2024’s Honor Magic 6 RSR. But Motorola’s use of it in the Signature 27 is certainly nice to see, especially given the other spec shared. 12,000 nits of brightness. That rather insane figure is apparently the peak brightness on this new flagship.

Hardware shots of the phone also show the textured backs on the two color variants as well as the curved glass around the display.

I think it’s a interesting phone with a good camera system. But it also depend on the price. Anyway nice to see Motorola compete in the flagship space again.

The battery capacity isn’t revealed, but Winget says that it is “sizable.” Similarly, the vapor chamber is described as “40% larger” but a point of comparison wasn’t given.

It’s all shaping up for a powerful flagship in every sense of the word. Motorola has confirmed that Signature 27 will launch on November 5, and I don’t know about you, but I’m only getting more excited about it.

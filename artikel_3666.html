Holiday content returns to Netflix in November, with the monthly slate weighted toward dozens of Christmas and seasonally themed films and shows as well as season nine of The Great British Baking Show: Holidays (Nov. 23). Netflix will also be hosting a Thanksgiving Eve NFL game with the Packers vs. Rams matchup on Nov. 25.

Other titles of note include The Altruists (Nov. 19), a limited drama series about the rise and fall—and crimes—of FTX founder Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison (played by Julia Garner of Ozark and Inventing Anna fame). Jessica Chastain stars in the Netflix original film Heartland (Nov. 13), a thriller about a former country music star whose niece disappears.

On the documentary side, there's Ron Howard's Avedon (Nov. 11), a film about the photographer's life and influence, plus comedy specials from Mike Tyson (Return of the Mike, Nov. 3), Janelle James (How Romantic, Nov. 10), and Mike Ward (Modest, Nov. 17).

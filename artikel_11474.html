BTSC Monday Night Football thread: Bears at Seahawks

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The NFL's Week 8 will conclude with one of the most intriguing games of the week, with the 5-2 Bears hosting the 4-3 Seahawks in primetime. Besides both teams being contenders in the NFC, the game should also feature two of the biggest blockbuster trades of the recent week: Guard Quenton Nelson is now a Seattle Seahawk, and defensive tackle Jeffrey Simmons is now a Chicago Bear.

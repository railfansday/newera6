Bo Van Pelt Locks In 2026 WITB For Fall Season

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Bo Van Pelt has finalized his equipment setup for the October 2026 tournament season, showing a blend of Callaway, Srixon, and Cleveland gear. The veteran player made these specific choices public on November 1, just as the fall swing kicked into high gear. GolfWRX broke the story with detailed in-hand photos that confirm every shaft and head in the bag. This lineup highlights a preference for specific steel shafts and modern driver technology designed for the PGA Tour's latest course setups.

The driver situation sees Van Pelt relying on the Callaway Quantum Max set at 9 degrees. He pairs this with a Fujikura Ventus Black 6 X shaft to generate stability off the tee. For shorter tee shots, the bag holds a Cobra King TEC-MD mini driver at 13 degrees, fitted with a Fujikura Ventus Blue 7 X shaft. Fairway wood duties fall to a Ping G440 Max at 21 degrees, which uses a heavier Fujikura Ventus Black 8 X shaft for added control. GolfWRX noted these specific loft and shaft combinations are critical for his trajectory on longer holes.

Van Pelt's iron and utility setup leans heavily on Srixon models. He carries Srixon ZXi5 irons in the 5-iron slot and Srixon ZXi7 models for the 6-iron through pitching wedge range. Both iron sets utilize Nippon N.S. Pro Modus3 Tour125 S shafts. The utility section features a Srixon ZXiU at 20 degrees with a Fujikura Ventus HB Blue 8 X shaft, alongside a Srixon ZX at 23 degrees fitted with a Nippon N.S. Pro Modus3 Tour125 S shaft. This mix allows him to hit specific distances while maintaining a consistent feel through the bag.

Short game equipment includes Cleveland RTZ 2 wedges in 50 and 60 degrees. The 50-degree wedge has a mid-sole grind, while the 58 and 60-degree models feature the 588-8 grind. These wedges are fitted with Nippon N.S. Pro WV125 Tour Only shafts. Van Pelt putts with an Odyssey Ai-One CH #7. He grips his clubs with Golf Pride MCC+4 Midsize and Golf Pride Victory Cord options depending on the shot. The ball of choice remains the Titleist Pro V1, a staple for Tour professionals seeking consistency.

He uses Nippon steel shafts for his irons and wedges, which suggests a preference for a specific kick point and feel. The driver and fairway woods switch to Fujikura Ventus models, known for their stability and low spin characteristics. GolfWRX reported that these specific shafts help him manage the ball flight when wind is a factor during fall events.

Van Pelt decided to keep his Callaway driver and Ping fairway wood. The mini driver from Cobra adds versatility for tight tee boxes. The setup lets him hit a high percentage of fairways while still having the distance to reach par‑5s in two shots.

This equipment list gives fans a clear look at what Van Pelt will use in upcoming tournaments. The specific lofts and shafts indicate he is ready for the conditions expected in October and November. GolfWRX confirmed these details on November 1, giving observers a full view of his gear before the next major event. The consistency in his iron and wedge selection suggests he trusts these clubs to perform under pressure.

Van Pelt's setup remains unchanged from the previous weeks, showing confidence in his current configuration. The use of the Titleist Pro V1 ball aligns with many top players on tour.

7 Celebrity Outfits You Should Steal This Month

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

With the holidays looming and next year’s awards season on the horizon, there’s no rest for the A-list weary. Between hitting up screenings, press junkets, and the general business of being a star, you’ll occasionally catch famous folks kicking their feet up in suites at SoFi Stadium or risking a courtside collision with a referee at an NBA game.

From Harry Styles getting his heart rate up on morning runs over the Williamsburg Bridge to Ethan Hawke turning up for a SAG screening, Glenn Close pulling an absolute grail piece from a film she starred in alongside Robert Redford, and Nicholas Hoult hopping between planes, the celebrity style circuit is showing no signs of slowing down.

Maybe this is the season you’ve been influenced to go all in on corduroy, resurrect those vintage graphic tees gathering dust in your tote bins, or finally dip your dainty toes into the jazz shoe craze. Whatever your sartorial itch, let these celebrities do the legwork for you.

The Hawkester has worn corduroy before, and this look calls to mind Robin Williams in Dead Poets Society, whose affinity for the fabric felt like part of the curriculum. Hawke styles the suit in a way that feels less adjunct professor and more stoner student, thanks to a ratty graphic tee and tinted shades. Rather than dress shoes, he opts for well-worn dark brown Sambas, which contrast nicely with his acorn-colored suit. Carpe diem, and all that.

The star of Netflix’s upcoming show The Altruists demonstrates how dressing in a flowy, monochromatic palette doesn’t have to feel boring. The burgundy trench coat injects just enough color to keep things interesting. Leave it open so you don’t look like Inspector Gadget, and throw on tinted sunglasses, because they’re never a bad idea, no matter the fit.

With his Together, Together tour wrapping up at the end of the month, Harry’s New York stroll fits will likely be inducted into the annals of celeb big fits. He seems to have tapped into the lore of ’90s-era skaters, layering a long-sleeved tee under a T-shirt. Add light-wash jeans, all-white sneakers, and Apple earbuds, and you’ve got a man who feels gloriously analog in a digital world.

Glenn Close shows us how to build an outfit around a statement piece, in this case, a jacket from The Natural, the 1984 film in which she co-starred. Throw on navy slacks, loafers, and a crisp white Oxford shirt, and you’re good to go. Of course, it helps when you have iconic films attached to your name, too.

Example 1,088 of why denim-on-denim is always the answer to the in-flight outfit dilemma. Mr. Hoult opts for a charcoal-hued Canadian tuxedo, offset by black boots and a dad cap. Denim can take a beating. Whether from turbulence-induced coffee spills or an overenthusiastic recline, it provides a welcome layer of warmth on those cold and bone-dry flights.

As the oldest brother in Sweden’s Skarsgård acting dynasty, Alexander proves that an age-old graphic tee and Chuck Taylors will always be a can’t-miss combination. The louche, open trench coat adds a bit of flare to the fit. We also have to note, when you’re 6’4” with a chin chiseled enough to double as an ice luge, it’s hard for anything to look bad on you.

People love to claim that black and brown don’t belong together. I guess they’ve never had a Turtle Pecan Blizzard. The black shirt and blazer flirt with Y2K corporate-core, but the khaki-colored, pleated, baggy chinos and jazz shoes give the whole kit some much-needed attitude.

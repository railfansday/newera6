Self-made millionaire says GTA 6 could actually save players $5k

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A self-made millionaire believes Grand Theft Auto 6 will actually “save” players around $5000 each, provided they play for at least 50 days.

With the calendar finally turning over to November, the long-awaited release of GTA 6 is finally within touching distance. Players have started making plans for November 19, including already booking days off work. Sick days to play the game might cost the US economy around $1.5 billion, too.

However, despite the release possibly affecting the economy, finance expert Mark Tilbury believes it could save players around $5000 each.

In his October 31 video, the self-made millionaire noted that if the game takes 80 hours to complete, it’ll cost you around $1.25 per hour, but that could drop as low as 50 cents once you start adding in free roaming.

“Let’s say you play for four hours every evening, that’s 50 nights of entertainment,” Tilbury added.

“Compare that to going out, where you could easily spend $100 in a single night, thats why buying GTA 6 actually saves you $5000.”

Four hours of play each night is pretty conservative, seeing as people have waited over a decade for the game. Though, you won’t be able to speedrun through GTA 6 anyway.

A post shared by Mark Tilbury – CEO & YouTuber (@marktilbury)

Tilbury’s video has already garnered plenty of attention too, with viewers jokingly calling it the “boy math” that they’ve “been waiting for” at last.

“Thanks, I’ll show it to my parents,” another joked. “Finally, a financial advisor I trust,” another commented. “Say no more, I will buy 4 digital copies; that’s $20,000 in my pocket,” another said.

As it stands, the game isn’t the only way people are making money from GTA 6. Some resellers have been scooping up promotional items in Miami and reselling them online for a massive markup in price.

That resale war looks like it will only continue, seeing as a collaboration between Nike and Rockstar Games has been leaked online too.

Travis Scott’s Cactus Jack brand is expected to be used on sneakers, T-shirts, hoodies, and other items as well. The sneakers alone are set to cost $130, so the demand will be pretty high.

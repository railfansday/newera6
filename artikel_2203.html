Emi Martinez explains Chelsea move as World Cup heartbreak left goalkeeper needing a fresh start

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Graduate Sports Journalist with a love for football.

Emi Martinez has revealed that his move to Chelsea helped him recover mentally from the pain of losing the World Cup final with Argentina.

The goalkeeper left Aston Villa during the summer, with Chelsea confirming a contract until 2029.

He has now explained the personal motivation behind changing clubs.

Speaking after Argentina’s win over Burkina Faso, Martinez discussed his response to the World Cup disappointment in comments reported by TyC Sports.

“I tried changing clubs to refresh myself mentally, and I succeeded.”

Martinez was answering a question about an earlier social-media post that had raised doubts over his Argentina future. He explained that he now felt reinvigorated and ready to continue representing his country.

His explanation gives supporters a clearer understanding of a transfer that formed part of Chelsea’s summer goalkeeping changes.

Those changes also included Filip Jorgensen’s loan move to Strasbourg, as Xabi Alonso reshaped his options.

Martinez’s international break has since brought another positive moment. He received an ovation during the Argentina victory in which Chelsea team-mate Valentin Barco supplied the seventh goal.

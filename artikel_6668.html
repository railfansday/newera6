Nearly 6 Million Bodum French Presses Are Being Recalled After Dozens Of Burn Injuries

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

What to know before your next cup of coffee.

Before you break out your beans and get brewing your next morning cup of coffee, listen up. A popular French press coffee maker has just been recalled for breaking and causing serious burn injuries. Here’s what we know.

Numerous Bodum 8-cup and 12-cup French press coffee makers are being recalled nationwide. Affected models include Bistro, Brazil, Caffettiera, Chambord, Crema, Java, Jesper, and Kenya French press machines, all with replaceable glass carafes. The recall affects about 5.9 million machines.

These machines have been sold for over half a century, from 1974 through October 2026. Retailers including Target, Walmart, and Amazon sold the affected coffee makers for anywhere between $15 and $65.

According to the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission, the carafe can break unexpectedly during use if the glass is damaged or cracked—including if a metal spoon is used to stir the coffee—posing a risk of severe burn injuries. To date, Bodum USA has identified 70 reports of carafes breaking during use, 48 of which involved burns.

Consumers with the affected Bodum products should stop using metal spoons or other utensils to stir their coffee within the carafe. Owners of the affected French Press models can contact the company to receive a plastic Bodum stirring spoon and a warning label reminding them not to use cracked or damaged carafes or metal spoons.

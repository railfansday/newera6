Every 2027 Color of the Year Announced So Far

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Every item on this page was chosen by an ELLE Decor editor. We may earn commission on some of the items you choose to buy.

The 2027 Color of the Year announcements are well underway, and a few common threads are emerging. Across the selections, paint brands are favoring warmth, depth, and colors they say are easy to live with—from softened blues and earthy greens to muted pinks and browns. The shades vary, but the thinking behind them often overlaps: a desire for homes that feel personal and comfortable.

As additional brands unveil their selections in the coming months, here is every 2027 Color of the Year we know so far.

Benjamin Moore has named Webster Green (HC-130) its 2027 Color of the Year, choosing an established shade from its Historical Color Collection. The brand’s senior manager of color marketing, Hannah Yeo, describes it as a “simply quintessential shade of green.” The team specifically sought a color without an obvious association with a particular decade—they wanted something that felt timeless.

Webster Green also anchors Benjamin Moore’s eight-color Color Trends 2027 palette, which pairs soft blues and pinks with rich copper, olive, and burgundy tones. “Webster Green HC-130 reflects this timeless appeal by pairing reassuring stability with optimistic energy,” Andrea Magno, the company’s vice president of brand, color, and integrated marketing, said in the announcement.

Little Greene has named Marle (335), a softened, warm brown from its newly launched paint collection, its 2027 Color of the Year. “The choice comes as brown makes a real comeback in both fashion and interiors,” the paint brand says in a press release. “As our understanding of neutrals has evolved, people have been drawn to brown's comforting appeal. They're also looking for complex, balanced shades that bring color and personality but stay neutral and easy to scheme.”

The brand suggests it for all-over color, including color-drenched rooms, for a cozy, cocooning effect.

Graham & Brown’s 2027 Color of the Year, Rose Earth, is a muted mineral pink with an earthy cast. The British interiors brand points to natural clay pigments as the shade’s inspiration, and positions it as an easy partner for warm neutrals, soft sage, and cooler tones.

Rose Earth arrives alongside the brand’s 2027 Design of the Year, Roselle Grove, a hand-painted floral wallpaper informed by the long history of chintz, and its Mural of the Year, Red Rose County, a painterly take on the Lancashire landscape. “Together, these designs tell our story of craft, color and heritage, each in their own unique ways,” says Iona Graham, Graham & Brown’s brand and marketing controller, in a statement.

Clark+Kensington’s Color of the Year is Vantage Point (38B-4), a rich midtone blue with a slightly softened quality. The shade is available exclusively at Ace Hardware, which developed the selection with the trend-forecasting firm Colour Hive.

“Homeowners are looking for more moments of connection at hoe, and color can have a powerful impact on how a space makes us feel,” Kim Kefko, Ace Hardware’s chief marketing officer, says in a statement. “Vantage Point brings a sense of warmth and character to interiors, offering a versatile blue that feels fresh for 2027 yet timeless enough to live with for years to come.”

Sherwin-Williams and its Lowe’s-exclusive HGTV Home by Sherwin-Williams paint line have named Celery, a soft yellow-green, their shared 2027 Color of the Year. Available as Celery SW 6421 and Celery HGSW6421, the shade was selected from the brand’s 2027 Colormix Trend Forecast, Rewild, a broader collection of dark greens, warm whites, plum browns, blues, reds, and purples. One of Rewild’s 10 “Continuity Colors,” Celery is positioned as a lighter, yellow-leaning alternative to the deeper hues in the forecast.

“Celery was chosen as the 2027 Color of the Year because it reflects today’s priorities: wellness, balance and connection,” Ashley Banbury, Sherwin-Williams’s color marketing manager, says.

Glidden has named Artifact (PPG10-16) its 2027 Color of the Year. The color is a warm, smoky blue—a familiar, easygoing shade.

“When everything feels like a lot, we feel like your space shouldn’t,” Ashley McCollum, Glidden’s color marketing manager, tells ELLE Decor. She describes Artifact as a “very fresh realignment”—a calm, grounded color that can refresh a room without the expense or disruption of a larger renovation. The brand suggests using it on walls, cabinetry, ceilings, and exterior details, though McCollum also pointed to a color-drenched bedroom or study as especially good applications.

Behr has named Grounded (T27-01), a brown-leaning olive green, its 2027 Color of the Year. The newly developed shade sits between moss and olive, with a brown cast that distinguishes it from the gray-green sages that have dominated paint decks in recent years.

“We created a completely new color that wasn’t in our collection before,” Kayla Kratz, Behr’s head of color, tells ELLE Decor. The company tested dozens of olive greens before landing on Grounded, which follows 2026’s smoky jade Hidden Gem. “We wanted this color with depth and dimension that just comes to life,” Kratz says.

Dutch Boy Paints is going all in on orange for 2027. Its Color of the Year, Deep Rooted (311-7DB), is a burnished, earth-toned shade with a terracotta-like cast.

The company shows the color alongside creamy whites and related orange tones, but also with blush pink and bright blue, or a muted blue-gray and forest green. “Deep Rooted offers a color that feels rich, grounding and deeply human,” Lisbeth Parada, Dutch Boy Paints’s color marketing manager, says.

Paint company Valspar has named Cottage Door (8004-38E) its 2027 Color of the Year. The brand describes the shade as a reflective midtone neutral blue, with both warm and cool undertones intended to give it broad appeal across interiors.

According to the brand, the pick reflects consumer interest in spaces that feel honest and grounding. “Cottage Door captures blue’s natural sense of security and trust while serving as a colorful new neutral that layers effortless into the home,” Valspar’s director of color marketing, Sue Kim, says. The company recommends pairing the blue with its warm gray Moon Shot (8005-2B), muted pink Apricot Blush (8003-8A), and light wood tones.

We will continue updating this story as 2027 Colors of the Year are announced, a rollout that typically begins in late summer and continues through December.

That smartwatch on your wrist knows your sleep score, heart rate and even how many steps you took on the way to your favorite coffee shop. But does it know how to earn your trust? For this People’s Picks, we want to know which wearables you’d vouch for.

CNET’s People’s Picks survey gives you the chance to share your thoughts on the tech devices and services you use and love the most. This month, it’s all about wearables like fitness trackers, smartwatches and smart glasses. You can take our 2-minute survey and tell us how your device holds up in terms of battery life, accuracy, comfort, app experience and overall satisfaction. The top-rated picks will make it into our roundup, so be sure to check back soon to see if your pick made the cut.

You can get all the specs from the retail site, but you can get real-world user insight, like whether the device survives months of daily wear or whether the insights feel accurate. That’s why we want to hear from the very ones using these products daily. Whether you’re an early adopter or have been loyal to your wearable for years, sharing your feedback in our survey helps others decide whether the tech they’re considering is a good pick.

The survey is open throughout November, so be sure to share your thoughts before it closes.

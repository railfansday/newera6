Ohio Supreme Court suspends law license of attorney convicted of voting multiple times

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Ohio Supreme Court on Monday suspended the law license of a man convicted of illegally voting in Ohio and Florida in 2020 and 2022. (Image from Shutterstock)

The Ohio Supreme Court on Monday suspended the law license of a man convicted of illegally voting in both Ohio and Florida in 2020 and 2022.

James Saunders, a former attorney for the IRS, was convicted of two felony counts of illegal voting after casting ballots in both Cuyahoga County, Ohio, and Broward County, Florida, during the 2020 and 2022 general elections. His last known business address is in Washington, D.C., according to the state supreme court in its Oct. 26 order.

The state supreme court imposed the suspension after Saunders failed to answer a disciplinary complaint filed by the Cleveland Metropolitan Bar Association, according to a story by Cleveland.com.

Saunders had previously been disbarred in Florida. His suspension in Ohio is indefinite, the Ohio Supreme Court said, and he “will not be reinstated to the practice of law in Ohio until such time as respondent is reinstated to the practice of law in the state of Florida.”

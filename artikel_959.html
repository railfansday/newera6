Canelo Alvarez could have "influenced" the referee to take a point off Christian Mbilli during their world title fight, claims Mbilli's promoter Camille Estephan.

Canelo beat Mbilli by unanimous decision in Riyadh on Saturday to reclaim the WBC world super-middleweight title with scores of 115-112, 116-111 and 117-110 in the Mexican superstar's favour.

But in the eighth round, Mbilli's hopes of a late charge were stalled when referee Hector Afu docked a point for hitting on the back of the head after Canelo gestured to the official following an exchange on the ropes.

"Close fight, hats off to Canelo - a great performance," Estephan said.

"As far as the ref, It seemed like he was influenced to take that point off. But I'm not in his shoes. I can't understand what went through his mind.

"Regardless, there will be many more big fights for Christian. He belongs."

Canelo was returning to boxing a year after his stunning loss to Terence Crawford that cost him his undisputed super-middleweight crown and ended his reign as holder of the division's four major titles.

Mbilli was elevated to full WBC champion after Crawford's departure from the division, having previously held ⁠the interim title with his victory over Maciej Sulecki in June 2025.

The 31-year-old Cameroon-born boxer had fought to ​a split draw against Lester Martinez on the undercard of the Alvarez-Crawford showdown, ending ​a run of 29 consecutive professional victories, while the loss now to Cancelo is his first as a professional.

In a post on Instagram, translated from French, Mbilli wrote: "Last night didn't go the way I wanted. I'll learn from this experience, get back in the gym, and come back stronger.

"Thank you to my team and to all of you for your messages of support."

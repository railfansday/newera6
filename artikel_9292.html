Californian Michelin-starred restaurant serves up book on the art of hospitality and farming

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Sonoma Wine Country’s SingleThread focuses on aesthetics as much as seasonality and local produce in its cuisine © John Troxell

Art of Luxury magazine, published twice per year by The Art Newspaper, explores how grande marque fashion, jewellery, travel and lifestyle interact with artists, the art market and the museums and heritage sector.

Kaiseki is a type of Japanese dining offering up multiple courses that together form one long and delicious experience. This is precisely what is on offer at SingleThread, a three-star Michelin restaurant in California’s Sonoma Wine Country, so it’s perhaps no surprise that its proprietors, Kyle and Katina Connaughton, describe themselves as storytellers. But SingleThread is also a 24-acre farm, and a five-room inn, and works closely with nearby ranchers who rear the meat it uses. It’s a multi-volume affair.

Now, all its wisdom, history, instructions for ikebana flower arranging, planting diagrams for the kitchen garden, and a number of recipes are gathered together in one book. As with its restaurant ethos, it’s a publication that assumes you have a great deal of time and inclination to spare: there’s an entire section on the negi onion.

On the other hand, if you’re unlikely to visit Sonoma any time soon, or don’t fancy joining a months-long waiting list, this book is a treat. The photography is stunning and, unlike a ten-course meal—which, however delightful, can feel like being held hostage and will cost a bare minimum of £500—you can dip in and out at leisure in the comfort of your own home.

How to Write a CV That Gets You Hired in 2026

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Posted on Nov 3 • Originally published at all-done.xyz

Most hiring managers decide very quickly whether a CV is worth a second look, and in many cases software screens it before a person does. That means a CV has two audiences: the tracking system and the human reader. The good news is that most rejections come from problems you can fix in one careful rewrite.

Disclosure: I am the maker of All Done, a site with 39 free online tools. This post is a cross-post of my guide on All Done, linked at the end.

Use a simple order that both people and software can follow: contact details, a short professional summary, core skills, experience in reverse order, then education and certifications. Use standard headings such as Work Experience, Education, and Skills, rather than creative labels. A single column works best, because multi-column layouts, tables, and graphics can scramble when a system parses the file.

Length depends on where you apply. Two pages is a common maximum in the UK, while early-career applicants in the US are often expected to keep it to one page. Save the file with a clear name, such as Firstname-Lastname-CV-2026.

A line that only describes you in general terms is hard to evaluate. A line with a result is much stronger. For each role, try this pattern: an action verb, what you did, and a measurable outcome. Examples include revenue gained, time saved, team size, or a percentage improvement. Your own numbers matter more than polished wording, because only you know what you actually achieved.

Keep the design plain and readable: one column, a standard font, and contact details in the main body rather than in a header or footer. Check for typos in skill names, because a misspelled skill may not match a search at all. In most UK and US applications, leave out a photo. Most importantly, tailor the CV to each posting instead of sending the same version everywhere; mirror the language of the job description where it honestly matches your experience.

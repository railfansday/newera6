AAWireless expands beyond Android Auto adapters with new cooled Qi2 car charger

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

AAWireless is known for helping you convert a wired Android Auto connection into a wireless one, but the company is now launching a new product – an active-cooled Qi2 car charger.

“AAWireless Charge” is a Qi2 25W wireless car charger that costs $50. The charger is compatible with MagSafe, Pixelsnap, and Qi2 in general, and supports up to 25W charging speeds on compatible phones.

“Charge” also includes active cooling support with a built-in fan that the company says is “quiet” when active.

Included in the box you’ll find both an air vent mount as well as an adhesive base. There are also two magnetic ring adapters in the box for “Qi2 Ready” phones that don’t have magnets built-in or a magnetic case available. AAWireless says to use your car’s 12V outlet with the included adapter for “best performance,” but you can also use existing USB-C connections so long as they deliver enough power.

To be clear, there’s no wireless adapter built into the charger (as cool as that would be), but you can use this alongside an AAWireless adapter while driving, the company specifically mentions.

While AAWireless adapters remain available on Amazon, “AAWireless Charge” is available solely from the company’s website.

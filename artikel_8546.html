The hybrid withdrawal plan will leave me with an $80K RMD in a dozen years from the remaining earnings and company match. This is where the complexity of managing taxes meets the complexity of having 15 different ‘bucket refill’ strategies, as ‘bucket 1’ has to be refilled at one point. I avoid that situation by having the distribution levers into cash I choose from. I had a pension plan, but it was later converted into a cash balance plan and dissolved before I could collect it. When I accounted for the pension, it was accounted for as an income stream, but the CBP plan termination rollover was rolled into the 401k into fixed income. The pensioner has no choice but to account for monthly pension checks as a taxable income stream. This is why I built the majority of equities in the taxable account and use municipal funds. The Roth I plan to never touch and is at 80/20 AA.

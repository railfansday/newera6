Spanish match abandoned as footballer is rushed to hospital after sickening collision with goalkeeper left him unconscious - with team-mates in tears as he is revived in ambulance

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

By HARRY BAMFORTH, LIVE SPORTS COVERAGE REPORTER

Published: 14:16 EDT, 2 November 2026 | Updated: 15:14 EDT, 2 November 2026

Tenerife striker Jesus de ​Miguel has been admitted to intensive care after collapsing during a Spanish second division clash with Andorra on Monday.

The 30-year-old is in stable condition, the club has confirmed, after a horrifying collision with goalkeeper ‌Jesus Owono led to the fixture being abandoned deep into stoppage time with the scores level at 2-2.

A club statement read: 'CD Tenerife player Jesús de Miguel is admitted to the ICU at the University Hospital of Nuestra Señora de Candelaria, stable despite the severity of his condition.

'The medical services at said hospital will be present and will report when there are updates.'

De Miguel is believed to have suffered a cardiac arrest while being transported to hospital and had to be revived inside the ambulance, according to Spanish media outlet Marca.

It has also been reported that while the Spaniard is stable, he remains unconscious.

Jesus de ​Miguel (pictured playing for former club Castellon) has been taken to intensive care

Tenerife's De ​Miguel was wiped out by Jesus Owono during a Spanish second tier match

The game had entered the seventh minute of stoppage time when De Miguel looked set to race away on the counter-attack for Tenerife in search of a winner.

As he watched a long ball drop into his path, the striker appeared to be struck in the chest by Owono's flying knee as he attempted to clear the ball near the halfway line.

After initially rolling around on the floor, De Miguel became motionless, and medical staff rushed onto the pitch to attend to him.

De Miguel was seen to for over five minutes while his team-mates were visibly distressed, with some appearing to be in tears.

Owono was initially given a red card for the challenge before the decision was soon taken by the referee to abandon the fixture.

The stadium has been in complete silence while De Miguel was being treated, but as he was taken off the pitch the striker was given a standing ovation by the crowd.

De Miguel was rushed to hospital following the incident after appearing motionless on the pitch

After being taken to an ambulance on a stretcher, there is believed to have been an approximate 15-minute wait before they made their way to the hospital.

Andorra also released a statement, which read: 'We want to send all our strength and best wishes to Jesus de Miguel after the serious incident suffered during today's match.

'We hope he recovers soon. A very strong hug to the player, his family and Tenerife.'

Despite remaining unconcsious in intensive care the player is believed to be 'breathing on his own'.

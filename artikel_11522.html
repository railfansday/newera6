DI Men's Transfer Portal Tracker 2027

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

IL is pleased to introduce our Men's Lacrosse Transfer Portal Tracker for 2027.

The NCAA Transfer Portal opened to graduate men's lacrosse players on Thursday, Oct. 1. The graduate students who enter with that designation are largely expressing their interest to transfer for the 2027-28 academic year, and the 2028 spring lacrosse season.

With the implementation of the NCAA's age-based eligibility standard (known casually as "5-in-5"), Portal activity is expected to be particularly high among graduates from colleges, universities and conferences that either don't offer graduate programs or don't allow grad students to compete in sports. That's why this list is disproportionately populated by Ivy League and Patriot League athletes.

The undergraduate window for entering opens on Dec. 1, 2026, and those athletes will largely be expressing their interest in transferring mid-year in order to play at their new destination for the 2027 spring lacrosse season.

Each outgoing portal entrant's listed class is his projected eligibility for next season, and those entering as grad students are noted as "Gr." This list will be updated as players enter or withdraw from the Portal or make a confirmed decision. To submit a confirmed decision or recent entrant, e-mail kbrown@insidelacrosse.com, mattkinnear@insidelacrosse.com and tfoy@insidelacrosse.com or DM @insidelacrosse on Instagram.

Anthony McMullan, SSDM, Gr. to Notre Dame

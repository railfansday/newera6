Canadian law firms see increased cyberattacks, data breaches

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Four Canadian law firms have said they experienced recent data breaches amid concerns that cyberattacks are increasingly targeting the legal and financial industries. (Image from Shutterstock)

Four Canadian law firms have said they experienced recent data breaches amid concerns that cyberattacks are increasingly targeting the legal and financial industries.

McCarthy Tétrault, Borden Ladner Gervais, Bennett Jones and Davies Ward Phillips & Vineberg said the damage has been contained and that they are alerting clients affected by the breaches. The firms also said they have engaged cybersecurity companies to investigate and remediate, according to a story by Law.com.

The federal Canadian Centre for Cyber Security issued a warning last week about an ongoing phishing campaign targeting organizations in the legal and financial sectors.

“Multiple Canadian firms have been targeted by individuals impersonating internal IT support and directing employees to fraudulent sign-in pages,” according to the warning.

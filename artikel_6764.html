When to Watch New Episodes of <i><em>The Gilded Age</em></i> Season 4

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Warning: Spoilers for season 3 of The Gilded Age.

Tensions will undoubtedly come to a head in The Gilded Age season 4. The HBO period drama, created and written by Julian Fellowes, will continue to follow the wealthiest figures of 1880s New York—from both old money and new money—as they navigate tense marriages, feuds, and a slowly crumbling social structure.

Season 3 gave fans a clue to what might unfold in the upcoming season: Bertha and George’s relationship is on the rocks, and a divorce might be in their future. Larry and Marian could very well tie the knot, but Peggy and Dr. William Kirkland’s marriage plans might be paused because of his overbearing mother. Gladys also has a major question mark, too, after revealing she’s pregnant at the end of the season.

This season sees its regular cast—which includes Carrie Coon, Cynthia Nixon, Louisa Jacobson, Denée Benton, Taissa Farmiga, Morgan Spector, and Christine Baranski—returning, with the addition of new power players like James Scully (You), Dennis Haysbert (24), Maggie Kuntz (John Proctor is the Villain), Neal Huff (Mare of Easttown), and Bonnie Milligan (Search Party).

Here’s how to stay up to date with new episodes of The Gilded Age.

Season 4 of The Gilded Age contains a total of eight episodes set to run from November 1 through December 20.

New episodes of The Gilded Age will premiere on HBO and stream on HBO Max each Sunday at 9 P.M. ET/6 P.M. PT. See the full release schedule below.

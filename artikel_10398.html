The official Phantom Blade Zero smart glasses from Viture are here

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Viture Phantom Beast features a unique design, comes in a special metal-and-leather scabbard carrying case, and includes three exclusive Phantom Blade Zero collectibles.

Viture is rolling out another pair of smart glasses based on its highly acclaimed Viture Beast release from earlier this year. Viture Phantom Beast is the company’s second special edition pair of XR Glasses, this time partnering with S-GAME for the highly anticipated release of Phantom Blade Zero today, October 30, for $599 at Viture’s website.

Phantom Beast uses the same excellent 3DoF tracking and clean optics as the standard Viture Beast XR Glasses, but everything else is new. That includes a new color scheme as well as metal decals on the temples, modeled after Phantom Zero Blade’s Soul Blade. The carrying case “unsheaths” this time around and is made of metal and leather, giving a higher-quality feel to the overall package.

Viture Phantom Beast is plug-and-play ready for any smartphone or computer that supports USB-C display output. The glasses then display a virtual screen equivalent to a 174-inch display (the size and distance are adjustable in-glasses), with a 1200p resolution per eye, 120Hz refresh rate, 1,250-nit peak brightness, 9-level electrochromic dimming, native 3DoF tracking, and built-in HARMAN AudioEFX speakers in the temples.

If you want to connect them to a PS5, you’ll need to grab this $29 cable. Viture also remains one of the only smart glasses that work with a Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, but you’ll need this Mobile Dock Mini, which doubles as a 10,000mAh battery pack to amplify the Switch’s battery life.

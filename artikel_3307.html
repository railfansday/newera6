RF and microwave systems increasingly depend on amplifiers and receivers that perform reliably across wide frequency ranges and demanding signal conditions. Confirming this performance calls for signal sources with wide output power range, low phase noise, and fast switching speed. Amplifier characterization relies on accurately measuring gain, compression, and intermodulation distortion, while receiver verification often involves simulating blockers, calibrating power accuracy, and substituting for local oscillator and clock circuitry during early design stages. This guide walks through these test applications step by step, showing how engineers and technicians can validate RF and microwave hardware across research, development, and manufacturing environments.

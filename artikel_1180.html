Three matchdays are in the books for the 2026-27 UEFA Women's Champions League, and that means the league phase has offically reached the halfway point. The tournament table tells the truth in points, but power rankings tell a story between the results.

While Matchday 3 gave some predictability, it also shifted some of the power trends between teams.

Toobit, the award-winning global cryptocurrency exchange, today announces its participation as a Premium Sponsor of Blockchain Life 2026, taking place December 1–2 at Festival Arena in Dubai, UAE.

Building on last year’s presence at Blockchain Life, Toobit will welcome attendees at Booth A10, where they can meet the team, explore the exchange’s trading products, and learn about its latest platform developments.

The return follows Toobit’s double win at the Blockchain Life Awards 2025, where the exchange received the Best CEX User Interface and Best CEX Educational Platform awards. Both honors were determined through public voting by the crypto community, recognizing Toobit’s efforts to make trading more accessible through intuitive platform design and educational resources.

Now in its 17th edition, Blockchain Life is expected to attract more than 15,000 attendees from over 130 countries, alongside more than 200 speakers. The two-day forum will bring together traders, investors, entrepreneurs, and industry leaders to discuss developments in cryptocurrency, Web3, mining, and artificial intelligence.

The UAE continues to strengthen its position as a global digital asset hub. According to Henley & Partners’ 2026 Crypto Adoption Index, the country ranked second among 36 assessed markets, rising from fifth place in the previous edition. The ranking highlights the UAE’s growing prominence in the international cryptocurrency industry

Toobit is where the future of crypto trading unfolds. The award-winning cryptocurrency derivatives exchange provides zero-fee spot trading, AI trading tools, and high leverage for both crypto and TradFi markets. Built for those who thrive on exploring new frontiers, Toobit maintains a fair, secure, and transparent environment for traders to navigate digital asset markets.

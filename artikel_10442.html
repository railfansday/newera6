Google Home update rolling out with Nest Cam, Matter, energy-saving improvements & fixes

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

There’s a new Google Home update rolling out that delivers improvements for Nest Cam owners, Matter devices, and more.

Detailed on Google’s changelog, there’s a host of new updates rolling out for the Google Home ecosystem, many of which can be found directly in the Home app. And there’s a lot in this one. The October 29 update is one of the biggest in recent memory.

Beyond that, there are some bigger improvements to the Google Home app in this latest update. Matter devices, for example, now supports showing energy data from compatible devices through Home for Android, while Matter-connected doors and windows will now show the correct icons and controls through the app. Google explains:

The app now recognizes specific Matter closure types—including garage doors, gates, windows, blinds, and cabinets—displaying the correct device icon and controls instead of treating every closure as a generic gate.

There are also new one-tap shortcuts for thermostats to help you save energy during colder months.

And all of that is just the app itself. Google is also rolling out firmware updates for various home devices including Chromecast with Google TV.

Kristen Bell’s Viral BaubleBar Monogram Tote Is Less Than $60 Right Now—And It’s My Favorite Personalized Accessory

If there’s any opportunity to get my initials on something, I’m all for it. I don’t care if it gives sorority-girl vibes or reminds me of my elementary school days—personalized pieces are such a classic way to make something feel a little more me.

And after spotting Kristen Bell carrying the iconic BaubleBar Custom Alpha tote customized with her initials (a style also vetted by Emma Roberts and Alix Earle, might I add!), I’m officially sold on getting one of my own. Even better? The brand is currently offering 25% off during its annual custom event, bringing the price from $78 down to $58.50—basically $20 off!

While out in New York City ahead of the premiere of her hit Netflix show Nobody Wants This, which she stars in alongside Adam Brody, Kristen Bell wore a simple yet elevated outfit featuring a white tee, blazer, jeans, pointed-toe heels, and a bag with her initials “KB” on it that gave the whole look an extra punch. The small size fits all the everyday essentials, plus a Kindle or a pouch for miscellaneous on-the-go items. Its canvas material also makes it a durable option for everyday use. And this updated version comes with a trolley sleeve, making it easy to slide over your suitcase handle when traveling!

As for customization, you can choose from 19 different tote colors and 13 letter colors to make it your own. You’re allowed up to three characters for your monogram, so you can go with your first, middle, and last initials, just your first and middle, or, if you’re like me and have a three-letter nickname (or official government name!), you have that option, too.

Listen, I love a personalized gift for yourself, but I gotta say, these totes make amazing presents for the loved ones in your life! You really can’t go wrong with something customized—it feels thoughtful, personal, and like you put in extra effort to find something special. Have a bachelorette party or wedding coming up? These would also make great goodie bags for your guests! Either way, this deal couldn’t have come at a better time with the holiday season right around the corner.

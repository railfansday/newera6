Sydney Sweeney’s 131-Minute R-Rated Franchise Starter Officially Confirms New 2027 Release

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Sydney Sweeney has the Midas touch at the moment. Not only is she one of Hollywood's brightest young stars on-screen, she's also becoming one of the most canny producers off-screen too, and this latest development involving one of her biggest breakout hits so far is proof positive of that. Last year, Sweeney thrilled audiences with her role as a housemaid who got a little more than she bargained for, and now, audiences can see it all in a new light.

Lionsgate has officially confirmed that The Housemaid will make its theatrical stage debut on September 14, 2027, at London's Richmond Theatre, ahead of a planned tour. According to Deadline, the new production will adapt Freida McFadden's bestselling novel for the stage, expanding the franchise beyond the enormously successful 2025 movie.

The original movie, directed by Paul Feig (Bridesmaids) from a screenplay by Rebecca Sonnenshine, followed Millie Calloway, a young woman with a troubled past who accepts a job working as a live-in maid for a wealthy family. Unfortunately, what initially seems like an opportunity to rebuild her life quickly becomes a nightmare as she discovers that her new employers are hiding some deeply disturbing secrets.

The film stars Sweeney (Euphoria) as Millie Calloway, Amanda Seyfried (Mamma Mia!) as Nina Winchester, Brandon Sklenar (1923) as Andrew Winchester, Michele Morrone (365 Days) as Enzo, Elizabeth Perkins (Big) as Evelyn Winchester, Amanda Joy Erickson as Lexi, and Megan Ferguson (The Comedians) as Jillian.

The franchise's success has already resulted in a sequel, The Housemaid's Secret, which began production in late October. Sweeney will return as Millie alongside Morrone as Enzo, with Kirsten Dunst, Paul Anthony Kelly, Brittany Snow, and Stephen Kalyn joining the cast. Jenefer Brown, Lionsgate's President of Global Products & Experiences added:

With the stage play debuting next year and our sequel film in production, the momentum behind The Housemaid franchise continues to grow. We’re thrilled to work with Simon, Hanna, Bekah, and Matthew to bring this story to the stage in an entirely new way, giving audiences the chance to experience its suspense, twists, and turns live.

The Housemaid stage adaptation premieres at Richmond Theatre in London on September 14, 2027, while The Housemaid's Secret is scheduled to arrive in theaters on December 17, 2027. The original movie is streaming now on HBO Max in the United States.

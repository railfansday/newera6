Tottenham injury update: Lucas Bergvall, Mykhailo Mudryk and Savio latest news and return dates

Tottenham supporters were left sweating over Archie Gray after the versatile midfielder was substituted at half time against Crystal Palace.

The England youth international, who deputised for Pedro Porro once more at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, had opened the scoring for the hosts in their 2-0 win on Saturday evening.

However, he was withdrawn at the interval by Roberto De Zerbi, replaced by Porro for the second half.

De Zerbi reassured Spurs fans that Gray was merely subbed off as he had been booked in the opening period, and there was no injury concern.

He did, however, admit that Porro, who has returned from a one-month lay-off with a hamstring problem, had requested not to start the match after revealing to his manager that he did not feel capable of playing from the off.

Otherwise, here is all the latest Tottenham injury news and return dates.

Tosin looked impressive against Coventry in his first start in his new colours, but he picked up a calf problem in that first outing.

He did not feature against his former side Chelsea, and was once more omitted from the squad to face Palace.

Potential return date: Saturday November 7, vs Leeds (A)

Swedish midfielder Bergvall has been sidelined since the 1-1 draw at Manchester United.

He picked up a knock at Old Trafford which was thought to not be serious, with De Zerbi saying that he would be out for a fortnight.

He did not feature against Chelsea or Palace, but could well be back in contention for the trip to Leeds.

“Bergvall had a problem in Manchester early on in the first chance he created for [Dominic] Solanke,” De Zerbi said earlier this month.

“He felt pain in his knee but felt fine and on Sunday he came to the training ground and he'll be out for two weeks.”

New signing Savio also suffered an injury against United, picking up a muscle strain.

He did not play against either Chelsea or Palace, and De Zerbi revealed that the Brazilian was “injured” ahead of the encounter with the Eagles over the weekend.

The Italian said that Spurs had lost the winger for “some weeks” ahead of their trip to Marbella, but he did give a more positive update on the forward ahead of the game against Palace, saying that Savio would likely return around the November international break.

Potential return date: Saturday November 21, vs Ipswich (H)

Summer loanee Mudryk has played just once for Spurs since joining from Chelsea.

The winger picked up what De Zerbi called a “stupid” ankle injury in training following his debut against Nottingham Forest, and he was thought to be out for between six to eight weeks back in September.

It was thought that he would be in contention to come back in late October, but he was missing from the squad to face Palace, and as such, he will likely be back in action this month.

Potential return date: Saturday November 7, vs Leeds (A)

Frenchman Odobert has been a long-term absentee, recovering from an ACL tear that he picked up against Newcastle back in February.

The winger is believed to be progressing towards an eventual return, which could come next month.

De Zerbi, however, has been cautious in his updates on the forward.

“I don't know, because he has not started yet to work with us,” the Italian said recently. “I have no idea [when he will return].”

Dutchman Simons is in the same boat as Odobert, suffering a torn ACL.

He ruptured the ligament against Wolves in April last season, and has not featured at all this term.

He has been seen working with a ball once more at Hotspur Way, but a full return to the field is expected after the turn of the year.

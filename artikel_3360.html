Amazon currently has the 32-inch Samsung Odyssey OLED G8 (G80SH) for $999.99, down from $1,299.99, and price trackers show that this is its lowest price so far. This is the newer 4K OLED model, not Samsung’s similarly named G80HS, which is a 6K IPS variant, and it makes the most sense for someone who wants one screen for both high-end gaming and everyday desktop use without moving to a larger ultrawide.

Its 4K QD-OLED panel runs at 240Hz, which gives the G80SH enough speed for competitive shooters and racing games while still keeping slower, more cinematic games looking sharp. You will need a powerful PC to get anywhere close to 4K at 240Hz, but the monitor is at least equipped for newer graphics hardware with DisplayPort 2.1 and HDMI 2.1. It also has USB-C with up to 98W of power delivery, so if this monitor doubles as a work setup, a compatible laptop can send video and charge over the same cable instead of adding another wire to your desk. FreeSync Premium Pro and G-SYNC compatibility should also help keep tearing under control, though OLED monitors can still show visible VRR flicker in darker scenes when frame pacing is inconsistent.

The bigger visual difference, though, shows up in darker games and HDR content, where blacks stay properly black and bright effects stand out without the blooming you often see on LCD monitors. That said, QD-OLED does have its own drawbacks, since blacks can take on a purplish tint in a brightly lit room, while burn-in remains a long-term concern—though Samsung does include active cooling and several screen-protection features to reduce uneven wear and says burn-in from normal use is covered under warranty. If you mostly play at lower frame rates or do not care much about OLED contrast, this is probably more monitor than you need, but for someone with the graphics hardware to take advantage of 4K at 240Hz, this discount makes the G80SH much easier to justify.

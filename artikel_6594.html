Paul Hollywood Once Had A Very Different Look &amp; <i><em>Bake Off </em></i>Fans Know Exactly Who He Resembles

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Turns out the man behind the Hollywood handshake has quite the origin story.

If you know Paul Hollywood from The Great British Bake Off, you know the routine: a steely blue-eyed stare, a brutally honest critique, and, if you're lucky, one of those coveted Hollywood handshakes. But the man who's been judging everybody else's baking since 2010 nearly ended up in an entirely different creative field—and he had a whole lot more hair while figuring it out.

In an Instagram video posted on October 25, Hollywood revealed how he went from aspiring artist to professional baker, a career change that apparently came down to a £500 haircut.

After leaving school at 16, Hollywood enrolled in art school, where he tried everything from pottery and photography to textiles, fine art, and sculpture. He eventually settled on the latter, and by all accounts, was having a pretty great time. Then his father, who owned several bakeries, came along with a different career proposal.

Hollywood wasn't exactly eager to leave art school behind as he was enjoying life there all while sporting a glorious head of long hair. So his dad sweetened the deal by offering him a hefty chunk of change to chop it all off and join the family business.

Apparently, that was enough to get the scissors moving. Hollywood shaved his head, traded sculpture for sourdough, and started learning the ropes.

He said he took to baking surprisingly quickly, even embracing the rather brutal hours. Hollywood recalled getting up at 1 or 2 a.m. six days a week, with Friday shifts sometimes starting at 11 p.m.

From there, he went on to work at major hotels across the U.K., eventually took his talents abroad, and found himself on television. As Hollywood put it, "The rest is history."

But the most entertaining part of this story arrived the following day, when Hollywood posted photographic evidence of the hair that’d been worth £500.

"Pictures mid long hair obviously with cat too !!" he captioned the throwback, which shows a much younger Hollywood holding a fluffy cat while sporting shoulder-length dark hair. It's quite a departure from the neatly cropped silver look we've come to know, and somehow, the cat isn't even the most talked-about part of the picture.

That's because fans immediately noticed a striking resemblance to someone else in the Bake Off tent: co-host Noel Fielding.

"Looking like Noel here Mr H!" one person commented. "Thought this was Noel for a second 😆," another admitted, while a third agreed, "this could easily be Noel."

Hollywood wasn't about to let the comparisons go unanswered, either. "Agree, I’m older so he was channeling me!" he joked in response.

Now I'd like Noel to dig up a photo of himself with short hair. It seems only fair.

The Division 2 Echoes of Central Park DLC release countdown: Exact date and time

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The Echoes of Central Park DLC is coming soon to The Division 2, and we won’t have to wait long to try out the major expansion. Ubisoft has already teased the DLC, which will feature a new map and plenty of new story content for players to explore. First, let’s find out when Central Park becomes available as an in-game region to all of us.

The Echoes of Central Park DLC goes live worldwide on Tuesday, Nov. 3. The release date was announced earlier by the developers, and I am not expecting any change this late. The following countdown will expire once the DLC goes live.

The countdown is based on the following times.

The new DLC allows you to explore Central Park for the first time. The park is now home to a new threat that lurks in the shadows. You’ll not only face them, but also uncover the park’s mystery.

You’ll find three reluctant heroes to guide you through the park. Each ally has a unique gameplay style, so you have the choice of who should accompany you.

Echoes of Central Park also brings a new endgame experience. Once you complete the story, you can return to the location to dictate the fight, pursue enemies, and experience the different modifiers that are at play.

Once you defeat the different enemies present at Central Park, you’ll gain six new skill variants inspired by the same horrors you face. Overall, there’s plenty to experience, whether you’re a veteran or new to the series.

Get the American Express® Business Gold Card if your biggest business costs fall in its 4X categories, such as advertising, software, and restaurants, and you’ll use its statement credits. Your top two each month earn 4 points per dollar, on up to $150,000 a year. If those costs are small, take the no-fee Blue Business Plus instead.

Its six bonus categories are U.S. advertising, electronics and software, restaurants, gas stations, and wireless service, plus transit, while the personal Gold Card’s top rate goes only to restaurants and U.S. supermarkets. Amex says freelancers, gig workers, and sole proprietors can apply, giving a Social Security number if they have no employer identification number (EIN).

At 1 cent a point, $2,000 a month in its top two categories, with nothing else on the card, earns $480 a year more than the Blue Business Plus would, or $105 after the $375 fee, not counting credits. Owners who spend mostly elsewhere and won’t use the credits do better with the no-fee Blue Business Plus.

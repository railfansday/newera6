Motorola has already confirmed its Signature 27 flagship is launching in November

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Following a “coming months” teaser earlier this year, Motorola has confirmed that its flagship Signature 27 device is launching in the US in November.

The Motorola Signature 27 is the brand’s first proper flagship smartphone in years. Announced in September, it’s set to pack Qualcomm’s new Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 6 chipset and, based on more recent leaks, it’s also going to have some killer cameras to back that up.

Motorola also confirmed this one would be coming to the US, but only offered a timeline of “in the coming months.” But, as it turns out, a more specific timeline has been available this whole time.

A landing page on Motorola’s US website has confirmed a November launch for the device. Motorola says:

Unveiled at the Snapdragon Summit, the new motorola signature 27 delivers unprecedented speeds with Qualcomm’s Snapdragon® 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 mobile platform, plus breakthrough AI and an elite-level camera system. Stay tuned for more, coming this November.

This landing page isn’t new, with the November timeline having been in place since the September announcement, but it went under the radar until Motorola added the same note to a landing page in India as spotted by GSMArena. It was only mentioned in passing around the initial announcement.

A November launch date is much earlier than Motorola’s announcement initially suggested, and also lines up with this week’s retail leak. As for an exact timeline, we’ll have to wait and see, but it’s clearly coming up very soon.

Get the no-annual-fee U.S. Bank Shield® Visa® Card if you’ll pay off a big purchase or a balance transfer before its 0% interest period ends. Purchases get 0% for 21 billing cycles, as do balances you move in the first 60 days. If you pay in full every month, pick a card that pays cash back on everyday spending.

The Shield replaced U.S. Bank’s Visa Platinum, which the bank no longer offers. Business owners have a card of their own, the U.S. Bank Business Shield Visa.

Readers who need the Shield’s full 0% for 21 billing cycles should get it and plan to clear the balance before the 0% ends. U.S. Bank’s Cash+ Visa Signature, with 0% for 15 billing cycles plus up to 5% cash back, is the better pick for a balance you’ll clear in that time.

Why Capri Holdings Stock Crushed it Today

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The company was the target of a clutch of analyst price target raises.

Still riding the positive momentum from a satisfying quarterly earnings report, Capri Holdings (CPRI +7.33%) stock cruised to a more than 7% gain on Monday. Also providing plenty of fuel for this was a set of analyst price target increases on the fashion conglomerate's stock.

Capri's second quarter of fiscal 2027 results were published Friday morning. Although revenue declined by almost 6% year over year to $809 million, the company flipped to a non-GAAP (adjusted) net income of $45 million. On top of that, it obliterated analyst profitability estimates and comfortably beat on the top line.

On Monday, analysts tracking the stock reacted overwhelmingly positively to the consumer discretionary company's performance. Several expressed this by raising their Capri price targets.

Among these was Brooke Roach from the ever-influential investment bank Goldman Sachs (GS +0.47%). She lifted hers to $24 from $18, albeit while maintaining her neutral recommendation. A more bullish note was authored by Barclays' (BCS +1.39%) Adrienne Yih, who now feels the stock is worth $24 apiece, up from her preceding $19. She kept her overweight (i.e., buy) recommendation intact.

I'm always rather hesitant about fashion stocks, as they're subject to trends, whims, and the health of the economy. That said, Capri's Jimmy Choo line did especially well and looks set to continue doing so, even if the larger Michael Kors unit is currently in turnaround mode. Meanwhile, management is effectively trimming costs behind the scenes. If I were to consider buying any fashion title, I'd certainly add this one to the list.

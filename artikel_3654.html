Homes with shedding pets, mixed flooring, and low furniture can quickly reveal the shortcomings of a basic robot vacuum. The Roborock Qrevo CurvX is built to handle those challenges, and it is currently available on Amazon for $749.99, down from $1,499.99. That is a $750 discount, and price trackers also indicate it is the lowest price recorded for this model. And while $750 is still a considerable amount to spend on a cleaning appliance, the discount brings it closer to midrange model pricing while retaining features normally associated with flagship machines.

The CurvX earns that higher price by doing more than simply vacuuming an open stretch of floor—its 22,000 pascals of suction can handle pet hair, crumbs, and debris caught in carpet, while the DuoDivide main brush guides hair toward the center to reduce tangles. There’s also an extendable side brush that reaches along walls and into corners, and a lifting chassis that helps the robot cross rugs, exercise mats, and other raised surfaces without getting stuck. Its mop pads are similarly adaptable, extending toward room edges for better coverage and lifting by up to 17 millimeters over carpet so the CurvX can move between hard floors and rugs without dragging a wet mop across both.

When it encounters low furniture, its top-mounted LiDAR sensor retracts to reduce the robot’s height to 3.14 inches, allowing it to clean beneath most sofas and beds. And once the CurvX finishes cleaning, its docking station takes care of much of the work that would otherwise fall to you—it empties the dustbin, refills the water tank, washes the mop pads with water heated to 176 degrees Fahrenheit, and dries them with air heated to 113 degrees. It also has a dirt sensor that can prompt it to wash its mop pads again or repeat a cleaning pass when an area needs more attention. The Roborock app gives you further control, allowing you to clean specific rooms, create schedules, set virtual barriers, and mark areas the robot should avoid, while SmartPlan 2.0 adapts the routine to different rooms and floor types.

Sheffield United are preparing to visit Deepdale to take on the Championship basement boys Preston North End.

“We needed to put in a 95-minute performance together,” said Wilder. “We’re trying to find that consistency.

“A lot of clubs are still finding their way after 12 games and they’re all looking for that consistency. It was up to us to take the initiative and we certainly did. Everybody turned up.

“Hopefully we can kick on from tonight’s performance. I thought we were the better team and fully deserved the win.”

Here are the key TV and streaming details as Sheffield United prepare to do battle with Preston.

Kick-off at Deepdale is scheduled for 7:45pm on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Yes - those not making the journey to Deepdale will be able to watch the action unfold from the comfort of their own homes. The fixture has not been given top billing on any of the main Sky Sports channels but can be viewed as an alternative broadcast via the Sky Sports+ service. Subscripions can be purchased online.

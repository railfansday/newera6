Enroll in Freedom Debt Relief only if you can’t repay your unsecured debt even on a nonprofit debt management plan. Its fee, 15% to 25% of what you enroll, comes only after you approve a settlement and its first payment goes out. Still, your credit score will likely drop, and forgiven debt can be taxed as income.

Freedom Debt Relief negotiates down unsecured debts and pays each settlement from an account you fund monthly. It’s for people with $7,500 or more of such debt who are struggling after a hardship.

Freedom Debt Relief suits people who can’t repay unsecured debt even at a nonprofit debt management plan’s lower rates, can keep up deposits for up to four years, and would rather pay its fee than negotiate with creditors themselves. Borrowers who could repay on such a plan should take it, and those in the 10 states Freedom skips can compare Accredited Debt Relief, which offers evaluations in all 50 states.

Swansea City must offer new deal to emerging talent after outstanding first-team breakthrough: Opinion

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Swansea City have been the surprise team in the Championship this campaign. The Swans sit 2nd in the table and have the joint-best defensive record in the league.

Their brilliant start after 13 games isn’t the only shock to Vitor Matos, with the emergence of Filip Lissah from the academy also welcome. The 21-year-old spent last season on loan at Falkirk in the Scottish Premiership, and his displays there have translated to the Welsh outfit’s first team.

The Swansea City defender has missed just one game this term due to suspension. With his improving performances each week, conversations within the club must be held to discuss his future, especially with his contract only running until 2028.

The Swans can extend Lissah’s current deal by 12 months when his contract expires at the end of the 2027 season. They are certain to take up the option, but they should address any future uncertainty by offering him a long-term improved deal.

The 21-year-old has been one of Swansea City’s standout players this term. Regardless of his contract status, the Welsh outfit must secure his future. The academy graduate has made 91 line-breaking passes this campaign and won 73 duels.

Despite being naturally a centre-back, Lissah has proven to be a dependable option in defence for his boyhood club. He has put in terrific displays also at right-back. The Czech U21 international has made 84 defensive contributions alongside winning 15 fouls for Matos’ side.

His quality in attack only adds value that the Swans must protect. He has made 506 successful passes this campaign and 26 accurate long balls. With his first goal for the club last Saturday too, the ceiling is sky high for the youngster.

Swansea City could have had hit a real dip in form should Lissah not have been able to step up at right-back. He has already proven to be a quality asset at centre-back with a particularly dominant display against Derby County back in August, where he made 12 clearances and won 86% of his aerial duels.

With Josh Key sidelined back in September for a month, the Swans technically had no replacements for the 26-year-old. When Lissah was suspended for a game, Matos had to turn to Ronald, and the Welsh outfit suffered defeat against West Ham.

Now, with Key returning to action at the weekend against Blackburn Rovers, the 21-year-old faces some competition for a spot in the starting XI. However, given the pedigree of his displays in his debut Championship season, it’s become his position to lose.

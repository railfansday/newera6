PSA: Today’s the last day to purchase discounted cushion kits for Beats 360

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Apple introduced the new Beats 360 headphones about a month and a half ago. I reviewed them, and really appreciated their customizable design, the fact that they offer automatic device switching, as well as their comfort. At the time, Beats offered launch pricing on additional sets of cushions, bringing them down from $50 to just $10. After today, that steep discount will be ending.

With Beats 360, the name of the game was the customization, so the company offered steeply discounted cushions so you could mix and match your headphones and make them feel like yours.

There’s two types of cushions: Performance Knit and UltraPlush, and the sets of cushions normally cost $50. There’s a variety of color options for each cushion style, though personally I find the UltraPlush to be a must-buy.

Performance Knit is available in six color options: Black, Cloud, Pink, Sky, Navy, and Volt. The first four are headphone color-matching, and the latter two are unique options. All are available for just $9.99 for the rest of today.

UltraPlush is available in only two color options: Black and Cloud. To me, these are far more comfortable, so if you own Beats 360 and haven’t yet picked up a set of these, I’d recommend it.

Patriots Trade Idea Lands Eagles Super Bowl Champion for Very Cheap Price

It's only a matter of time before teams like the Philadelphia Eagles decide to trade certain players, with the expectation that it's going to happen in the next couple of days.

With the NFL trade deadline on Tuesday, Nov. 10, there's still another week of football left before teams have to make some tough decisions. For the New England Patriots, there's reason to believe multiple players in Philadelphia could help on both sides of the ball.

With the most obvious needs being a wide receiver or edge rusher, a trade between the Patriots and Eagles for either position doesn't seem likely.

However, there's also an argument to be made that Philadelphia has a cornerback who could be a great fit in New England. That guy would be Riq Woolen, who is on a one-year contract and shouldn't cost much in a potential trade. A recent trade proposal would send him to New England for just a fifth-round pick.

"CB Riq Woolen and a sixth-round pick for a fifth-round pick

"It looks like Carlton Davis will make his return to the Patriots' lineup Sunday after a neck injury kept him out of the last three games. If that's the case, cornerback isn't as big of a need as these other positions. But the Patriots still lack depth, and Woolen could provide some for a playoff run. Woolen was a part of Seattle's great defense a year ago that slowed the Patriots in the Super Bowl, but he's on just a one-year deal that wouldn't be a big lift to take on.

"So if the Pats opt for depth at cornerback, Woolen could be an option," Chad Graff of The Athletic wrote.

Getting a sixth-round pick in return and only having to move a fifth would be an easy decision for the Patriots if they want to go in this direction.

The question is whether New England believes that it's getting healthier in the secondary or whether it believes that there are other pressing needs that these assets could be used for.

I'm of the belief that adding a corner wouldn't be the worst idea, but to waste these assets and not use them on a wide receiver or an edge rusher would be a bit questionable.

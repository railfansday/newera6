Hailey Bieber Shares a Peek at Her Son Jack’s Halloween Costume

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Justin and Hailey Bieber dressed up as an angel and a devil for Halloween, and their son, Jack Blues Bieber, had a sweet little costume of his own.

On Saturday, Hailey posted an Instagram Story revealing her son’s ghost-inspired look. In the photo, Jack walks down the street in a flowing white hooded cape.

That same day, Justin shared his own costume. He was in a head-to-toe red outfit, complete with a beanie featuring devil horns and matching sunglasses from his own fashion label, Skylrk.

Hailey, meanwhile, channeled an angel in a white feathered bra, matching wings, and a halo. She completed the look with black shorts and black heels.

This isn’t the first time the Biebers have made Halloween a family affair. In 2024, the couple celebrated their first Halloween as parents by dressing up as characters from the Disney Channel series Kim Possible. Hailey portrayed the titular crime-fighting heroine, while Justin dressed as her best friend, Ron Stoppable. Baby Jack joined them as Ron’s beloved pet naked mole rat, Rufus, in a tiny pink onesie.

Last year, the family went the superhero route, dressing up as the Parr family from Pixar’s The Incredibles. Hailey transformed into Elastigirl, while Justin became Mr. Incredible, and Jack fittingly took on the role of baby Jack-Jack.

Hailey also shared a second costume for her son that year, with Jack channeling his father during his early music days in the iconic purple hoodie and white denim look Justin wore.

10 Lazy Pasta Dinners For When You Need Comfort Food Fast

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

If you’re like me, pasta is often on the mind—I may not be running a marathon, but you couldn’t tell from my weeknight dinner menu. There are so many uniquely delicious pasta recipes out there that it feels like you could boil up a new dish each night and never get tired. That being said, some pasta recipes are admittedly quite involved, which isn’t always appealing after a long day. That's where these 10 lazy pasta dinners come in handy. They still bring all of the flavor and comfort of more involved recipes, but with a minimal amount of effort on your part. From one-pot wonders to store-bought hacks and no-boil beauties, these pasta dinners are easy, cheesy, and sure to satisfy.

Let’s be honest—nothing sounds better after a long day than the classic, cheesy, saucy comfort food, but it’s also the last thing we want to take the time to prepare. Enter: this lazy lasagna. With some store-bought hacks, you can achieve a weeknight-friendly lasagna that’s every bit as comforting.

Pasta dinners have a reputation for being quick, but let's be honest: Bringing a pot of water to a boil takes forever. Okay, maybe not actually forever, but it can feel that way. Enter this clever no-boil baked chicken pasta, which allows the oven to do the work.

Our dream weeknight dinner is throwing a few ingredients into a pan, shoving it into the oven, then eating it out of that same pan in no time. This baked feta gnocchi is bringing all that and more. Featuring pantry superstar shelf-stable gnocchi, this riff on the viral baked feta pasta is even easier to make than the OG

This is a true 30-minute meal, and it calls for ingredients that you might already have on hand, saving you a trip to the grocery store. This saucy one-pan dinner features a base of fresh garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, thyme, heavy cream, and Parmesan tossed with pillowy-soft and quick-cooking gnocchi, then topped with a pinch of red pepper flakes for a little extra spicy kick.

When dinnertime indecision calls, there’s only one true answer: a meal mash-up that satisfies two cravings at once. This taco spaghetti is one of our most-loved examples. It pairs all of your favorite taco fixings with spaghetti rather than taco shells. The best part? Everything here comes together in one pot.

This four-ingredient, 20-minute Boursin mac and cheese is one that everyone can get behind. We admit that there is a time and place for a roux-based, gooey baked cheese with crispy toppings, but we’ll also admit that this is the best mac and cheese we’ve ever made and by far the simplest.

Craving chicken Parm but not in the mood for all the time it requires to prepare this labor of love dish? Opt for our chicken nugget Parmesan casserole instead. Store-bought chicken nuggets are a much faster swap-in for breading and pan-frying chicken cutlets—just layer them in between cheesy pasta, then top it with more chicken nuggets, more sauce.

One of our most-popular dinners, this one-pan chicken bacon gnocchi is a weeknight favorite for very good reason. The chicken here is cooked in the rendered bacon fat, making it tender and juicy, and the luxuriously rich and creamy sauce comes together almost effortlessly by combining broth, cream, and Parmigiano Reggiano.

This tangy, rich pasta kind of feels like mac & cheese’s cool older cousin. Maybe that’s because it comes complete with 3 layers of umami flavor: kimchi, Parmesan, and gochujang, a Korean fermented red chili paste.

Baked spaghetti is what comfort food is all about—cheesy, hearty, and completely adaptable to whatever you have on hand. While we could never get tired of classic spaghetti with homemade meat sauce, when we need a shortcut to the same flavors, we turn to this casserole.

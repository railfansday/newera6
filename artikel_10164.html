Win the Agentic Era, Together: Introducing the Docker Navigator Partner Program

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Secure, minimal container images for every team, free with enterprise features, if needed.

Over the past year, almost every conversation I’ve had with a partner has ended in the same place. Their customers are moving AI agents out of the lab and into production, and the moment they do, the question changes. It stops being “Can we build this?” and becomes “Can we trust it, secure it, and govern it?” The partners who can answer that second set of questions are the partners customers keep.

Today, we are launching the Docker Navigator Partner Program to help you become that partner. This post explains what we’ve built, why we built it around AI governance, and how you can get the most from it.

AI adoption is not following a normal technology curve. Every team, every vendor, every model provider, and every hyperscaler is racing to ship agentic capability, and no single company sees the whole stack. Identity, observability, supply chain security, MCP gateways, models, and clouds are each building their piece. What enterprises are missing is a trusted, common layer that lets those pieces work together safely.

That’s Docker’s role. Docker is where agents, models, and tools actually run: vertically integrated where it matters, and horizontally open to everything our customers already use. We describe it as being the Trusted Navigator for the agentic era; the constant that helps customers make sense of a landscape moving faster than any one team can track.

Partners are how that promise reaches customers. Whether you build technology, run a cloud, deliver services, or sell through the channel, you extend our reach and turn the Docker platform into a complete, governable stack. So the program carries the same name, and its tiers describe the journey a partner takes with us.

Every partner in the Docker Navigator Partner Program is navigating the agentic era with Docker. The tiers mark how far along that journey you are, and the top tier carries the program’s name on purpose: it’s the title you earn when you have shown you can navigate it for customers.

If you’re already a Docker partner, you enter the new program at the tier that matches your status today. Nothing is taken away, and your tier is protected until 30 April 2027 while we work with you on the requirements.

No one wins the agentic era alone, and Docker has spent much of 2026 demonstrating our belief in a collaborative ecosystem and approach. For example, in September, Docker became a founding member of the Blueprint Alliance alongside Okta, AWS, CrowdStrike, Databricks, Google Cloud, Lovable, Proofpoint, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Wiz and Zscaler, with GE Appliances and World Central Kitchen serving as strategic advisors. Announced at Oktane in Las Vegas, the Alliance has agreed on a shared architecture for securing AI agents, built around four questions every enterprise is asking: Where are my agents? What can they do? What are they doing? How do I respond? Our part is the environment where agents execute: isolation, runtime governance, and a secure supply chain that extends trust beyond the model to the place the agent runs.

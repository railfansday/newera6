Boga’s limited minutes put focus on Zhegrova before Lazio

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Luciano Spalletti outlined different considerations for Jérémie Boga and Edon Zhegrova before Juventus faced Lazio: Boga was fit for around half an hour, while Zhegrova’s performances had earned him a case for more playing time. His remarks, reported by Pazzi di Fanta and echoed in Juventus’ official press-conference account, point to contrasting immediate options rather than a settled hierarchy.

The distinction is grounded in what Spalletti said about their readiness and impact, not in an announced tactical role for either player. Boga remained available, but with his minutes limited; Zhegrova had impressed the coach with qualities and unpredictable play. Juventus’ official account also said the winger could start against Lazio.

For Boga, the clearest point was physical: Spalletti said he had about 30 minutes in his legs. That makes him an option for a defined contribution, rather than a player the coach said was ready to last a full match. It does not mean Boga was unavailable or ruled out of the selection.

Zhegrova’s case was different. Spalletti praised the qualities he had shown and said his performances warranted more playing time. The official Juventus account added that his unpredictable play could surprise opponents, while also reporting that he could start against Lazio.

That contrast suggests Zhegrova offered a possible route to changing the match through his attacking unpredictability, whereas Boga’s immediate involvement was constrained by fitness. It is an interpretation of Spalletti’s comments, not confirmation that the coach assigned them fixed or mutually exclusive positions. The pair’s different recent contributions have also featured in Juventus coverage of Zhegrova’s attacking impact.

Spalletti’s comments on team balance add an important qualification. The official account said the coach was weighing how to fit several attacking players into the side, taking both the opponent and the team’s overall balance into consideration. Praise for Zhegrova did not, by itself, guarantee a starting place.

The coach also rejected a rigid division between starters and substitutes, saying Juventus needed to widen the group of players considered starters. He was still assessing his selection and how the game might develop, with the possibility that starting certain attackers together would leave fewer options to influence the match later.

That broader approach prevents a simple reading in which Boga becomes a permanent bench option and Zhegrova takes over a defined role. Spalletti’s remarks were about the choices available for this fixture: Boga could contribute for roughly half an hour, while Zhegrova’s performances had strengthened his claim to more minutes. Neither statement settles their status over the season.

There is also a difference between being praised and being selected. Spalletti’s assessment gave Zhegrova positive momentum, and Juventus’ account explicitly left open a start against Lazio, but the coach said the final decision still depended on balance and the opposition. Boga, meanwhile, remained available within the fitness limit Spalletti had described.

The immediate comparison is therefore one of management: Boga’s involvement had a stated physical ceiling, while Zhegrova’s was supported by the coach’s view of his performances and unpredictability. That points to different ways they might help Juventus, without establishing a permanent tactical distinction. Boga’s recent place in the side has also been discussed alongside his selection in an attacking role.

Spalletti’s comments leave the practical question to the team selection and the match itself. Juventus’ official account said Zhegrova could start; Boga was available for around 30 minutes. Those were the clearest parameters ahead of Lazio, with no fixed pecking order announced.

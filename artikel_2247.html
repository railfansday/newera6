Martin O'Neill admits it's 'remarkable' Celtic still top after Falkirk loss

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Martin O'Neill acknowledges that it is "remarkable" that Celtic remain top of the Scottish Premiership after dropping more points against Falkirk.

The Hoops were well beaten by the Bairns, 2-0, as they lost their second game of the season.

Their defeat followed a 2-2 draw with Dundee United last midweek and added to a gloomy recent spate of poor performances and results in all competitions which sits at just three wins from 10 matches in all competitions.

For O'Neill he insists he saw enough from his side this afternoon to suggest they should have beaten their opponents.

But he admits they missed too many chances, as has happened too often this campaign already with Celts missing the chance to go seven clear at the summit.

"Is there any worry? Remarkably we're still top of the league," O'Neill said when asked the question by Sky Sports' Luke Shanley.

"We came from a mile behind last year to win the league. On two or three occasions as well. We're still in a decent position at this minute and we've played a lot of football as well."

Pushed by the reporter as to whether he feels Celtic could still be better, O'Neill added: "Of course we could. I'm not sure about your point here, I've just tried to explain to you, we missed goal after goal today.

"Their goalkeeper was magnificent, man of the match, so if we take even half our chances we would've won today."

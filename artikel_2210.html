West Ham v West Bromwich Albion | Nuno changes two again as line-up announced

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

The start of another three games in eight days mini-football-fest begins at London stadium this afternoon with West Ham taking on promotion rivals West Bromwich Albion.

Nuno’s men then travel to Cardiff for Wednesday night’s fixture before another long away trip next Saturday at Blackburn Rovers complete the pre-international break calendar. Nine points on offer and it must be ‘favourites’ West Ham’s plan to grab all nine.

With no Dinos Mavropanos this afternoon and Joel Veltman unlikely to start, the defence will be singled out for scrutiny as Nuno grapples with filling the Greek’s boots: Will it be teenage Golambeckis who gets the nod?

Manor Solomon is dropped, Airidas Golambeckis starts for Dinos and Edwin Austbø starts on the left in what looks suspiciously like the ‘lets not give a goal away in the first half, chaps‘ line up:

Hermansen | Walker-Peters, Golambeckis, Kilman, Morato | Bowen, Engels, Mukasa , Austbø | Kanté, Pablo

Interestingly, Joel Veltman and Soungoutou Magassa – but not Ollie Scarles – make the bench in what must be the strongest subs’ list in the Championship – all ready for the second half…

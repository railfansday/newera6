Burnt Sugar Caramel Cake

This burnt sugar caramel cake has a rich, deeply caramelized sugar flavor that sets it apart from other caramel cakes. The secret is the homemade burnt sugar syrup, made by cooking sugar until melted and caramelized, then carefully adding boiling water.

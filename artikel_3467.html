‘Star Trek: The Next Generation’ Officially Returns in 2027

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

As its 40th anniversary approaches next year, Star Trek: The Next Generation remains one of the most popular incarnations of the long-running science fiction franchise. Now, a decidedly different take on the crew's adventures is on its way — and it's not much bigger than a tricorder. It will look at the USS Enterprise-D like never before...through a mirror, darkly.

The Mirror Universe was first introduced in the Star Trek: The Original Series episode "Mirror, Mirror." In that episode, the Enterprise crew accidentally swap places with their counterparts from an alternate universe. In that universe, the peaceful United Federation of Planets never was, as humanity embraced war and greed and violence to form the conquering Terran Empire. The concept of the Mirror Universe has been explored in several subsequent Star Trek series, including Deep Space Nine, Enterprise, and Discovery.

The Next Generation never returned to the Mirror Universe on-screen, but several comic books from IDW Publishing did. In a series of three miniseries, they take a look at the crew of the ISS Enterprise and its merciless captain, Jean-Luc Picard, as they explore new worlds to conquer, and seek out new life forms and civilizations to enslave and pillage. Now, all of IDW's Mirror Universe TNG books are being collected into one handy collected edition. Star Trek: The Next Generation – The Mirror Universe: IDW Classic Collections is 408 pocket-sized pages, measuring six inches by nine inches. It will only set you back $13.99 USD or its equivalent in gold-pressed latinum, and is set to be released on February 2, 2027.

The collected edition collects three full miniseries: Mirror Broken (six issues), Through the Mirror (five issues), and Terra Incognita (six issues). It also collects a one-shot story that reveals the origins of Data's Mirror counterpart. The stories in the collection are written by David and Scott Tipton (Spock: Reflections), alongside artists J.K. Woodward (Fallen Angel), Marcus To (Excalibur), Chris Johnson (Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles), Josh Hood (JLA: Scary Monsters), Carlos Nieto (Ultimate Black Panther), Débora Caríta (Dejah Thoris), Tony Shasteen (Star Trek: The Q Gambit), and Ángel Hernández (Star Trek/Green Lantern: The Spectrum War).

Star Trek: The Next Generation – The Mirror Universe: IDW Classic Collections trade paperback will be available on store shelves on February 2, 2027. Stay tuned to Collider for future updates.

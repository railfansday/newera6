Arsecast Extra Episode 719 – 02.11.2026

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Welcome to another Arsecast Extra, the Arsenal podcast, with myself and James from @gunnerblog.

In this episode we look back at the massive win at Anfield on Sunday after Kai Havertz’s first half goal was enough for all three points. We analyse the Gunners strong start to the game, and opportunities to score a second, before the game swung back towards the home side in the last part of the first half. There’s discussion of Jurrien Timber’s foul on Alexander Isak which probably should have seen Liverpool awarded a penalty, and the big miss from Alexis Mac Allister, before we look at how Arsenal’s hugely impressive defensive strength kept the opposition at arm’s length for the second half, and what this result means for the title challenge even at this relatively early point of the season. In part two we answer questions about egregious play-acting, Mikel Arteta’s subs, Declan Rice’s standing in the pantheon of Arsenal signings, Bruno Guimaraes’ integration thus far, and lots more.

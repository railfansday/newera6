$5M matching grant moves University of Texas at El Paso closer to opening law school

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Heather Wilson (center), the president of the University of Texas at El Paso, speaks at a news conference announcing that the University of Texas at El Paso will soon launch a law school after the Woody and Gayle Hunt Family Foundation pledged a $5 million matching grant. (Photo from the University of Texas at El Paso’s Oct. 28 press release)

The University of Texas at El Paso inched closer to launching a law school after the Woody and Gayle Hunt Family Foundation pledged a $5 million matching grant.

The school’s leaders aim to present the vision for the law school to the Texas legislature in 2027, with the goal of opening in 2030.

In October 2025, the Paul L. Foster Family Foundation also announced a $5 million matching grant, and El Paso, Texas, law firm Tawney, Acosta & Chaparro committed to a $5 million gift in April. Other El Paso firms—including Farah Law, Flores Mendez Law, ScottHulse Attorneys at Law, and Dallas-based Kastl Law—have made donations that will be matched by the Woody and Gayle Hunt Family Foundation’s grant.

The law school would focus on public and private international law involving the United States and Mexico and aims to address the shortage of fluent bilingual lawyers.

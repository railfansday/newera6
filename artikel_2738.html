Kickoff time set for Clemson football vs. Georgia Tech

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Nov. 2—CLEMSON — The kickoff time for Clemson football's next home game is set.

Clemson will host Georgia Tech on Nov. 14 at 12:30 p.m. The game will be broadcast on The CW.

The Tigers (5-3, 3-2 ACC) are headed to Syracuse on Nov. 7. Clemson took down Florida State 36-24 on Oct. 31 in Tallahassee, Fla. FSU coach Mike Norvell was fired the day after the game.

Georgia Tech (2-6, 1-4) hosts Louisville this weekend. It fell 34-11 at Pitt on Oct. 31.

The Yellow Jackets lead the all-time series 51-36-2 going back to 1898. The two teams played every season from 1983-2023, with Clemson winning the last nine in that stretch, before skipping a year in 2024. Georgia Tech won the most recent matchup 24-21 in 2025 on a walk-off field goal.

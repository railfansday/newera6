Anthropic’s Claude AI predicts an exceptionally bullish move for Bitcoin Cash (BCH) by January 1, 2027. It suggests a price range of $2,000 to $4,000, with a possible stretch target above $5,000 if a full-fledged crypto bull market returns and a major catalyst emerges.

This scenario envisions a robust bull market in late 2026, driven by the adoption of Bitcoin Cash (BCH) as a preferred payment method by a major global payments network or a consortium of merchants.

Such adoption would leverage BCH’s larger block size, enabling low-cost transactions and generating substantial demand, thereby revitalizing its narrative.

(SOURCE: Claude AI Predicts Bitcoin Cash to $5K)

In this speculative scenario, Bitcoin Cash (BCH) could rise from about $280–$285 to $2,000-$4,000, with the possibility of reaching $5,000 by early 2027. This potential surge would be driven by real-world payment demand and speculative enthusiasm.

However, achieving such a significant increase would require unprecedented coordination within the Bitcoin Cash ecosystem, making this scenario more of a thought exercise than a realistic prediction.

On higher timeframes, Bitcoin Cash (BCH) has retraced into the $270–$280 range after failing to hold above $300–$320. Price is now testing near-term support after recovering from the mid-$200s. In a typical bull market, a sustained breakout above the $300–$350 range would pave the way for higher price targets.

Under the extreme scenario of increased payment adoption, breaking through major resistance levels could lead to significant measured-move extensions and Fibonacci projections from a multi-year base. This could theoretically support a price surge into the $2,000–$4,000+ range if trading volume and real-world usage increase substantially.

The Relative Strength Index (RSI) has cooled into neutral-to-oversold territory on shorter timeframes, which might signal a rebound if buyers enter the market. There is still considerable room for growth, as the price could reach extreme overbought levels typically seen during parabolic advances.

Key near-term support levels are located in the $270–$280 and $250–$260 zones. Holding these levels would help maintain the broader recovery structure while the market adjusts to any major narrative changes.

Overall, although the current chart shows short-term weakness, only a significant rise in real-world payment adoption could justify the multi-thousand-percent increase suggested by the $2,000–$5,000 price targets.

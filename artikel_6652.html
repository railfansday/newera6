Snickers Apple Salad

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

Candy in salad might seem contradictory, but this Snickers apple salad is here to prove it can be done. Chopped apples are tossed with Snickers in a creamy vanilla dressing and drizzled with caramel for a cool and sweet dessert that will surprise and delight everyone at bonfire night. Trust me—it’s a Midwest classic for a reason.

What People Are Saying:“Childhood favorite! ❤️” - NanaManda2025

Start by making a salt water bath for the apples. In a big bowl, stir together water and salt in a bowl until the salt is dissolved. Keep the bowl nearby while you cut the apples into 1/2″ pieces, adding the apples to the salt bath as soon as they’re cut—this will stop them from browning. When all the apples have been cut and fully submerged in the water, drain the apples and transfer about one-eighth of them to a small container—these will be for topping the salad. Cover and refrigerate them until you’re ready to serve the salad.

In a large bowl, whisk together the milk and pudding mix until thickened (this won’t take longer than a minute). Use a flexible rubber spatula to gently fold in the Cool Whip.

Then fold in the remaining apples and most of the chopped Snickers until incorporated.

Transfer the salad to a serving bowl and chill in the refrigerator until it’s cold—1 hour should be just fine. When you’re ready to serve, top with the remaining apples and chopped Snickers, and drizzle with caramel sauce.

The full list of ingredients and instructions can be found in the recipe below.

While you can technically keep Snickers apple salad up to 3 days in the refrigerator, it’s really best the day it’s made. The longer it sits, the looser it gets. By day 3, it’s basically Snickers apple soup.

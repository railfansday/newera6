Watch: Nintendo Treehouse Plays Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy

📅 03 November 2026 | Sumber: Baca Asli

A special broadcast timed in with Pikmin Day

Update [Sun 1st Nov 2026, 5pm]: This Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy Treehouse broadcast has now been uploaded to Nintendo's YouTube channel.

Original Story: Apart from its Pikmin Day Direct, Nintendo has announced it's hosting a special Treehouse broadcast for Pikmin 4 - Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy.

Its airs later today on YouTube and will give fans a better look at this version of the game, and what you can expect before you jump into it on 12th November 2026. Here are the times for the upcoming Treehouse Direct:

Nintendo: Join us tomorrow on Pikmin Day for Nintendo Treehouse Plays:#Pikmin4 – Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Dandori Academy. Watch on demand via YouTube on Nov 1 at 9am PT: ninten.do/6012a2HZG

Nintendo is also releasing a free demo for Pikmin 4's Switch 2 Edition, which will give players access to the opening area and two stages of Dandori Academy. This will include the ability to transfer save data across to the full game.
